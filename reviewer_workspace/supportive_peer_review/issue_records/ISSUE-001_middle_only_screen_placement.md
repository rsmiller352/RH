# ISSUE-001 — Middle-only screen placement is asserted, not argued, in the public materials

**Severity: Level 4 (submission risk).** Classification: PERSUASIVENESS_RISK escalated to submission risk.

## Exact location
Manuscript §13.4 ("The factor acts once, after quadratic-support formation, and only on the middle rank"), with §13.0 framing and §13.2's support construction; card BRIDGE_016_007 §0 (claim), §4 (Read_409 definition), §8 (falsifier 11: "a fixed sector replaces the middle rank").

## Exact claim under review
That the finite-screen factor s_409 = 409·sin(π/409)/π acts on the middle quadratic support and only the middle quadratic support — the step producing R^scr_mid/min = s_409·R^int_mid/min while R^scr_max/min = R^int_max/min.

## Context examined (protocol stages 1–5)
- *Local:* §13.4 complete; §13.2–13.3 (support construction, pushout); §13.0 ("The spectrum supplies 206; the recovery record supplies the two-face structure").
- *Section:* all of §13; the recovery clause ("division by s_409 recovers the intrinsic middle ratio").
- *Manuscript:* §11 (two-face record), §12 (rank formation), §14 (consumption of the screened triple), abstract ("regular-screen factor acts once on the already formed middle quadratic support"), conclusion.
- *Cards:* BRIDGE_016_007 in full (claim, construction, covariance clause "never acts on an amplitude and never uses a fixed sector label", recovery record, falsifiers, source map); LEM_016_006; THM_016_003.
- *Charitable reconstructions attempted:* (i) placement follows from the construction because only the middle rank *has* a completed count consumed by a two-face event — the min rank is the unit reference (ratio 1, degenerate) and the max rank's floor is never given a completed-count role; (ii) rank-covariance ("never uses a fixed sector label") is the argument — the screen attaches to the *rank* mid, which is presentation-invariant, so no sector is chosen; (iii) the placement is a constitutive identification whose exclusivity is proven in the private system (card's source map cites "corrected placement and particle-role discipline," SRC_000219 — not in the package).

## Why the charitable interpretations do not fully resolve it
(i) is a *plausible reconstruction the reviewer supplied* — neither the manuscript nor any included card states it; a reader cannot adopt an argument that is not there. (ii) answers "why not a labeled sector" but not "why the mid rank rather than the max rank, or all ranks" — covariance constrains *how* the screen attaches, not *where*. (iii) concedes the point for the public materials: the exclusivity argument, if it exists, is outside the supplied sources. Under the review's own burden rule, the step stands in the public record as a constructed-and-asserted placement.

## Reader consequence
This is the exact step a competent skeptical referee will isolate: a ~10⁻⁵ multiplicative correction applied to precisely the rank that produces the muon agreement, in a construction the abstract candidly dates to after muon exposure. Without a placement argument, the natural referee sentence is: "the screen is a post-hoc correction with a geometric costume." The paper's honesty elsewhere makes this survivable in review only if the placement is either argued or explicitly typed as a constitutive identification with its falsifier displayed in-text.

## Smallest sufficient repair
Add one paragraph (2–4 sentences) to §13.4, doing one of:
- **(A) State the placement argument** if it is the reconstruction (i): the screen consumes a completed count; among the three ranks only the middle supplies one (n_mid = 206 from the proved interval), the minimum being the reference with degenerate count 1 and the maximum ratio never entering a completed-count role in the carried record; hence the screen has exactly one lawful attachment point. (If this is the authors' actual argument, its absence is purely presentational and this issue drops to Level 2 once stated. Note the max-rank floor ⌊3477.47⌋ = 3477 exists numerically — the paragraph must say why no completed-count role attaches to it, e.g., because the two-face record supplies supports only for the event whose completed count is n_mid.)
- **(B) Type the placement as a constitutive identification** (per the paper's own §14-style honesty): state that middle-only action is a constitutive step of the construction, restate falsifier 11 in-text ("if a fixed sector, or any rank other than the middle, is found to require the screen, the Bridge fails"), and note the intrinsic ratio's recoverability.
Either repair is small; (A) is stronger if available. The issue is *not* a request to re-derive anything — it is a request to put the placement's warrant (or its honest typing) where the reader can see it.
