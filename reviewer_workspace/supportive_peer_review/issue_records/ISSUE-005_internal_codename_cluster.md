# ISSUE-005 — Internal codename cluster in the public text

**Severity: Level 3 (persuasiveness risk).** Classification: PERSUASIVENESS_RISK (internal-language leakage).

## Exact locations
"S02 book"/"S02 book layer": §3 (line region of the c = 3 reading), §8.6, §8.8. "the typed T₃ remainder slot": §7.7. "a P0 prediction": §8.10 (boundary list; §8.9 context). "the lens-calibration identity": §8.9 and §8.10. "lower C4 carrier assignments": §10.3. "the promoted pointwise co-reading": §3. "the value-free closure office": §3. Named-but-unstated theorems: §4.1, §4.2 (×2), §8.9 ("the time-bridge checklist… the seventh item").

## Exact claim under review
Whether these tokens are resolvable by the intended reader from the manuscript (plus, generously, the cards) — and if not, what a referee infers from them.

## Context examined
Every occurrence read with full paragraph and section context (stages 1–3). Cards (stage 4): the tokens resolve *internally* — S02 is spine-layer numbering (cards' folder taxonomy; SEC03's internal title is "THE_S02_BOOK_AND_MATRIX_LADDER"), T₃ a system-account slot, P0 a prediction class, the checklist is GATE_001_004's nine-point contract — but no included card defines them *for a reader*, and the manuscript reader has no cards anyway (no in-text reference exists). Charitable reconstruction (stage 5): each token's surrounding sentence supplies enough context that the *argument* survives deletion of the token; none carries load-bearing content unavailable in plain words.

## Why the charitable interpretation is insufficient
Precisely because the tokens carry no public content, their only effect on a reader is signaling: an undefined private nomenclature showing through the publication surface. Individually each is Level 2; as a cluster (nine distinct tokens, three sections) they furnish a referee's easiest concrete evidence for "this paper was extracted from a private system and not fully translated" — feeding desk-rejection risk R6. §8.9–8.10 is the densest cluster (four tokens in two paragraphs).

## Reader consequence
Credibility leak at triage and referee stages; zero compensating benefit to any reader.

## Smallest sufficient repair (mechanical; ~30 minutes)
Per the leakage audit table: replace "S02 book" → "the Section 3 base book" (3×); "typed T₃ remainder slot" → "the system account's remainder slot"; "a P0 prediction" → "a measured prediction"; delete "the lens-calibration identity" from both non-claim lists; replace the "lower C4 carrier assignments" sentence → "no further structure of the four-cell frame is used in this paper"; delete/replace "promoted" as status adjective (§3, §9); define or rewrite around "the value-free closure office" (§3); resolve named-unstated theorems via the numbering decision (ISSUE-002 item 3). The §11.0 pattern ("The formal authority retains its internal identifier. The publication uses only the result…") is the house style the repairs should follow.
