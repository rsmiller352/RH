# ISSUE-006 — The constitutive identification of the support triple with charged-lepton masses is never stated as such

**Severity: Level 3 (reader-comprehension risk).** Classification: READER-COMPREHENSION_RISK.

## Exact location
§14.1 (naming map) and §14.2 (electron anchoring); background in §8.6 (support readout).

## Exact claim under review
The step by which three intrinsic dimensionless "quadratic supports" become *charged-lepton mass* reports. The paper types the naming map as "a downstream naming convention over already derived ranks" — correct for the *ordering* — but the physical identification (that these supports are the quantities an observer reads as charged-lepton masses, effected by admitting the electron record as the comparison standard for q_min) is never stated in one place as the constitutive step it is.

## Context examined
- *Local/section:* §14.1–14.3 complete; the naming map's honest limits ("does not claim that laboratory masses selected the ordering") — which fence what the map does *not* do without stating what the anchoring *does* do.
- *Manuscript:* §8.6 (support readout as the mass-side reading, "does not define a kilogram"); §8.7 (reporting map); abstract ("complete relative three-entry report"); conclusion.
- *Cards:* ROLEDEF_016_004 ("does not derive a particle identity"); BRIDGE_016_008 (the electron datum as "the only experimental derivation input"); THM_016_009 ("not an empirical-confirmation claim").
- *Charitable reconstruction:* the identification is distributed and deliberate: §8 builds "support" as the mass-side readout; §14 anchors the minimum to the electron; the reader is expected to compose these. The composition is available.

## Why the charitable interpretation is insufficient
The predictable referee question — "why is this triple the charged leptons rather than any other three positive quantities?" — currently has no sentence to point to. The paper's honest answer (the theory derives one distinguished three-rank support spectrum; identifying its minimum with the electron record is the constitutive physical step; the identification's empirical content is then exhausted by two ratio comparisons and the Koide shape) is modest, defensible, and consistent with everything the paper says — but the reader must construct it. In a paper this disciplined about typing every step, the *one* physical identification is the step most in need of explicit typing.

## Reader consequence
"Why leptons?" lands as an unanswered objection instead of an answered-by-design one; the paper's claim strength is mis-read as larger (particle identities derived) or smaller (numerology with names) than it is.

## Smallest sufficient repair
Two to three sentences at §14.2 (or the end of §14.1), e.g.: *"Admitting the electron record as the comparison standard for q_min is the constitutive identification of this paper: it types the derived support triple as the charged-lepton mass report. The theory does not derive particle identities; it derives one distinguished three-rank spectrum, and the identification's empirical content is exactly the two dimensionless ratio comparisons (and the downstream Koide shape) that the remainder of the paper reports."*
