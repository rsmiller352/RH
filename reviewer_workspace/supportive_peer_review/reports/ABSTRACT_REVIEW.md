## Assessment

The abstract is unusually honest and structurally complete, and its claim-status hygiene should be preserved verbatim wherever possible. Its two weaknesses are omissions, not excesses: (1) no problem statement; (2) no statement of the mass-ratio outputs' relation to measurement (a post-derivation comparison the paper is entitled to state with the same typing it already uses for Koide). A third, smaller weakness is jargon density in the middle third.

## Suggested revision (optional; preserves all claims and qualifications)

Changes: adds one problem-framing sentence at the head; adds one typed comparison sentence; lightens two dense sentences; keeps all existing caveats. The authors should treat this as a patch illustration, not replacement prose.

> The measured charged-lepton mass ratios are unexplained inputs of the Standard Model. ## Verdict
Material journal-facing weaknesses: the two omissions above (problem statement; outcome statement). Both are additions that preserve claim strength exactly. Priority: strongly recommended.
