# Manuscript Strengths

This file protects strong material from unnecessary revision. Passages listed here should be preserved substantially unchanged unless a listed issue record specifically touches them.

## Global strengths

1. **Falsifiability discipline.** Ten of fifteen sections end with a concrete "this layer can lose, and says how" list. This is the manuscript's signature and its single largest credibility asset. Preserve every one.
2. **Status typing of every value.** The claim-status ledger found *zero* fitted quantities and zero mistyped claims: every number is explicitly derived, defined, calibrated, or checked-afterward, and the paper says which. The typing vocabulary (intrinsic / presentation / bridge / readout / calibration) is applied with near-perfect consistency across 56 pages.
3. **Honest epistemic disclosures.** The abstract's post-muon-exposure sentence; "not an absolute prediction of the electron mass"; "not a blind held-out prediction"; Koide "not an exact identity." These sentences are rare in this genre and must survive every revision. They are the paper's best answer to the fitting accusation.
4. **Anticipatory disambiguation.** §10 exists to separate two numerically equal 2/3s before any critic can conflate them; §11 separates (2,1)-counts from |C| = 3; §4.1 separates c = 3 from spatial dimension; §8.8 separates c⊗2 from c². The paper consistently finds its own weak-looking joints and reinforces them first.
5. **Dimensional hygiene.** Everything is dimensionless until §14.2; ℏ never leaks inland; units enter exactly once. (Verified mechanically.) As clean as this reviewer has seen in a foundational manuscript.
6. **Observer language.** Fully operational throughout; three explicit consciousness-denials; no anthropomorphism found (see OBSERVER_LANGUAGE_AUDIT). The clockhood passage ("Nothing is inherently a clock") is quotable and defensible.
7. **Section transitions.** Every section opens by recapping what it inherited and closes by fencing what it did not establish. The reader always knows what changed.
8. **Checkability of the core mathematics.** §12's double derivation of 2/3; the exponent-forcing argument m·9^s = 9m; §13.1's Pell certificates and Taylor degrees; §13.3's both-bounds pushout argument with both wrong alternatives excluded; §4.5's inball uniqueness proof. All independently verified in this review (numerics to 30+ digits; every printed decimal exact).
