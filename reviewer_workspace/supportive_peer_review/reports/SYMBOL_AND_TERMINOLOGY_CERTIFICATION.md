# Symbol and Terminology Certification

## Basis
Complete audit of Phase 6: mechanical occurrence extraction over the full manuscript text with manual verification against the PDF for every load-bearing symbol; master ledger of 80+ symbol families; collision, undefined-symbol, and occurrence reports; manuscript↔card notation diff; terminology ledger covering all theory-specific terms including the charter's watch list.

## Certified findings

1. **No symbol changes meaning silently within its declared scope.** All reuses are subscript-, font-, or section-scoped, or else recorded as findings below.
2. **Dimensional discipline is fully consistent.** Every quantity is dimensionless until the calibration display in §14.2; ℏ occurs only inside typed bridge clauses; MeV only in §§14–15. Zero violations found.
3. **The intrinsic/observer boundary is notationally maintained** except two documented blurs (A#/A#_o; I_bulk/I_bulk,o) that the prose elsewhere polices; mathematics unaffected.
4. **Definition-before-use holds** except the five documented items (1/√2 coefficient; Π₀; Σ_rel; §10.4's forward-used spectral apparatus; internal codenames) — all with one-sentence-scale repairs.
5. **Two notation conflicts require repair before submission:** the α→x renaming of the spectral phase across §§12–13, and the undefined zero-subscript Π₀ colliding with Π_o in §12.3's load-bearing display (mathematics verified correct in both cases).
6. **Manuscript↔card notation agrees** up to the documented systematic diffs (λ/Λ; α/x; dropped card decorations); no diff changes any value or claim.
7. **Terminology is defined-before-use and stable** for all core terms; the exceptions are exactly the internal codenames (S02, T₃, P0, lens-calibration identity; C4 partially), plus three terms recommended for orientation sentences (the three senses of "closure"; a global definition of "forced"; the title phrase "observer-rooted," which never occurs in the body).
8. **The status vocabulary** (derived/defined/selected/calibrated/fitted/assumed/checked-afterward) **is applied consistently and correctly throughout**; the claim-status ledger found zero mistyped and zero fitted claims.

## Certification statement
Subject to the repairs enumerated in items 4–5 and the codename replacements (all mechanical; itemized in the prioritized revision plan, Tiers 1.7–1.8 and 3.3), the reviewer certifies the manuscript's symbol system and controlled terminology as **consistent, complete, and load-bearing-safe**: no identified notation or terminology defect affects the validity of any derivation step, and every identified defect has a specified smallest repair.
