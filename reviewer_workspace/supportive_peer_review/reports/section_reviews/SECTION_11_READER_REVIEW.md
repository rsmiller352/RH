**Comprehension assessment.** Good at the formal level; thinner at the motivational level. Every formal statement is explicit and small (injections, tags, tuple projections). The section's two firewalls are stated with full clarity: (i) the counts (2,1) are "not identified, multiplied, added, averaged, or otherwise combined"; (ii) |C| = 3 comes from the rooted C₃ structure, and "the numerical statement n_post + n_route = 2 + 1 = 3 is not the derivation of |C| = 3" — the paper again defusing a numerology reading before it can be made.

But the event γ_Ψ itself arrives from nowhere: what event? produced by what? why exactly two faces, and why counts (2,1)? The supporting cards (DEF_011_001, LEM_011_003) give the Ψ-closure event a definition in the four-cell frame with the posting side cost-eligible and the routing side unpriced — one orienting sentence of that kind in the manuscript ("the event is the four-cell frame's completed closure/distinction; its posting face carries the priced completion, its routing face the unpriced connective, whence the counts (2,1)") would convert the section from formal-record-received to construction-understood. As it stands, this is the paper's largest single "trust me" for the attentive reader — not because anything is wrong, but because the object's origin story is entirely card-side. The question is whether the *minimum* disclosed is sufficient; my assessment: one more sentence of origin is needed for a physics reader to carry conviction into §13.)
