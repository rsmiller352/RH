# Section 6 — Aperture and Resolution

**Purpose.** Define histories, internal action 𝒮_Φ, the resolution unit u_Φ, the action gap, the resolution floor, the coherence aperture with its three regimes, and the two bridge-side faces (suppression, focusing).

**Comprehension assessment.** High. The internal/bridge split is the section's organizing idea and it is enforced sentence by sentence. The floor theorem is clean; the three-regimes partition (§6.8) is given with its disjointness argument and even polices the ∅ symbol against the zero discipline — thorough to the point of fastidiousness, but consistent.

**Strongest passages.** §6.3's unit-relativity of u_Φ ("The invariant is the one-unit threshold, not the printed size of the unit"). §6.9's insistence that the nonstationary condition is load-bearing in the suppression clause — a real physics point stated correctly. §6.10's no-shift claim for focusing ("Focusing changes resolution; it does not move event or action locations").

**Reader friction.** (1) The reader meets ℏ for the first time here, inside bridge clauses; the typing is careful, but a physicist skimming may miss that ℏ never becomes internal — the section says it, repeatedly, and still the visual presence of ∆S/ℏ invites the misread. (2) A#'s constraint 0 < A# ≤ 1 is posited for the bridge readout without derivation (acceptable for a bridge parameter; one clause saying "constrained by definition of a resolution-degrading readout" would seal it). (3) What the aperture layer contributes downstream is not stated here; the reader discovers in §12 that the aperture concept underwrites A#_o = 2/3. A forward pointer would motivate the section's presence in the mass paper.

**Notation.** 𝒮_Φ, u_Φ, g_Φ, A_Φ, A#, I_bulk, I_screen — all defined at first use. The later A#/A#_o and I_bulk/I_bulk,o reuse (§12) originates here (collision X5/X6).

**Transitions.** Clean; the §7 seam ("the discrete/continuous seam… belongs to the next section") is explicitly deferred.

**Persuasive strengths.** The section demonstrates that the theory can talk *about* standard quantum-mechanical furniture (action, phase, ℏ, stationary phase) without importing it — the strongest evidence in the paper that the bridge discipline is real rather than rhetorical.

**Credibility risks.** Low, given the typing. The word "aperture" and 0 < A# ≤ 1 may look like optics analogy; §6.10's careful scoping mostly defuses it.

**Verified issues.** None at Level ≥3.

**Smallest improvements.**
1. Forward pointer: "the one-pass aperture value used by the spectral construction (§12) is an instance of this aperture concept" (also repairs collision X5's silence).
2. One clause on why 0 < A# ≤ 1 (optional).
