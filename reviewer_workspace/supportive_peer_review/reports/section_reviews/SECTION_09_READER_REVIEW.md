# Section 9 — Recovery and Presentation Discipline

**Purpose.** Fix the rules for rendered presentations: construction records must remain recoverable; operations on rendered reports require recovery first; unit changes are presentation-only; organizational displays license no algebra.

**Comprehension assessment.** Good. The section is short, formal, and self-contained. The RCI package and operation-level recovery formalism are notation-heavy for what they say, but each formula is glossed. The scoping sentence ("This is a scoped rule for rendered observer-local readouts… not a new universal demand") prevents the strongest misreading.

**Why the section matters downstream.** Its yield is consumed at exactly the right moments: §11's exact recovery record, §13.4's carried Rec_409, §14.2's invertible bridge, and the repeated organizational-display fences. A reader may not see this on first pass — the section reads as pure discipline with no object-level payoff. One forward sentence ("this contract is what later makes the 409 construction and the electron bridge invertible rather than merely numerical," §§11, 13, 14) would motivate it.

**Strongest passages.** §9.4's κ-rescaling identity (q′u′ = qu) as the entire content of unit conventionality, stated in three lines. §9.5's list of what the arrays do *not* license (matrix multiplication, determinants, eigenvalues…) — blunt and effective.

**Reader friction.** The Render/Recover/RCI notation (Rec_O, Idx_O, π_idx, OpRec, UseΦ_O ↓) is the heaviest formal notation in the paper relative to conceptual content; the intended reader can process it, but the section would lose nothing if the display count were halved. This is the one section where *more* prose and *less* formalism would serve.

**Notation.** All new symbols defined at use; Φ as generic operation collides with nothing (local).

**Transitions.** Good.

**Persuasive strengths.** The section converts "we will not cheat with presentation" from promise to mechanism.

**Credibility risks.** Low. Some referees will consider the section overhead; its brevity keeps the cost acceptable.

**Verified issues.** None.

**Smallest improvements.**
1. Forward sentence naming the three downstream consumption sites.
2. Optional: compress the display inventory (e.g., fold the two projection equations into prose).
