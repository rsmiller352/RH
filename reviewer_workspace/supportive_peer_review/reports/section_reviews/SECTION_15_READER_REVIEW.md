# Section 15 — Independent Koide Validation

**Purpose.** Evaluate the scale-free Koide functional on the completed report and state the ≈1.35 ppm consistency with the 2/3 benchmark, with the independence claim typed precisely.

**Comprehension assessment.** High. The section is short, correct, and disciplined. Scale cancellation is proved in two lines; the exact output and the two derived deltas are displayed; the concluding status sentence is a model of calibrated claim strength: "The independently derived charged-lepton report is Koide-consistent at approximately the 1.35-ppm level."

**Independence handling.** The section does everything the subtitle requires of it at the logical level: (i) the report is complete before the section begins (stated at §15.0 and true by construction order); (ii) the benchmark 2/3 is explicitly separated from both internal 2/3's ("it does not supply the rooted action aperture and is not inferred from the separately displayed C4 invariant"); (iii) the ppm figure is typed as consistency, "not an exact identity"; (iv) the failure conditions of the independence claim are enumerated (if Koide had selected 2/9, adjusted s_409, chosen 206/409, altered rank assignment, or fit either ratio); (v) the word "independent" is explicitly limited to logical ordering, and the post-data-exposure caveat is restated. This is the strongest independence hygiene this reviewer has seen around a Koide claim. Full audit: reports/KOIDE_INDEPENDENCE_AUDIT.md.

**Reader friction.** (1) A reader may want one sentence on *why* 2/3 is the interesting benchmark (one line recalling Koide's Q = (Σm)/(Σ√m)² = 2/3 empirical observation with the 1983 citation is present via reference [2] but the relation's empirical status — that measured charged-lepton masses satisfy it to high precision — is never stated; the section validates against the *algebraic* benchmark without telling the reader that the benchmark is itself empirically realized). Adding one sentence ("the measured charged-lepton masses satisfy Q ≈ 2/3 to within experimental uncertainty, which is why the benchmark is diagnostic") would make the validation legible to a reader who does not already know Koide. (2) The near-miss structure (9×10⁻⁷ above exactly 2/3) invites the question "is the deviation a prediction?" — the paper does not comment; one sentence noting that the derived shape *differs* from exact 2/3 by a definite computable amount (and that exact equality is not claimed and would in fact be a different theory) would convert an ambiguity into a sharp statement, and is arguably a falsifiable fingerprint worth owning.

**Strongest passages.** The failure-condition list; the closing boxed-style admissible conclusion.

**Notation.** 𝒬_K, r_μ, r_τ, Δ_K clean.

**Verified issues.** None at Level ≥3. The two friction items are Level 2 orientation gaps.
