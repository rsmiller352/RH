# Section 13 — The Exact 206-to-409 Construction

**Purpose.** Prove 206 < R_int < 207 without measured input; build the 409-class screen from the two-face record; derive s_409 and apply it once to the middle quadratic support.

**Comprehension assessment.** §13.1 is exemplary — Pell certificates displayed, Taylor-bound degrees stated, "No decimal and no measured muon value participates in this result." §13.3's pushout is handled with the both-bounds argument and both wrong alternatives (406, 412) explicitly excluded. §13.4's regular-screen formula is stated compactly (the full winding argument lives in the card; the in-text version is adequate).

**The section's central exposure: middle-only placement.** The screen factor is the difference between the intrinsic 206.7703 and the reported 206.7683; it is a ~10⁻⁵ correction applied to exactly one of the three ranks. The manuscript's §13.0 framing ("The spectrum supplies 206; the recovery record supplies the two-face structure needed to combine two 206-element supports") explains *how* the construction runs but not *why it attaches to the middle rank and only the middle rank*. Neither §13 nor the supporting Bridge card gives a reader-visible argument excluding the alternatives a referee will immediately pose:
- why does the *maximum* rank acquire no analogous screen (e.g., two 3477-element supports → s_6951)?
- why does the *minimum* rank acquire none (its ratio to itself being 1 gives n = 1 — arguably degenerate, which is a hint the paper could use)?
The card asserts placement ("acts once, after quadratic-support formation, on the middle rank"), lists "a fixed sector replaces the middle rank" among falsifiers, and cites a "corrected placement and particle-role discipline" source **not included in the public package**. Within the supplied materials, middle-only placement is therefore a *constitutive step that is asserted with machinery rather than argued* — the single most consequential such step in the paper, on the rank that produces the muon agreement, in a construction the abstract candidly dates to after muon exposure. The honesty of the disclosure is exactly right; the absence of a structural placement argument is the paper's most predictable serious-referee objection. (Candidate issue CI-5; classified Level 3/4 in Phase 9.)

**A hint the paper leaves unused.** The construction's own logic suggests a possible answer: the screen count is generated by the *floor of a rank ratio* (n_mid = 206), and only the middle rank has a nondegenerate two-face pairing in the carried record; the minimum rank is the reference (ratio 1) and the maximum rank's ratio floor is never assigned a completed-count role. If the intended argument is "only the middle rank's completed count enters a two-face event," the paper should say so in two or three sentences. If the placement is instead a constitutive identification whose alternatives are excluded elsewhere in the private system, the paper should say *that*, and type the step accordingly (constitutive identification, not forced consequence). Either repair is small; the silence is what is costly.

**Strongest passages.** §13.1 in full. The 412/406 exclusion. "They are not 206 physical events." The independent-provenance paragraph for |C| = 3.

**Notation.** x = 2/9 vs §12's α (collision X1 — fix here). Γ, U, C_root clean.

**Transitions.** Entry states the section's two tasks; exit hands (q_min, s_409·q_mid, q_max) to §14 cleanly.

**Credibility risks.** Concentrated in middle-only placement (above). Secondary: the reflexivity of 206 (the middle ratio generates the count that corrects the middle ratio) will strike some readers as bootstrap-flavored; the construction is well-defined (the intrinsic ratio exists before the screen and is recoverable by dividing out s_409), and one sentence acknowledging and resolving the apparent circularity ("the screen consumes the completed count, not the ratio's value; the intrinsic ratio remains the recoverable source") — the paper effectively has this in §13.4's recovery statement — could be sharpened to defuse it explicitly.

**Verified issues.** CI-5 (middle-only placement argument absent from public materials) — the review's principal finding.

**Smallest improvements.**
1. **Add a placement paragraph to §13.4** (2–4 sentences): why the middle rank and only the middle rank receives the screen, or an explicit typing of the placement as a constitutive identification with its falsifier restated in-text.
2. Unify the phase symbol with §12 (α).
3. Optional: one sentence explicitly defusing the 206-reflexivity appearance.
