# Section 8 — Observer and Readout Architecture

**Purpose.** Define the readout locus, registration, the observer/reference role, clockhood, the time and support readouts, the observer-scale reporting map, and the energy correspondence; certify structural completeness while refusing the measured-physics unlock.

**Comprehension assessment.** High, and centrally important: this is the section that must carry the title's meaning. The observer is defined operationally with complete clarity ("The word 'observer' is therefore not a psychological word here. It names a relational role"). The locus/registration/reference progression is well staged; clockhood as a *role* ("Nothing is inherently a clock") is one of the paper's most quotable and defensible moves. The reciprocal pair (t(L), s(L)) with m_base·t_base = I preserved under reporting is clean.

**The asymmetry passage (§8.3) is the paper's hidden keystone.** "By occupying the reference role, X selects which oriented side is read as reference. The resulting asymmetry is an orientation selection, not a signed amount and not an act of creation." This is the only place the title's "asymmetry" is explained — and the title phrase "observer-rooted" is never assembled anywhere in the body (terminology ledger). A reader who reaches §8.3 alert will connect it; many will not. **One paragraph, here or in §1/Introduction, explicitly assembling the title — the asymmetry is rooted in the observer's occupation of the reference role; what is intrinsic (the unordered spectrum, the ratios) vs what is observer-relative (the ordered presentation, the readout) — is the single most valuable presentational addition available to this paper.**

**Reader friction.** (1) §8.6/§8.8's active-m vs m_base distinction is stated twice and still demands attention; the non-circularity fence (§8.8, "That substitution would put the time extent back into the support source") is important and dense — it defends against a real circularity accusation and deserves its own breathing room. (2) "S02 book" recurs twice here (leakage). (3) §8.9's structural-completion passage names "the time-bridge checklist," "the seventh item," "the lens-calibration identity," and "a P0 prediction" — four internal referents in two paragraphs, none publicly defined; this is the paper's densest leakage cluster. The *content* (structural completeness certified; measured-physics unlock refused; predictions inadmissible until comparison laws are derived) is valuable and clearly stated around the tags.

**Strongest passages.** §8.3; §8.4 (clockhood); the §8.8 non-circularity fence; the section boundary list.

**Notation.** 𝒟(L), reg, Obs, t(L), s(L) clean. E = m·Cap restated with c⊗2 fenced from c².

**Persuasive strengths.** The section makes "observer language does not imply consciousness" structurally true rather than merely asserted — the observer has a defined type signature.

**Credibility risks.** The leakage cluster (§8.9–8.10) is the worst in the paper; a referee hitting "P0 prediction" and "lens-calibration identity" will correctly infer an internal research apparatus and may question the paper's self-containment.

**Verified issues.** Feeds CI-7 (leakage cluster), CI-6 (title assembly).

**Smallest improvements.**
1. **Add the title-assembly paragraph at §8.3 (or Introduction).**
2. Rewrite §8.9–8.10 dropping "P0", "lens-calibration identity", and "the seventh item" in favor of plain descriptions (the checklist can be named descriptively: "the nine-point readout-specification contract stated in §1.4's terms").
3. Replace "S02 book" occurrences (with §3's public name).
