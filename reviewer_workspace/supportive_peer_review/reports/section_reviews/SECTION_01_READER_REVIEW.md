# Section 1 — Relational Existence: The Foundational Axiom

**Purpose.** Install the single axiom (existence is relational distinguishability), derive I = 1, and fix the admission rules (positivity, no zero object, no signed primitives, no background, measurement as comparison) that every later layer must respect.

**What the reader has / what remains.** Nothing yet; the section is the entry gate. After it: the axiom, I = 1, the number-class discipline, the no-background rule, the measurement admissibility rule, and the program statement (§1.6).

**Comprehension assessment.** High. The prose is calm and unusually careful. The 0/1 boundary argument (§1.1), the cancellation-as-theorem proof sketch (§1.2), and the measurement admissibility formalism (§1.4) are all followable by the intended reader without external material. §1.2's distinction between primitive admission and downstream exact representations ("number class alone neither grants nor denies admissibility") is load-bearing for the whole paper (it later licenses 2π, √2, and complex phases) and is stated clearly here — an important early move that many readers will only appreciate retroactively; a forward pointer ("this licensing is used at §§2.8, 4.2, 12") would help.

**Strongest passages.** The falsifiability paragraph ("This foundation can also lose, and it says how") — starting the paper's signature discipline on page 5. The Alice/Bob apples passage as gentle on-ramp. §1.4's ¬∃q(PrimitiveScalar…) display, which makes the anti-primitive-scalar stance precise rather than rhetorical.

**Reader friction.** (1) §1.1's move from "something exists" to "existence is relational distinguishability" is philosophically compressed: the argument that existence *never* arrives as a bare object gets three sentences. A skeptic can grant it as an axiom (which is all the paper needs) — but the text presents it partly as argued, partly as posited; one sentence separating "motivation" from "axiom status" would sharpen it. (2) The section-numbering seam: §1.4 mentions "especially in Section 8" and "Sections 8 and 14" — accurate, and welcome.

**Notation.** I, 𝒟⁺, Adm/Std/Cmp/Meas introduced cleanly. No issues.

**Transitions.** Ends with an explicit scope statement. Good.

**Persuasive strengths.** The section reads as philosophy done with an engineer's discipline; the failure-mode paragraph buys significant credibility.

**Credibility risks.** Low within the section. The single-axiom claim is the paper's largest promissory note; §1 wisely does not overdraw it.

**Verified issues.** None at Level ≥3. Level 1: Σ_rel-style items do not occur here; no undefined symbols.

**Smallest improvements.**
1. One sentence distinguishing the axiom's motivation from its status as posit (optional).
2. Forward pointer from the number-class licensing paragraph to its later uses (optional, cheap, high value).
