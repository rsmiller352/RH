# Section 10 — Count Flow and the Scoped C4 Invariant

**Purpose.** Derive the observer-side flow count S_flow = (c−1)/c = 2/3 from the four-cell reference frame; record it as a separate invariant; and — the section's real mission — separate it from the numerically equal projector/action value ν(Π_o) = 2/3 that the spectral construction uses.

**Comprehension assessment.** Good. The derivation of S_flow is short and honest (one co-present indistinguishable cell; c−1 = 2 distinguishable out of c = 3 read cells; reference-choice independence checked). The separation message is delivered three times (in §10.3, §10.4, and later §15.2), which is the right level of redundancy for the paper's most dangerous numerical coincidence.

**Why this section earns its place.** Two independently derived 2/3's, one of which becomes the spectral phase and the other of which resembles the Koide benchmark, is precisely the configuration a numerology-alert referee is trained to attack. The paper's decision to surface the coincidence itself, type both objects, and deny the identification *before* any critic can raise it is tactically and scientifically correct. This anticipatory-disambiguation pattern is among the manuscript's strongest credibility assets.

**Reader friction.** (1) The section title's "C4" tag is never expanded into words ("the four-cell frame invariant") in a definition sentence; the reader assembles it from §10.2 (ledger U5, mild form). (2) "It neither displays nor uses lower C4 carrier assignments; their broader interpretation and publication treatment lie outside the present derivation" — this sentence speaks to an internal audience about internal objects (lower carrier assignments) the public reader has never heard of; it raises questions it refuses to answer. Dropping it, or replacing with "no further structure of the four-cell frame is used in this paper," loses nothing. (3) §10.4's forward use of the §12 apparatus (Π_o, P, ν) is noted in the symbol audit (U4); a one-line pointer would fix it.

**Strongest passages.** "Their numerical equality does not identify the two typed objects." The reference-choice independence check in §10.2 — small, but exactly the kind of check that builds trust.

**Notation.** S_flow, C4inv fine; forward-used spectral symbols flagged.

**Transitions.** Good.

**Credibility risks.** Low — the section *reduces* risk; its residual exposures are the internal-audience sentence and the unexpanded tag.

**Verified issues.** Feeds CI-7 (C4 tag; internal-audience sentence).
