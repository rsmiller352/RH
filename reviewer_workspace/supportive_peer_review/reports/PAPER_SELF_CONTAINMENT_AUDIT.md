## Division-of-labor assessment
The intended division (narrative, load-bearing statements, exclusions, calibration logic in the paper; algebraic machinery, expanded certificates, proof mechanics, traceability in the cards) is **achieved almost everywhere** — e.g., §13.1 states the Taylor-bound degrees and Pell certificates while the card carries the exact rational endpoints and margin certificates: exactly right. No case was found of detailed symbolic machinery that should move *into* the paper wholesale; every REQUIRED item above is a narrative sentence or paragraph, not a derivation transplant. Conversely, nothing in the manuscript is card-bloat that should move out.

## Reader-experience test
Applying the charter's ideal ("I completely understood the scientific argument from the manuscript; the cards made me more confident every omitted step was verified"): the first clause currently fails at three points (γ_Ψ origin; 1/√2; middle placement) and strains at one (t_base = 2π). The second clause fails for a different reason — the reader is never told the cards exist, and if handed them, meets the governance-block problem (leakage audit B).
