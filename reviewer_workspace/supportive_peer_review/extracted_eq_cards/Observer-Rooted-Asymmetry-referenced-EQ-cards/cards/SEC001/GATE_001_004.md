## 1) Plain language

S06 (the Junction) is the section that proposes the framework's central conjecture: that the
substrate's **update accounting** and the aperture's **phase accounting** are one bookkeeping — update
rate = phase rate. That conjecture is hard, NEW-WORK, and comes **last**. Before any of it, this card
does the disciplined thing: it writes down, up front, **exactly what would have to be proved** for the
junction to hold, and **what later cards are allowed to attempt** — and it proves none of it.

Two pieces. First, an **inherited contract**: the corpus already has a 9-item checklist for any
"bridge from internal bookkeeping to physical time" (source variable, target, units, calibration,
clock target, domain, relativistic status, failure condition, dependency direction) — until all nine
are met, things like time-dilation and measured rates stay **blocked**. We import that checklist as
the standard the junction must meet. Second, **eight junction-specific obligations OB-A…OB-H**: build
the tick↔closure map (and let it be able to fail), prove the rate equality, define the cost operator
before any energy symbol, **prove-or-refute** whether the discrete ±1 phase is a reduction of the
continuous one (never assume it), keep the inherited guards, allow no per-system constants, audit
E=mc² for circularity, and compile the substrate with zero observer inputs.

This card just **registers** all of that as OPEN, points each obligation at the card that will discharge
it, and fixes the order (the conjecture last). It also carries the **seam safety rails** from S05 into
S06 — most importantly that the accumulator reading "zero" means **"no carried remainder, account
open,"** a typed predicate, and **never** the forbidden I = 0 object.
