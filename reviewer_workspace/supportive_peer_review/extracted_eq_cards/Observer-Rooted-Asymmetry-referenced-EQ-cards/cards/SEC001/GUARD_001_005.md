# No sign group in disguise (F-153b)

## 0) Governance & Scope Lock

Prohibition card (GUARD): states one scope firewall, once. Other cards REFERENCE this
card; they never restate it (TD-013 anti-litany rule — duplicated prohibition blocks are a
validator warning). This card derives nothing, depends only on the convention whose hazard
it fences, and is never load-bearing for any computation.

## 1) Plain language

The labels that record direction are not allowed to become arithmetic. The moment two
"reversals" are declared to multiply out to "forward," the minus sign has been rebuilt
out of labels — same algebra, new costume — and the whole point of keeping quantity
strictly positive is lost. [Wording amended 2026-06-12 — D-046 proposal 4 (P3 v6
vocabulary, D-044); was "keeping quantity nonnegative"; no obligation, proof, or witness
content touched.]

## 2) Statement (the prohibition, stated once)

No internal card may equip orientation tags (defined at DEF_002_002) with a composition law under
which the composite of two reversals equals the identity reading — in ANY notation:
(−)·(−) = (+), σ² = e, a ℤ/2 action on tags, parity multiplication, or an equivalent
presentation. Such a law is the sign group imported under a new name (F-153b; TD-002 in
costume) and voids the P3 representation the tags exist to protect.

**Licensed alternatives (the only two):**
1. Tags as pure bookkeeping with no arithmetic at all (DEF_002_002's defined rule).
2. A relationally justified composition — derived from relation-swaps as re-description,
   arriving as a NEW card that argues the justification explicitly and is reviewed against
   this guard before adoption.

**Exemption:** BRIDGE cards (P7) — signed notation is licensed there, with its translation
map; this guard governs internal cards only.

## 5) Scope & non-overclaim

Fences tag algebra only. Does not prohibit orientation tags (DEF_002_002 licenses them),
does not prohibit BRIDGE-side signs, does not adjudicate the adequacy program
(DEF_002_004 §8). Referenced by: DEF_S01_005/007, AX_S01_006, DEF_002_002, DEF_002_016
(generated downstream list will confirm).

## 6) Per-symbol accounting

Local: none introduced (σ, e, ℤ/2 appear as quoted hazard notation inside the prohibition,
mention not use). Imported: σ (the tag concept) ← DEF_002_002. Undeclared: none.

## 7) Verification

Not applicable — structural prohibition. Mechanical support: the validator's TD-002
reversion scan and the promotion checklist's notation-discipline item ([M] scan + [H]
residue) enforce what this card states; any witness would be a lexical scan wearing a
verification label (TD-014), so none is specified.

## 9) Notes

If a future card legitimately needs composed orientations (e.g. walk concatenation
semantics meeting oriented transfers), the licensed route is alternative 2 of §2 — a new
card with a relational derivation, never a silent tag algebra. The free-group machinery
once contemplated for C2 (now preserved only as a documented, not-installed historical
alternative at retired CONV_S01_003 §4 alternative 2, post-D-027/D-064) composes WALKS,
not tags; that distinction is exactly the boundary this guard keeps visible.
