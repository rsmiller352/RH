# No measurement without a relational comparison standard — the S01 measurement-admissibility gate. A measurement is NOT a primitive scalar; it is an observer/readout COMPARISON of an admitted distinguishable relation-token x against an admitted relational comparison standard s. Meas_L(x;s)↓ ⟺ Adm(x) ∧ Std_L(s) ∧ Cmp_L(x,s), with Std_L(s) ⇒ Adm(s); the bare form Meas_L(x) is ill-typed (no measurement of x alone). A unit is a selected standard-role Unit_L(s):=Adm(s)∧Std_L(s); a reported value q_s(x) is defined only if the comparison is licensed; no_entry (the non-admission boundary marker, never admitted) can never be a standard. Change of standard is a unit change only if it preserves the recoverable comparison content. S01-CLEAN: defines no physical unit, introduces no second/gram/meter/kilogram/joule/SI/clock/calibration constant/smooth magnitude, derives no SI scale, performs no calibration proof. Named unit systems appear ONLY as fenced forward-pointers (GUARD_001_006: pointers only, never load-bearing): SI unit ≠ primitive admitted relation-token; any SI report enters LATER as admitted relational content + admitted comparison standard + bridge/calibration convention. Gates every downstream use of a named unit / smooth magnitude / observer-scale value before distinguishability + a comparison standard are admitted.

## 0) Governance & Scope Lock

**GATE card.** This card does **not** define physical units, does not introduce seconds, grams, meters, kilograms, joules, clocks, SI, calibration constants, or smooth physical magnitudes. It states the upstream admissibility rule:

> A measurement is **not** a primitive scalar.
> A measurement is an observer/readout **comparison** against an **admitted relational comparison standard**.

**Layer ceiling S01 (S01-clean).** The gate's content is justified only by layers ≤ S01 (`AX_001_001`, `THM_002_007`); it imports no bridge-layer apparatus, derives no SI scale, performs no calibration proof, and treats no physical unit as an object. Named unit systems appear only as **fenced forward-pointers** (`GUARD_001_006`: pointers only, never load-bearing). This card may be cited later to **block** any use of named units or smooth magnitudes before distinguishability and a comparison standard have been admitted.

## 1) Plain language

Measurement is comparison.

A length is read against a stick. A weight is read against another weight. A named unit is a **selected comparison standard**, not a thing mathematics produces by itself.

So the theory cannot begin with "seconds," "kilograms," "joules," or any smooth measured value as primitive. The theory begins with relational distinguishability. Only after something is admitted as distinguishable can another admitted relation be selected as the **standard** against which it is compared.

A unit is therefore a **reporting role**. It is not fundamental existence.

## 2) Statement

Let `Adm(x)` mean that `x` is admitted into the theory as a distinguishable relation-token, in the sense of `AX_001_001`.

Let `Std_L(s)` mean that, for readout locus or comparison context `L`, the admitted item `s` is selected to serve as a relational comparison standard.

Let `Cmp_L(x,s)` mean that `x` is read against `s` by a licensed comparison relation.

Let `Meas_L(x;s)` mean a measurement-report of `x` in the comparison standard `s`.

From `THM_002_007`, the first admitted distinguishability has count

$$
I = 1 .
$$

The measurement gate is:

$$
\operatorname{Meas}_L(x;s)\downarrow
\iff
\operatorname{Adm}(x)
\wedge
\operatorname{Std}_L(s)
\wedge
\operatorname{Cmp}_L(x,s).
$$

The comparison standard must itself be admitted:

$$
\operatorname{Std}_L(s) \Rightarrow \operatorname{Adm}(s).
$$

So:

$$
\operatorname{Meas}_L(x;s)\downarrow
\Rightarrow
\operatorname{Adm}(x)
\wedge
\operatorname{Adm}(s)
\wedge
\operatorname{Cmp}_L(x,s).
$$

And the forbidden primitive-scalar form is:

$$
\neg \exists q\,
\big(
\operatorname{PrimitiveScalar}(q)
\wedge
q = \operatorname{Meas}_L(x)
\big).
$$

There is no measurement "of `x` alone." There is only a report of `x` relative to an admitted standard `s`. The bare `Meas_L(x)` is **ill-typed** — it does not denote, so no primitive scalar `q` can equal it (that is the `¬∃q` statement above). The well-typed form is `Meas_L(x;s)`.

## 3) Unit Role

A unit is a selected standard-role:

$$
\operatorname{Unit}_L(s)
:=
\operatorname{Adm}(s)
\wedge
\operatorname{Std}_L(s).
$$

A reported value in that unit has the form

$$
\operatorname{Report}_L(x;s)
=
q_s(x) ,
$$

where `q_s(x)` is **not primitive**. It is defined only if the comparison is licensed:

$$
q_s(x)\downarrow
\iff
\operatorname{Adm}(x)
\wedge
\operatorname{Unit}_L(s)
\wedge
\operatorname{Cmp}_L(x,s).
$$

If any input is missing, the report is undefined:

$$
\neg\operatorname{Adm}(x)
\vee
\neg\operatorname{Unit}_L(s)
\vee
\neg\operatorname{Cmp}_L(x,s)
\Rightarrow
q_s(x)\uparrow.
$$

**no_entry cannot serve as a standard.** By the gate, `Std_L(s) ⇒ Adm(s)`. The marker `no_entry` is the **non-admission boundary** (`AX_001_001`): it is never an admitted object, so `¬Adm(no_entry)`. Therefore `no_entry` can never satisfy `Std_L`, and a fortiori never `Unit_L`. (`no_entry` is a boundary marker, never an assignable value and never quantified over — P1/TD-001; it is not a zero-object — TD-026.)

## 4) Report-Equivalence Under Change of Standard

If two standards `s` and `s'` are both admitted comparison standards for the same underlying readout, then changing the standard changes the **report**, not the admitted underlying relation.

Let `C_L(x;s)` denote the underlying admitted comparison content recoverable from the report of `x` in standard `s`. Then:

$$
\operatorname{Report}_L(x;s) \sim \operatorname{Report}_L(x;s')
\iff
C_L(x;s) = C_L(x;s').
$$

A change of reporting standard is admissible only if it preserves the recoverable comparison content:

$$
s \leadsto s'
\text{ admissible}
\Rightarrow
C_L(x;s) = C_L(x;s').
$$

If the recoverable content is not preserved, the transformation is not a unit change; it is a different claim:

$$
C_L(x;s) \neq C_L(x;s')
\Rightarrow
\operatorname{Report}_L(x;s)
\not\sim
\operatorname{Report}_L(x;s').
$$

## 5) Gate Consequence

No later card may use a named unit, calibrated scale, smooth magnitude, or observer-scale value as primitive.

A later measurement/readout card must provide:

1. an admitted target `x`;
2. an admitted comparison standard `s`;
3. a licensed comparison relation `Cmp_L(x,s)`;
4. a report map from the comparison into the chosen standard;
5. recoverability of the underlying count/closure content if the report is used as evidence of theory-native structure.

In short:

$$
\text{measured value}
\neq
\text{primitive fact},
\qquad
\text{measured value}
=
\text{comparison report over admitted distinguishable structure}.
$$

## 6) Scope & Non-Overclaim

This card proves no physical unit and derives no SI scale.

It does not say seconds, grams, meters, kilograms, or joules are false. It says they are **later observer-scale reporting conventions**. They may enter only through a bridge or calibration card that states the comparison standard and preserves the dependency direction.

This card also does not define clockhood. A later clockhood card may instantiate this same pattern, but this S01 gate does not depend on clockhood.

## 7) SI Non-Primitivity Forward Pointer

This S01 gate does **not** derive, define, or calibrate SI units.

Seconds, kilograms, joules, meters, and other named unit systems are **not** admitted here as theory-native objects. At S01 they may be **named only** as later observer-scale reporting conventions.

A later bridge/calibration card may prove the non-uniqueness of such reporting conventions by an explicit rescaling argument. **That proof is not load-bearing here** (`GUARD_001_006`: forward references are pointers only).

S01 claim:

$$
\text{SI unit} \;\neq\; \text{primitive admitted relation-token}.
$$

Any SI report must enter later as:

$$
\text{admitted relational content}
\;+\;
\text{admitted comparison standard}
\;+\;
\text{bridge/calibration convention}.
$$

## 8) Falsifiers

- **F-S01G24-1.** A licensed construction admits a measurement-report `Meas_L(x)` with no comparison standard.
- **F-S01G24-2.** A licensed construction admits a unit standard `s` where `s` is not admitted as distinguishable (`Std_L(s)` without `Adm(s)`).
- **F-S01G24-3.** A licensed construction uses `no_entry` as a unit / comparison standard.
- **F-S01G24-4.** A later card uses seconds, kilograms, joules, meters, or any named unit as primitive input before a comparison/calibration bridge.
- **F-S01G24-5.** A later card changes units while changing the recoverable underlying comparison content and still claims equivalence.
- **F-S01G24-6.** This S01 gate is read as deriving/defining/calibrating an SI unit, or as importing bridge-layer rescaling apparatus load-bearingly.

## 9) Witness Spec

Structural/logical witness. Model admitted tokens `A` (target) and `S` (standard) as predicate inputs and evaluate the gate condition.

Pass case:

$$
\operatorname{Adm}(A)=\text{true},\quad
\operatorname{Adm}(S)=\text{true},\quad
\operatorname{Std}_L(S)=\text{true},\quad
\operatorname{Cmp}_L(A,S)=\text{true}
\;\Rightarrow\;
\operatorname{Meas}_L(A;S)\downarrow .
$$

Fail modes (each flips exactly one check; `--matrix` bijection, `--corrupt` catches):

- **Mode A:** no standard supplied; `Meas_L(A;S)` must be undefined.
- **Mode B:** keep `S` but `Adm(S)=false` while `Std_L(S)=true`; violates `Std⇒Adm`; measurement undefined.
- **Mode C:** the candidate standard is the non-admission boundary marker `no_entry` (`¬Adm`); the unit role must be rejected (`Std⇒Adm`).
- **Mode D:** assert a primitive scalar `Meas_L(A)` with no `S`; witness must fail (ill-typed).
- **Mode E:** change standard `S→S'` while changing recoverable content `C_L(A)` and still claim report-equivalence; witness must fail.
- **Mode F:** `Cmp_L(A,S)=false` (no licensed comparison); report `q_S(A)` undefined.
- **Mode G:** `Adm(A)=false` (target not admitted); measurement undefined.
- **Mode H:** treat an SI unit as a primitive admitted relation-token (deny the §7 forward-pointer); witness must fail.

## 10) Downstream Use

This card should be cited whenever a later section needs to say:

- units are reports, not primitives;
- calibration is a bridge, not a derivation from bare math;
- smooth measured values must be recoverable back to admitted count/closure structure before they can be used as theory evidence.

It is closeout card #1 of the OI-083 sequence (SI as observer-scale convention). The recoverable-count-index definition, the recovery-before-scaling gate, and the Koide count-recovery cards consume this gate and are supplied separately.
