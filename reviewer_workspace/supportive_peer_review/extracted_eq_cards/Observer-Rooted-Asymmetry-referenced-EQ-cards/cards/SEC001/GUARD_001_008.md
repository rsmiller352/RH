## 1) Plain language

Nothing in this book is ever booked as zero — and the book does not even own a zero to
book. Where old mathematics writes "the transfer was zero," this book says **no transfer
occurred**. Where it writes "zero events," this book says **no event exists**. Where it
writes "the balance difference is zero," this book says **the paired entries match** —
balance is checked by pairing every reading with its co-reading, never by subtracting to
a number. Where it writes "the residue is zero," this book says **no unpaired residue
remains**. Where it writes "the count is zero," this book says **the record is absent**.
An emptied register does not hold zero; it leaves the book. A ledger with no history does
not contain zero entries; no entries exist yet. Zero keeps exactly one honest job at the
ground: marking the boundary of non-existence — a label at the edge of the book, never a
thing inside it. A proposed rule that types a quantity "≥ 0" or books a zero has let old
mathematics' habit back in; the rule is the suspect, never this discipline.

## 2) Statement (the prohibition, stated once)

**THE PROHIBITION (D-043 typed-predicate strengthening, RSM, verbatim):**

> "The internal spine does not admit zero as a default quantity object. Zero may not
> appear in declared codomains, event registers, transfer quantities, payloads, or
> ledger objects. Absence, empty history, matched balance, and no-residue conditions
> must be expressed by typed predicates, not by a zero value."

Typings of the form "x ≥ 0" on a declared quantity are invalid; declared quantities are
**strictly positive by type** (the gate-pending DEF_S01_005/DEF_S01_007 amendments make
this literal: q ∈ 𝒟+, holdings valued in 𝒟+ on supports). A zero-valued declaration
books a non-event: it asserts the existence of nothing (grounding below).

**The five typed-predicate replacements (LICENSED CARD VOCABULARY — these phrases, or
their formal forms, replace zero-phrasings on every internal card):**

1. "zero transfer" → **no-transfer-occurred** (no post-state, no booking; DEF_002_004's
   refusal and ill-typed paths).
2. "zero event" → **no-event-exists** (nothing posted; nothing to read from either end).
3. "balance difference equals zero" → **the-paired-entries-match** (balance is proved by
   PAIRING, never subtraction — "Every source-reading has its exact target co-reading…
   Balance is: nothing is left unpaired." The check is a PREDICATE, not a number).
4. "residue is zero" → **no-unpaired-residue** (nothing is left unpaired).
5. "multiplicity is zero" / "count is zero" → **record-absent** (x ∉ supp(X); an absent
   record has NO multiplicity value — the comparison is membership, not arithmetic).
   Instantiated at holdings as the **register-absence predicate** (r ∉ supp(h): the
   register is not in the book).

"That avoids letting zero become a hidden object" (D-043).

**The readout/declaration split, RE-EXPRESSED (Q-c upgraded by the strengthening).** The
first draft licensed "computed zero readouts." Superseded text, quoted: *"COMPUTED
readouts may read zero — that IS the balancing role: multiplicities in balance
comparisons, closure residuals, totals agreeing, refusal counts, an emptied holding."*
Under the strengthening, those computed checks return **TYPED PREDICATES, not zero
values**: the paired-balance check IS predicate 3 (a verdict, not a number); a closure
residual reads predicate 4; an emptied holding is the register-absence case of predicate
5 (the register leaves the book — partial holdings, the gate-pending DEF_002_001
amendment; Q-b's "h(r) = 0_𝒟 survives as a computed balance fact … DEF_002_001 stands
unamended" is SUPERSEDED by RSM's own directive). What survives of the split: only
DECLARED internal objects are governed by the ban's five named surfaces (codomains,
event registers, transfer quantities, payloads, ledger objects); checking and verifying
remain free — they simply speak in predicates.
