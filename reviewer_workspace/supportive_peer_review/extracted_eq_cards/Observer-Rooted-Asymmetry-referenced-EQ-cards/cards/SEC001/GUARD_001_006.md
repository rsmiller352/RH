# Layer discipline — strict construction order (TD-020 as a card)

## 0) Governance & Scope Lock

Prohibition card (GUARD): states the construction-order firewall, once. Other cards
REFERENCE this card (TD-013); they never restate it as litany. Never load-bearing for any
computation; structurally enforced (validator layer lint) plus human-audited (checklist
item 10).

## 1) Plain language

The book is built in strict order from I = 1. Nothing early may be explained by anything
late — not even when the numbers match beautifully. If a later structure gives an earlier
constant new meaning, the later layer may say so THERE, by re-deriving it; it may never
reach back and become the earlier card's reason. A matching number is a coincidence until
the chain, read forward, makes it a consequence.

## 2) Statement (the prohibition, stated once)

1. An object at layer n may be **defined, justified, or semantically explained** only by
   what exists at layers ≤ n.
2. Forward references are **POINTERS only**: explicitly marked as such, never load-bearing
   ("meaning earned at S0k" is legal; "true because of S0k" is not).
3. **Numerically matching later structure is never citable as derivation** — the founding
   instance is the voided /4 ↔ four-cell fit (TD-020); the ruled instance is D-025.R10
   (four-cell facts are S03/S04-layer facts and flow forward only).
4. A later layer may **RE-derive or reinterpret** an earlier object explicitly, at its own
   layer — never retroactively ground it (cf. S05.5 re-deriving the unit: legal exactly
   because S01 never cites it).
5. Dependency edges and semantic story must run the SAME direction: a chain whose formal
   arrows point one way while its explanation points the other is in violation even if the
   graph is acyclic.

## 5) Scope & non-overclaim

Governs justification and semantics, not curiosity: exploratory notes may look anywhere
(over-extraction policy, D-020); CARDS obey the order. The guard does not order layers'
importance and does not prohibit later layers from giving early objects richer meaning —
only from exporting that meaning backward.

## 6) Per-symbol accounting

Local: none. Imported: none (the layer index n is card-local schema vocabulary, not a
quantity symbol). Undeclared: none.

## 7) Verification

Not applicable — structural prohibition. Mechanical support: validator layer lint (every
dependency's layer ≤ the card's layer) and the TD-020 reversion-pattern scan; semantic
enforcement is the promotion checklist's construction-order audit ([H], with F-117 as the
audit's worked precedent).

## 9) Notes

Every consuming S01 card's forbidden_inputs block references this guard instead of restating it;
the validator's anti-litany warning (TD-013) is the mechanical check that the referencing
stays referencing.
