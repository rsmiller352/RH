# Mathematics licensing — the P4 adjudication card (D-024.1 verbatim)

## 0) Governance & Scope Lock

Axiom card carrying an RSM-verbatim ruling as its core; the quote may not be paraphrased,
trimmed, or "improved." Adjudication content (§4) disposes of one named objection and
does nothing else. No geometric object is constructed or consumed here. Layer ceiling S01.

## 1) Plain language

Why is geometry the licensed mathematics of this book? Because geometry is the mathematics
in which nothing exists except by its relations — which is exactly the framework's ground
(P3: quantity is relational; P5: the observer is the relationship). The license says which
mathematics may carry the construction. It does not say the universe IS geometry — that
question stays open, on purpose.

## 2) Statement

**Core (D-024.1, RSM verbatim, axiom-grade):**

> "Geometry isn't reality; it is the mathematics of relation. That is why it is so
> powerful for physics. A point alone says almost nothing. It becomes meaningful only
> inside a structure that gives it relations — distance, adjacency, order, curvature, or
> connection. In that sense, there is no physically meaningful point without the graph,
> space, or field it belongs to."

**Licensing consequence (P4):** geometry is licensed as **relational mathematics** — the
internal mathematical substrate in which relationships are structurally maintained;
non-geometric formalisms may appear only in service of geometric statements or inside
BRIDGE cards.
