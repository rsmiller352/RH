## 4) Proof

### Step 1 — unique maximal inball

Write the promoted RD cell as

\[
C_u=
\bigcap_{n\in S_{12}}
\{p:n\cdot p\le 2u\},
\qquad
\lVert n\rVert=\sqrt2,
\qquad
r=\sqrt2u.
\]

Let an arbitrary inscribed ball have center `z` and radius `R`. For each
antipodal normal pair `n,-n`, containment requires

\[
n\cdot z+R\sqrt2\le2u,
\qquad
-n\cdot z+R\sqrt2\le2u.
\]

Adding gives

\[
2R\sqrt2\le4u,
\qquad
R\le\sqrt2u=r.
\]

If `R=r`, the first inequality gives `n dot z<=0` and the second gives
`n dot z>=0`, hence `n dot z=0` for every `n` in `S_12`. The normals span
three-space; for example `(1,1,0)`, `(1,-1,0)`, and `(1,0,1)` are linearly
independent. Therefore `z=0`. The centered radius-`r` ball is the unique
maximal inball. QED.

### Step 2 — exact RD boundary area

`THM_004_007` gives twelve congruent rhombic facets, each of area

\[
2\sqrt2\,u^2.
\]

Thus

\[
\operatorname{Area}(\partial C_u)
=24\sqrt2\,u^2.
\]

Substituting `u=r/sqrt(2)` gives

\[
\operatorname{Area}
(\partial\operatorname{Cell}_\lambda(r))
=12\sqrt2\,r^2.
\]

### Step 3 — exact sphere boundary area

For the centered radius-`r` sphere, the exact spherical parametrization has
area element

\[
r^2\sin\theta\,d\theta\,d\phi.
\]

Therefore

\[
\operatorname{Area}(\partial B_\lambda(r))
=
\int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^\pi
r^2\sin\theta\,d\theta\,d\phi
=4\pi r^2.
\]

This is model-internal Euclidean area, not an SI measurement.

### Step 4 — dual-measure coherence

Using the promoted volumes and Steps 2–3,

\[
\frac{\operatorname{Vol}(B_\lambda(r))}
{\operatorname{Vol}(\operatorname{Cell}_\lambda(r))}
=
\frac{(4/3)\pi r^3}{4\sqrt2r^3}
=
\frac{\pi}{3\sqrt2},
\]

and

\[
\frac{\operatorname{Area}(\partial B_\lambda(r))}
{\operatorname{Area}(\partial\operatorname{Cell}_\lambda(r))}
=
\frac{4\pi r^2}{12\sqrt2r^2}
=
\frac{\pi}{3\sqrt2}.
\]

Both bodies are centered and tangential at distance `r`. For the RD cell,
coning every facet to the center gives `V=(r/3)A`; for the sphere the exact
volume and area formulas give the same identity. Hence the matching normalized
measures have a common structural reason and are not target-matched numerical
coincidences. QED.

### Step 5 — volume complement versus boundary ratio

volume-share split. The two boundaries meet only at the twelve tangency points;
the sphere boundary is not a subset of the RD boundary. Therefore the boundary
`Omega` equality is a complementary ratio only, never a complementary surface
patch. QED.
