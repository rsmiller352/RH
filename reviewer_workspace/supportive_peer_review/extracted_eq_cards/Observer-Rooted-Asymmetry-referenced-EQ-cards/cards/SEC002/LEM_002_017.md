# The No-Terminal-Book lemma — no licensed operation carries a book with admitted content to a book with none: every licensed act conserves the admitted total (moves, accounts, closes — never annihilates), so admitted existence has no licensed exit and a non-empty book stays non-empty under every finite licensed composition — Recurrence Wave R4

## 1) Plain language

Once the book holds something, no legal move empties it. Every operation the framework
licenses is a transfer-shaped act: it moves an amount from one register to another, closes
an account into its paired record, or discharges it into its accounted destination — and in
every case the books say where the amount went. Nothing licensed destroys quantity
(conservation), nothing licensed posts an unpaired record (symmetry balance), and "no-entry"
is not an operation at all — absence describes, it does not act. Even operations licensed in
the future are covered: the only door into the licensed set is the protocol that makes an
operation prove its own balance clause, so whatever comes through that door is preserving
too. Put together: admitted existence has no licensed exit. A book that holds one unit can
be rearranged forever; it can never be legally emptied.

## 4) Proof (complete — every step names its forcing card)

**Step 1 (single transfer preserves nonemptiness + total).** By DEF_002_004 an admissible
transfer T(A→B; q) requires A ∈ supp(h) with h(A) ⪰ q (a nonempty source; q ∈ 𝒟+ strictly
positive) and posts q into B: B ∈ supp(h′) by (B3). By LEM_002_005 (amended statement) the
tag-wise total is conserved and **both supports are nonempty** — "no empty sum is ever
formed (there is no value for one to take)." So one admissible transfer maps
nonempty-support states to nonempty-support states, total invariant. ∎(step)

**Step 2 (every licensed operation is transfer-shaped-and-balance-closed).** By THM_002_012
the licensed set 𝒪 is **balance-closed**: "every member of 𝒪 posts ι_or-complete increments
and refuses totally" — no member posts an unpaired record, and in particular no member drops
admitted quantity with no paired account (that operation is exactly what is never licensed —
THM_002_012 falsifier 2's D-042 protocol). With LEM_002_005's conservation over the
generating transfer family (Step 1), every member of 𝒪 conserves the admitted total and
preserves nonempty support. ∎(operations)

**Step 3 (the universe is stable under extension — TD-018 discharged).** THM_002_012 scopes
𝒪 "as of this wave." By LEM_002_013 Step 3 (consumed, not re-proved): a future operation
becomes licensed ONLY through the D-042 protocol, where it must prove **its own balance
clause** — hence any future-licensed operation is itself balance-closed, and Step 2's
conclusion covers it. An operation outside every balance-clause protocol is the D-042
diagnosed suspect (wrong / missing-operation / reversion-drift, TD-025), not a member of the
quantified universe. So the preservation conclusion holds for everything that is, or ever
becomes, licensed — with its universe stated. ∎(universe)

**Step 4 (finite compositions — clause (b)).** By induction on composition length (a
finite, order-theoretic induction over the book-order sequence — no temporal content): the
identity composition preserves trivially; if a length-k composition preserves nonempty
support and total, appending one licensed operation preserves both (Steps 1–3). By
LEM_002_005's own corollary the tag-wise total is invariant under every finite admissible
sequence. A book with admitted content has strictly-positive total (𝒟+ — no zero holdings
exist to sum); the empty book has NO holdings at all (the all-registers-absent predicate) —
and a composition reaching it from a strictly-positive-total state would destroy the
conserved total (no 𝒟+ value can be the total of no holdings — the empty sum is never
formed, LEM_002_005). No such licensed composition exists. ∎(b)

**Step 5 (exit-completeness — clause (c)).** The remaining conceivable exits are barred
upstream, consumed not re-proved: an UNLICENSED status-change (a spontaneous vanish) is
LEM_002_013's non-erasure violation (its F-NE1 class — the D-042 suspect); "vanish by
no-entry" is barred because **no-entry is not an operation** (LEM_002_013 Step 5 — absence
describes, it does not act). With (b) covering every licensed route: there is no route from
a book with admitted content to a book with none. ∎(c)

**What this proof does NOT use:** no S02+ object (pure S01 calculus); no zero-valued
holding (emptiness = support-absence predicate); no claim that unlicensed acts are
impossible-in-principle (they are the diagnosed suspect class — D-042/TD-025, outside the
quantifier); no temporal language (compositions are book-order sequences); no property of
the walk-erasure layer (§5's split).
