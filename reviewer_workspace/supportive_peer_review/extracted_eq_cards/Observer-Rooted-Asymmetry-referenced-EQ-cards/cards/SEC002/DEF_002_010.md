# Declared involutions, records, and the balance predicate — the L-41 typing card (pairing form, restriction-scoped)

## 0) Governance & Scope Lock

Definition card: types the declared-involution concept, the record object, and the balance
predicate, and proves their well-formedness — nothing else. Exact symbolic/combinatorial
forms only; all counts exact. This card contains NO theorem about what is balanced: it
classifies arbitrary record multisets, reachable or not (the independence half of the
TD-011 split; the law lives at THM_002_012). Banned here: tag arithmetic of any kind
(§4(e)); signed objects; reachability content; S02+ objects. Layer ceiling S01.

## 1) Plain language

A book entry can be read from either of its two ends: the side the amount left, and the
side it arrived. Swapping the reading does not change the entry — read it twice-swapped
and you are back where you started. This card gives that swap a name and a type, calls
each oriented reading a record, and defines what it means for a pile of records to be
balanced: every reading is matched by its co-reading, pile by pile — not merely "the grand
totals agree," which turns out to be a strictly weaker demand. Things the swap leaves
fixed (a walk that is its own reverse, for instance) are exempt from the matching demand:
nothing distinct exists for them to pair with.

## 2) Statement

**(a) Declared involution (the type).** Let 𝒱 be a typed S01 ledger-object family (every
element typed before use, TD-009). A **declared involution** on 𝒱 is a map ι : 𝒱 → 𝒱
with ι(ι(x)) = x for every x ∈ 𝒱, entering the spine only as re-description — the swap of
a relational reading, never an operation on tags and never an actor in quantity arithmetic
(license argued against GUARD_001_005 in §4(e)). An element x is **ι-fixed** iff
ι(x) = x; otherwise x belongs to the **distinct ι-pair** {x, ι(x)}. The predicate of (e)
below is schematic in (𝒱, ι) — the DEF_002_009 §2 scope-clause pattern; what never
widens with the instance: the typing requirement and the distinct-pair restriction
scoping.

**(b) Records (typed here, before any balance statement — TD-009).** Over a register set
R (DEF_002_001) and quantity cone 𝒟≥0, the **record vocabulary** is

Rec(R, 𝒟≥0) := { ⟨σ, A, B, q⟩ : A, B ∈ R, A ≠ B, q ∈ 𝒟+ (strictly positive BY TYPE — DEF_S01_005/DEF_S01_007 as amended 2026-06-12; dated notes below), tag-matched to the register family, σ ∈ {σ_src, σ_tgt} },

where σ_src and σ_tgt are card-local names for the two distinguished readings of the
transfer relation instance T(A→B; q) per DEF_002_002 §2 (source→target and
target→source — the same booking read from its two ends; the datum (A, B, q) is the
instance datum of DEF_002_004's transfer relation). A **record** is an element of
Rec(R, 𝒟≥0): one oriented reading of one transfer instance. Records are bookkeeping data,
not quantities: no arithmetic is defined on records.

**(c) The orientation involution ι_or** on Rec(R, 𝒟≥0):

ι_or⟨σ_src, A, B, q⟩ := ⟨σ_tgt, A, B, q⟩,  ι_or⟨σ_tgt, A, B, q⟩ := ⟨σ_src, A, B, q⟩.

The reading swaps; the instance datum is untouched (in particular ι_or does NOT reverse
the transfer's direction: ⟨σ_tgt, A, B, q⟩ reads the A→B transfer from B's end, and is a
different record from ⟨σ_src, B, A, q⟩, which reads a B→A transfer). Well-formedness —
involutivity and fixed-point-freeness — proved at §4(a).

**(d) The walk-reverse instance ι_rev (the existing declared involution, recorded).** On
DEF_002_009's walks, ι_rev(w) := w̄ (the reverse walk). Involutivity proved at §4(b);
ι_rev HAS fixed elements (ε, and every walk equal to its own reverse — e.g. every
out-and-back w·w̄), which is why clause (e)'s exemption is stated generally.

**(e) The balance predicate (pairing form, restriction-quantified; support-typed per the
D-043 strengthening, second dated note above).** A **finite multiset** X over 𝒱 with
declared involution ι is a pair (supp(X), mult_X) with supp(X) ⊆ 𝒱 finite and
**mult_X : supp(X) → ℕ≥1** — multiplicities exist only on the support and are strictly
positive by type. For x ∉ supp(X) **the record is ABSENT** (record-absent typed
predicate, GUARD_001_008): no multiplicity value exists, and no mult = 0 comparison
occurs anywhere on this card.

- A **restriction class** is a finite P ⊆ 𝒱 that is a union of DISTINCT ι-pairs:
  ι(P) = P and no element of P is ι-fixed. (F-179 precision note B, verbatim-adjacent:
  ι-fixed elements belong to no restriction class — they are **parity-exempt**, since no
  distinct partner exists for them to pair with; an odd count of a fixed element is never
  an imbalance. In the ι_or instance this clause is vacuous — no fixed record exists,
  §4(a); in the ι_rev instance it is real.)
- X is **ι-balanced** iff for EVERY restriction class P and every x ∈ P, either **both
  x and ι(x) are absent** from X (matched absence — record-absent on both sides), or
  **both are present** with mult_X(x) = mult_X(ι(x)) (the-paired-entries-match) — every
  record pairs with its ι-co-reading, restriction by restriction, and absence is matched
  by absence.
- **Equivalence (proved §4(c)):** X is ι-balanced ⟺ for every non-fixed x ∈ 𝒱:
  **(support symmetry)** x ∈ supp(X) ⟺ ι(x) ∈ supp(X) — "x absent ⟺ ι(x) absent" —
  AND **(matching)** mult_X(x) = mult_X(ι(x)) for every non-fixed x ∈ supp(X) (both
  sides exist by support symmetry).
- **Guard clause (F-179 precision note A, verbatim-adjacent).** The single-global-
  aggregate form — for the record instance: "the σ_src-resolved total of X equals the
  σ_tgt-resolved total" — is STRICTLY WEAKER than ι_or-balance and is NOT this spine's
  balance predicate. Separation exhibit with exact counts at §4(d); the assignment-level
  mirror is F-179's 2-of-16 counterexample (2 of 16 assignments balance globally while
  violating the pairwise condition), itself the spine-level mirror of F-177's CHECK4b.
  Balance is quantified over ALL pair-closed finite restrictions, equivalently stated
  pointwise — never asserted as one global sum.

## 4) Well-formedness (DEF obligation)

**(a) ι_or is an involution with no fixed record.** Applying the reading swap twice
restores the original reading (σ_src ↦ σ_tgt ↦ σ_src and conversely) while the datum
(A, B, q) is untouched at both applications: ι_or(ι_or(x)) = x. No fixed record: ι_or(x)
and x always differ in the reading component (σ_src ≠ σ_tgt — the two readings are
DISTINGUISHED, DEF_002_002 §2), and the reading is a component of the record tuple. ∎

**(b) ι_rev is an involution; fixed elements exist.** Reversing a step sequence twice
restores the sequence, and each step's reverse-of-reverse is itself (DEF_002_009 §2/§3's
reverse): ι_rev(ι_rev(w)) = w. Fixed elements: ι_rev(ε) = ε (the empty sequence reversed
is itself); for any walk w, reverse(w·w̄) = w·w̄ (the reverse of a concatenation is the
reversed parts in swapped order). ∎

**(c) Equivalence of the restriction-quantified and pointwise forms (support-typed
re-walk, dated 2026-06-12).** (⟹) For non-fixed x, P := {x, ι(x)} is itself a
restriction class (one distinct pair); the condition at P gives exactly the pointwise
clause at x: both absent (support symmetry holds at x, vacuously matched), or both
present with mult_X(x) = mult_X(ι(x)) (support symmetry + matching). (⟸) Any
restriction class P is a union of distinct pairs, and the both-absent-or-both-present-
and-equal condition demanded at each pair is an instance of the pointwise support-
symmetry + matching clauses. Fixed elements appear in neither form (parity-exempt both
ways). No multiplicity is ever compared against an absence: every comparison in both
forms is made only where both sides exist. ∎

**(d) Strict weakness of the aggregate form (separation exhibit, exact counts).** Take
distinct registers A, B ∈ R, any sampled q (the ℚ≥0 model's 1 suffices), and set
r₁ := ⟨σ_src, A, B, q⟩, r₂ := ⟨σ_src, B, A, q⟩ (two DIFFERENT instance data). Let

X_sep := ⟦ r₁, r₁, ι_or(r₂), ι_or(r₂) ⟧, i.e. supp(X_sep) = {r₁, ι_or(r₂)}, mult 2 each.

Aggregate form: σ_src-resolved total = 2 (two copies of r₁), σ_tgt-resolved total = 2
(two copies of ι_or(r₂)) — the totals agree (computed count readouts inside a
verification comparison — scope convention, GUARD_001_008 §2; this is the WEAK form
being refuted, never the card's predicate). Pointwise (support-typed): r₁ ∈ supp(X_sep)
but **ι_or(r₁) ∉ supp(X_sep)** — the co-reading is record-absent, so SUPPORT SYMMETRY
FAILS at r₁ — X_sep is NOT ι_or-balanced. (No mult = 0 is written: the failure is a
membership fact, not a zero comparison.) Conversely balance implies the aggregate
identity: by support symmetry ι_or restricts to a bijection between the σ_src-members
and the σ_tgt-members of the support, and summing the matching identity over it equates
the totals. So the aggregate form is implied by, and strictly weaker than, balance. ∎
(X_sep needs only |R| = 2; it is also, by THM_002_012(b), not reachable — recorded
there, not used here.)

**(e) License against GUARD_001_005 (alternative 2, argued explicitly — the review
hook).** GUARD_001_005 §2 prohibits equipping orientation tags with a composition law
under which two reversals compose to the identity reading, in ANY notation — σ² = e is
named among the hazard forms. The involutions declared here satisfy ι∘ι = id, which WEARS
that shape; this card therefore proceeds only under the guard's licensed alternative 2 (a
relationally justified principle arriving as a NEW card that argues the justification
explicitly and is reviewed against the guard before adoption). The argument:

1. **ι is not a tag operation.** Its domain is a typed object family (records; walks) —
   never Σ_rel. No composite of TAGS is formed anywhere on this card or by any consumer
   it licenses: σ_src·σ_tgt is as undefined after this card as before it. DEF_002_002's
   no-tag-arithmetic clause is untouched; the fence stands.
2. **ι∘ι = id is function composition read as substitution of descriptions.**
   DEF_002_002 §2 already states the relational fact: "two successive swaps restore the
   original reading is substitution of descriptions, not a multiplication law." The
   involution equation is that sentence formalized on a typed map and PROVED from the
   swap's definition (§4(a)/(b)) — never posited as an algebra of tags.
3. **What the guard protects stays protected.** The hazard is the sign group doing
   cancellation arithmetic in costume. On this card ι never meets quantity arithmetic:
   records carry a reading tag and a magnitude (DEF_002_002's entry rule as read pre-amendment
   — strictly positive at the gate, proposal §3.4; drift A1);
   balance is COUNT COMPARISON (mult_X(x) = mult_X(ι(x))), never a signed sum; no
   quantity is negated, cancelled, or composed through ι. **Standing review hook:** any
   future card that uses an ι-pair to license a signed cancellation on quantities has
   left this license and entered the guard's banned class.
4. **Walk-vs-tag boundary (GUARD_001_005 §9) respected:** ι_rev composes nothing — it is
   one map on whole walks, not a concatenation semantics for tags; ι_or likewise composes
   nothing. The boundary the guard keeps visible is kept visible here.
