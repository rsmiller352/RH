# Reachability balance — the Law of Symmetry Conservation at S01 (Adm(X) ⟹ ι_or-balance; the licensed operation set is balance-closed)

## 0) Governance & Scope Lock

OPERATION SET AS OF THIS WAVE — exactly the admissible transfers of DEF_002_004 — and

## 1) Plain language

Every move the book allows is a transfer, and a transfer is one event written down twice:
once from the side the amount left, once from the side it arrived. So start from a blank
record ledger, make any finite number of allowed moves, and look at what has been written:
every reading sits beside its co-reading, pile by pile — nothing is ever left unpartnered.
That is the law: the allowed moves cannot create an unpaired record. It is not a wordplay
about what "balanced" means — a ledger CAN be unbalanced (one lone reading is). And the
law sits OVER the rule book, not under it: if a proposed new kind of move (creating
something, growing something) seems to write an unpaired record, the move is the suspect,
not the law — either the move is wrong, or a partner entry is missing and has to be found
(the books are telling us a piece is absent), or old habits of math have crept back in.
The law itself answers only at the top: it fails if the whole construction provably cannot
be carried through with balanced books. At this wave the converse also holds: every
balanced ledger can actually be produced by allowed moves — so balance exactly marks out
what the current moves can write.

## 4) Proof (complete)

**Step 0 (per-step booking lemma: each executed transfer contributes exactly one distinct
ι_or-pair; refusals contribute nothing).** Let t = T(A→B; q) be executed. By β's
definition the increment is β(t) = ⟦x_t, ι_or(x_t)⟧ where x_t = ⟨σ_src, A, B, q⟩: the
second posted entry ⟨σ_tgt, A, B, q⟩ IS ι_or(x_t) by DEF_002_010 §2(c) (the reading
swaps, the datum is untouched), and x_t ≠ ι_or(x_t) by fixed-point-freeness
(DEF_002_010 §4(a)). So supp(β(t)) = {x_t, ι_or(x_t)} — exactly one distinct ι_or-pair,
mult 1 each; every other record is ABSENT from the increment (record-absent, not
mult-zero) — the increment is ι_or-balanced (support symmetry and matching both hold on
the pair; DEF_002_010 §2(e) support-typed form, re-walked 2026-06-12). A refused attempt
contributes nothing: it lies outside the relation's graph (no post-state, DEF_002_004
§4), and β is defined only on the graph (β3) — the record state is untouched, with no
partial posting.

**Step 1 (balance is closed under ⊎; support-cased re-walk, 2026-06-12).** If X and Y
are ι_or-balanced then so is X ⊎ Y. Support of the union: supp(X ⊎ Y) = supp(X) ∪
supp(Y), and on it the multiplicity is the sum of the PRESENT components' values — an
absent component contributes NO TERM (not a zero summand; superseded phrasing:
*"multiplicities add … mult_X(x) + mult_Y(x)"* unconditionally). For every non-fixed x:
**(support symmetry)** x ∈ supp(X ⊎ Y) ⟺ x ∈ supp(X) ∨ x ∈ supp(Y) ⟺ ι_or(x) ∈
supp(X) ∨ ι_or(x) ∈ supp(Y) (component support symmetry, twice) ⟺ ι_or(x) ∈
supp(X ⊎ Y). **(Matching)** for x ∈ supp(X ⊎ Y): by component support symmetry, x and
ι_or(x) are present in exactly the SAME components; each present component contributes
equal terms at x and ι_or(x) (component matching), and the same terms are summed on both
sides — mult_{X⊎Y}(x) = mult_{X⊎Y}(ι_or(x)). (DEF_002_010 §4(c) returns the official
restriction-quantified form.)

**Step 2 (base; re-walked 2026-06-12).** X_0 is ι_or-balanced vacuously BY ABSENCE:
supp(X_0) = ∅ — no record exists, so the support-symmetry and matching clauses quantify
over nothing (the empty-history predicate; superseded phrasing: *"every multiplicity is
0 = 0"* — no zero comparison is made, nothing exists to compare).

**Step 3 (induction; (b)).** For a licensed generation of length n, induct on i: X_0 is
balanced (Step 2); X_i = X_{i−1} ⊎ β(t_i) is balanced by Step 0 (the increment is
balanced) and Step 1 (closure under ⊎). Hence X_n is ι_or-balanced. Since the generation
was arbitrary (any h₀, any licensed sequence, any n), Adm(X) ⟹ X balanced. ∎(b)

**Step 4 (non-vacuousness; (c); re-walked 2026-06-12).** ⟦x⟧ has x ∈ supp(⟦x⟧) while
ι_or(x) ∉ supp(⟦x⟧) (record-absent; no record is fixed) — support symmetry fails. ∎(c)

**Step 5 (converse; (d); support-typed re-walk 2026-06-12 — h₀ is a PARTIAL map; the
old "empty sum is 0_𝒟" clause is superseded and quoted below).** Let X be ι_or-balanced
and finite. Since ι_or has no fixed record, supp(X) partitions into distinct ι_or-pairs
(support symmetry: both members present), and matching gives both members of each pair
equal multiplicity m — with **m ∈ ℕ≥1 automatic by type** (multiplicities exist only on
supports, DEF_002_010 §2(e); the old side-condition "m > 0" is no longer a condition).
Each pair {⟨σ_src, A, B, q⟩, ⟨σ_tgt, A, B, q⟩} determines exactly one instance datum
(A, B, q) — distinct pairs, distinct data — and the datum is well-typed for a transfer
(A ≠ B, q tag-matched and strictly positive: Rec's typing, DEF_002_010 §2(b)). Build the
execution list L: for each pair of multiplicity m, m copies of t = T(A→B; q); fix any
order. Define the initial holdings as the PARTIAL map

supp(h₀) := { u ∈ R : u is the source register of at least one entry of L };
h₀(u) := the tag-wise sum of q over all entries of L with source register u (u ∈ supp(h₀))

— each a NONEMPTY finite sum of strictly positive quantities, hence in 𝒟+ by closure;
**registers with no outflow are ABSENT from h₀** (register-absence predicate — the
no-outflow-registers-absent case; no empty sum is ever formed). Superseded phrasing:
*"(a finite sum in 𝒟≥0; the empty sum is 0_𝒟)"*. **Invariant:** before each step, every
register u with remaining outflow (≥ 1 remaining entry of L sourced at u) is IN the
book (u ∈ supp(h_i)) with h_i(u) ⪰ its remaining-outflow sum; registers without
remaining outflow carry no obligation (absence of obligation, not a zero bound).
Initially: equality on supp(h₀), and only outflow-bearing registers exist. Maintenance —
executing t = T(A→B; q): registers u ∉ {A, B} keep holdings and remaining sums
unchanged. Target B: B's remaining-outflow sum is unchanged (outflow counts sources
only); B's holding grows by q (or B joins the book with q) — if B had an obligation
rem_B, then from h(B) ⪰ rem_B follows h(B) + q ⪰ rem_B (the larger side grew — order
clauses + semigroup laws only); if B was absent, B had NO remaining outflow (absent
registers are either never-sources or already-exhausted-and-emptied, see the exit
observation below) — no obligation to maintain. Source A: write rem_before = q +
rem_after for A's remaining-outflow sums (the executed entry is removed; rem_after may
be ABSENT — A's last outflow). If rem_after is absent: A's obligation discharges
entirely (no remaining outflow — no obligation), and the gate read h(A) ⪰ rem_before =
q directly. If rem_after exists: rem_before = q + rem_after ≻ q strictly, and from the
invariant h(A) ⪰ rem_before, so h(A) ≠ q — **A cannot leave the book while it still
has outflow obligations** (the exit observation: the exact case h(A) = q occurs only at
a last outflow); the post-state is the strict case, h′(A) + q = h(A), and from
h(A) = rem_before (+ d) = q + rem_after (+ d), cancellativity (THM_002_003 [re-pointed
2026-07-11; was AX_S01_006, superseded]) gives
h′(A) = rem_after (+ d): h′(A) ⪰ rem_after. **Admissibility of each step:** A has
remaining outflow (the current entry), so by the invariant A ∈ supp(h) and h(A) ⪰
rem_before ⪰ q (strictly more when rem_after exists; equality allowed when it is A's
last outflow) — DEF_002_004's amended gate (source present AND h(A) ⪰ q) is satisfied;
every step of L is admissible in the fixed order. The generated record state is ⊎ over
L of one distinct ι_or-pair per execution (Step 0) — exactly X, pair multiplicities
reproduced by construction. Count-reconcile: |X| = 2·|L|. So X is reachable. ∎(d)

**Step 6 (count identity; (e)).** |β(t)| = 2 for every executed t (Step 0), and refusals
post nothing, so |X_n| = 2n. (Cardinalities here are computed verification-context
counts — scope convention, GUARD_001_008 §2; the n = 0 instance is STATED as the
empty-history predicate of §3 — no records exist — never as a zero-count comparison.)
The aggregate identity follows from balance via DEF_002_010 §4(d)'s one-way direction.
∎(e)
