# Constraint of closure — ledger persistence (D-027 re-scope: bookkeeping closure, not 3D existence)

## 0) Governance & Scope Lock

Theorem card with a complete proof (§4). Exact symbolic/combinatorial reasoning only; all
counts exact integers. Everything claimed here is BOOKKEEPING: closure and persistence are
ledger facts (D-027), never statements of spatial existence. Banned on this card: spatial-
realization claims in either direction; spatial topology anywhere (line, ring, winding,
modular structure); asserted dynamics (F-114's defect); extended/congruence-closure
erasure; S02+ objects (GUARD_001_006). Layer ceiling S01.

## 1) Plain language

The book can close an entry without the world thereby containing a new thing. A chain of
transfers that wanders off and never returns is not a candidate for closing anything — the
closure question is not even asked of it. A chain that returns but is nothing more than an
exact out-and-back is booked as if it never happened. Every other returning chain leaves a
permanent mark in the book: a closure record. It takes at least three registers for such a
mark to be possible at all — with one or two, every return is an exact out-and-back.
Whether something marked in the book is realized as an object in space is a later
question (the four-cell condition, much later in the build); this layer neither asks nor
answers it.

## 2) Statement

Setting: the register-walk universe over a finite register set R, with the
whole-walk erasure equivalence ~ (defined here §3, over DEF_002_009 walks) and ledger persistence as typed in §3.

**(a) Candidate restriction (D-027, implemented).** A walk is a **closure candidate** iff
it is closed (returning). Non-returning walks are not closure candidates: the closure
question is not posed of them, and no erasure semantics applies or needs to apply to them
(consistency proved at §4 Step 2). Ruling implemented verbatim (D-027): "S01 closure is
bookkeeping closure, not 3D existence" — S01 may say that non-returning transfers do not
form persistent closed structure at S01; that is exactly clause (a).

**(b) Ledger-persistence iff (the theorem).** A closure candidate u leaves a persistent
ledger-closure record (u ≁ ε, §3) **iff u is neither ε nor an exact whole-walk
retracing** — that is, iff u admits no decomposition u = w·w̄.

**(c) Non-vacuousness threshold.** Persisting closure candidates exist in the universe
over R **iff |R| ≥ 3**. For |R| ≥ 3 the 3-register cycle (A→B, B→C, C→A) persists; for
|R| ≤ 2 every closure candidate is erasure-equivalent to ε.

**(d) Scoped non-claim (D-027; TD-018).** Nothing here decides spatial realization, in
either direction. Whether a persisting ledger-closure record is realized as a 3D object is
governed by the four-cell condition — an S03/S04-layer fact (D-025.R10), named here as a
forward POINTER only (GUARD_001_006). S01 does not assert realization and does not deny
it; the question is not posed at this layer.

**Corollary 1 (finite families).** A finite multiset of walks leaves some persistent
closure record iff at least one member is a persisting closure candidate. (Records are
per-booking: the convention identifies whole walks only — no cross-walk erasure exists to
license.)

**Corollary 2 (seam, S01→S02 handoff).** A persistent record at S01 is a ledger fact — a
closure record held in the book over R. Persistence therefore requires accounting
structure (registers and records). The specific category list of what is tracked, and all
remainder/residual column rules, are NOT claimed here; S02 derives them.

## 3) Input contract (GR-1)

Objects (each typed before use, TD-009):

- **Registers:** R ← DEF_002_001, a finite set of relationship registers.
- **Step:** a directed traversal A→B between registers A ≠ B. Distinctness is
  DEF_002_004's source ≠ target requirement on transfers, carried to the shape level: a
  step is the booked shape of a transfer, with the moved quantity not carried on this card
  (the closure question is about the shape of return, not the amount moved). Reverse of a
  step A→B is B→A.
- **Walk:** w — a finite chained sequence of steps (each step begins at the register where
  the previous one ends); ε — the empty walk (zero steps). Reverse w̄: the steps in
  reverse order, each reversed. Concatenation u·v: defined when v begins where u ends (or
  either is ε). This instantiates DEF_002_009's walk objects with register traversals as
  the steps; the identification below is the convention's, schematic in the step type
  (DEF_002_009 §2 scope clause; instantiation ruled legitimate at verifier re-grade
  2026-06-11 — §9(ii)).
- **Closed:** a nonempty walk is closed iff its last step ends at its first step's start
  register; ε is closed.
- **Erasure equivalence ~:** the reflexive-symmetric-transitive closure of the whole-walk
  relation { (w·w̄, ε) : w a walk } over DEF_002_009 walks, applying the LEM_002_008 no-record fact (F-171): whole walks only,
  no closure under concatenation contexts.
- **Closure candidate:** a closed walk (§2(a)).
- **Ledger persistence (typed here):** a closure candidate u **leaves a persistent
  ledger-closure record** iff u ≁ ε. "Leaves no record" is a booking fact (the booking is
  identified with ε); it is never the excluded zero state (IMPORT_001_007's licensed
  roles; ε is a walk, not an existence value — DEF_002_009 §2/§3).

Domains: finite walks over finite R; parametric in R. Quantifiers: universal over walks
and candidates. Finite-N surface: all walks of length ≤ N over |R| ≤ 4. Limit mode: none.

## 4) Proof (complete)

Notation: K := the ~-class of ε; K₀ := {ε} ∪ { w·w̄ : w a walk }.

**Step 1 (K = K₀; all other classes are singletons).** Let B := { (w·w̄, ε) : w }. Define
E := identity ∪ (K₀ × K₀). E is reflexive and symmetric by construction, and transitive
because every non-identity pair of E lies inside K₀ (if (x,y) and (y,z) are non-identity
pairs then x, z ∈ K₀, so (x,z) ∈ E). B ⊆ E since each (w·w̄, ε) ∈ K₀ × K₀. Conversely,
let E′ be any equivalence containing B: every element of K₀ is E′-related to ε (for w·w̄
directly via B; for ε reflexively), hence by symmetry and transitivity all of K₀ is
pairwise E′-related, so E ⊆ E′. Therefore E is the smallest equivalence containing B,
which is ~ as defined here §3 (the generated equivalence is the rst-closure of
the whole-walk relation only). Hence K = K₀, and every walk outside K₀ is alone in its
class.

**Step 2 (K contains only closed walks; the candidate exit is consistent and needs no
stipulations).** For w ≠ ε, the walk w·w̄ begins at the start of w and ends where w̄ ends
— the start of w; so it is closed. ε is closed. Hence K = K₀ contains only closed walks:
the licensed erasure system identifies NO non-closed walk with ε, and §2(a)'s definitional
exit installs nothing new — non-returning walks are simply outside the question, and the
convention already says nothing about them. F-172's counterexample is thereby dissolved
rather than repaired: its object (a 2-step non-returning chain, the register analogue of
the 1D walk it exhibited) is non-closed, hence not a closure candidate; the iff (b)
quantifies over candidates only, so no instance of it concerns that object — with zero
added erasure stipulations.

**Step 3 (the iff, §2(b)).** Let u be a closure candidate. By §3's definition, u persists
iff u ∉ K (since the class of u is K exactly when u ∈ K, and a singleton ≠ K otherwise).
By Step 1, u ∉ K iff u ≠ ε and u admits no decomposition u = w·w̄. ∎(b)

**Step 4 (threshold, §2(c)).**
- |R| ≥ 3: pick distinct A, B, C ∈ R. The 3-cycle u₃ := (A→B, B→C, C→A) is closed. Every
  element of K has even length (ε has length 0; w·w̄ has length 2·len(w)); u₃ has length
  3. So u₃ ∉ K: u₃ persists.
- |R| = 1: steps require two distinct registers (§3, from DEF_002_004's source ≠ target),
  so the universe is {ε}, and ε ∈ K — nothing persists. (Without that distinctness
  requirement a 1-step self-traversal would persist at |R| = 1; the requirement is
  load-bearing here.)
- |R| = 2, say R = {A, B}: from each register exactly one step departs (to the other
  register), so a walk is determined by its start register and its length. Positions
  alternate, so a closed walk has even length 2k. The candidate ε is in K trivially; so
  take u nonempty (without loss of generality): u is a closed candidate of length 2k ≥ 2
  starting (without loss of generality) at A. Let w be the unique walk of length k
  starting at A. Then w·w̄ is a walk of length 2k starting at A, and by uniqueness
  u = w·w̄ ∈ K — every candidate erases; nothing persists. ∎(c)

**Corollary 1:** ~ is a relation on single walks (Step 1's B relates whole walks to ε;
no rule relates one booking to another), so a multiset's records are its members' classes,
and some persistent closure record exists iff some member is a candidate outside K.
**Corollary 2** is (b) read at the seam: a persistent record is by §3's definition a class
of the ledger's walk universe over R — a booking fact; the accounting-structure
requirement follows; the category list and column rules are S02's. ∎

**What this proof does NOT use:** no dynamics (nothing "eventually reverses" — F-114's
banned move stays banned and is not needed); no extended erasure (the congruence-closure
extension is not consumed — §9(iii); F-172 refuted the superseded route that needed it);
no spatial structure (no line, ring, winding, or modular arithmetic — the superseded
witness's ℤ/3 ring is not re-read but replaced by the |R| ≥ 3 clause).

## 7) Verification (witness SPEC — implemented by the witness author 2026-06-11 as
tools/verifications/S01/THM_S01_004_witness.py, replacing the superseded ND-2 script)

**Licensed universe.** For each |R| ∈ {1, 2, 3, 4}, R a plain finite register set with NO
additional structure (no ring, line, order, or modular arithmetic of any kind), enumerate
ALL register walks of length ≤ N (N ≥ 6): steps are ordered pairs of distinct registers,
chained. Count-reconcile the enumeration against the closed form: walks of length ℓ ≥ 1
number |R|·(|R|−1)^ℓ (start register free; each step's target forced off the current
register), plus ε at length 0.

**Compute (two independent routes, then compare).** (i) Membership in K within the bound:
once by the relation (ε together with every w·w̄ for len(w) ≤ N/2), once by a direct
decomposition test (u has even length, its second half is the reverse of its first half).
(ii) Closedness of every enumerated walk.

**Assert.** (1) The two routes for K agree exactly, walk by walk (value-level, no
key-presence shortcuts). (2) Every member of K within bound is closed; NO non-closed walk
lies in K — the candidate exit needs no erasure stipulation (F-172's object class: every
2-step non-returning chain must be non-closed and untouched by the relation). (3) For
every closure candidate, the persistence classification (∉ K) matches §2(b)'s
characterization (not ε and not decomposable) exactly. (4) |R| ≤ 2: the count of
persisting candidates is exactly 0 at every length bound. (5) |R| ≥ 3: the 3-cycle
(A→B, B→C, C→A) is enumerated, classified persisting, and the persisting count is
positive — this clause is fully discharged at finite N ≥ 3 (existence is a finite
exhibit). All counts exact integers; no decimals anywhere; no floating point.

**Can-fail (--corrupt), REQUIRED — each mode must flip the run to a nonzero exit.**
Mode A: extend the erasure relation by a single congruence-context instance (erase one
w·w̄ inside a context, u·(w·w̄)·v treated as u·v) — assertion (1)'s two-route agreement
MUST fail. Mode B: add (u₃, ε) for the designated 3-cycle at |R| = 3 — assertion (5),
a single named conjunction (u₃ enumerated ∧ u₃ classified persisting ∧ persisting count
positive), MUST fail via its u₃-persistence conjunct (the count conjunct alone stays true:
erasing u₃ leaves the other persisters — 83 at N = 6, verifier-computed).

**Finite-N caveat:** as in frontmatter `what_breaks_at_finite_N`. A witness pass checks
the licensed model on the bounded universe; it never substitutes for §4's proof and never
