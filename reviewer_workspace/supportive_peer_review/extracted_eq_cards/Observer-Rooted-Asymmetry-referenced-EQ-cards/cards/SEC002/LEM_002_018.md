# The No-Last-Realizing-Relation lemma (serial realization) — an admitted unit is realized exactly as far as admitted relations realize it (no bare persistence); every admitted persisting unit FORCES a licensed closure to exist in the book; and the book's licensed-entry order has NO MAXIMAL ELEMENT while admitted content stands (which, by LEM_002_017, is always): a last entry would leave a standing obligation whose forced closure does not exist — contradiction. The realization structure of admitted existence is SERIAL — stated statically (no-maximal-element, an order fact), never temporally — Recurrence Wave R3, THE CRUX

## 1) Plain language

*(This section, alone on the card, uses the internal observer's temporal idiom — the
readout language; the claims and proof are order-theoretic.)*

Three facts, each already proved in this book, compose into something new. First: a thing
exists only as it is distinguished through relations — there is no existence held in
storage, apart from the relating (the axiom). Second: a persisting thing must be
re-identified across every span it persists, and its persistence must actually close —
balance its books — because an open span records that something happened without balancing
what happened (the Theorem of Time's first step). Third: nothing admitted can be legally
erased, and no legal move empties the book (persistence, conservation). Now suppose the
book had a last entry. After that entry the book still holds something — conservation
guarantees it. That something is admitted, and its account is not yet settled — the settling
would itself be a further entry, and there are none. But an admitted thing whose settling
exists nowhere in the book is a persisting existence that never closes — exactly what the
Theorem of Time's first step rules out: unclosed persistence leaves no balanced record, and
what leaves no record is not admitted at all. Contradiction. So there is no last entry. Not
because a clock pushes the book forward — nothing here moves — but because a ledger of
admitted existence with a final page is not a consistent object: its final page always owes
one more entry. The ticking the runtime will read off this structure is the count of those
owed entries; that is the sense in which the ticks are part of existence rather than
something existence does in time.

## 2) Statement

Work over the S01 ledger universe (the book): registers, partial holdings over 𝒟+,
records, walks, and the licensed operation set 𝒪 (DEF_002_001 / DEF_002_004 / THM_002_012
via LEM_S01_022/LEM_S01_026 — consumed through the named clauses below). **Book-order** is
the ledger's before/after structure (LEM_002_008 §3: an interval is any before/after span
of the book — bookkeeping only, never a metric duration). A **realizing relation** of an
admitted unit r is an admitted relation with r as an end that re-identifies r across a span
(LEM_002_008's re-identification) or acts on r as a licensed operation (LEM_002_013 Step 3
— transfer/discharge/closure; each is a relation by its own typing, DEF_002_004). An
admitted unit's obligation **stands at a ledger state** when the unit is admitted content
of that state and no licensed relation at-or-before that state has closed it (LEM_002_013's
accounting-obligation status).

> **(a) (Co-extension — no bare persistence.)** An admitted counted unit is admitted
> exactly as far (in book-order) as admitted relations realize it: across any span with no
> realizing relation, it has no record, and no record is non-admission. There is no
> admission-beyond-realization.
>
> **(b) (In-book closure forced.)** For every admitted counted unit r whose obligation
> stands: **a licensed closure of r exists in the book.** (A book containing an admitted
> persisting unit and no closure of it is not a consistent object of this calculus.)
>
> **(c) (No maximal element — serial realization.)** In any book whose ledger contains
> admitted content (guaranteed from the root: AX_S01_001/THM_S01_018 admit the first unit,
> and LEM_002_017 conserves admitted content through every licensed entry): **the
> licensed-entry order has no maximal element.** Every entry is followed (book-order) by a
> further entry. Equivalently: "ρ* is the book's last entry" is contradictory.

**Conclusion shape (the Cor 4 fence):** clause (c) is a negative-existence / serial-order
statement — the ABSENCE of a last element. No actual-infinite object is asserted or
admitted (THM_002_007 Cor 4); the book is never quantified over as a completed infinite
totality.

## 4) Proof (complete — every implication names its forcing card; D-062 A1)

**Step 1 (clause (a) — co-extension).** By AX_001_001 §2 (verbatim): "there is no admitted
existence without relational distinguishability" — an admitted unit's distinguishability is
held ONLY in admitted relations (LEM_002_013 Step 1: the admission-status is relationally
held — a relational fact, not an intrinsic one). By LEM_002_008 (P-reident), r persists as
one unit across a span ONLY IF re-identified across it; by its Step 2, across a span with
no re-identification r has "no record," and no record is the non-admission boundary
(AX_001_001 / IMPORT_001_007 Cor 1, consumed through LEM_002_008 Step 2). So r's admission
extends exactly as far as admitted relations realize it. ∎(a)
*(Forcing cards: AX_001_001 §2; LEM_002_013 Step 1; LEM_002_008 (P-reident) + Step 2.
No gloss: each cited clause is the card's stated content.)*

**Step 2 (clause (b) — the in-book closure is forced).** Let r be admitted with standing
obligation. By LEM_002_013 (positive face): r "remains accountable — an accounting
obligation on the books — until a licensed relation closes it." Bivalence (P-or-not-P): the
book contains a licensed closure of r, or it does not. Suppose NOT. Then r's obligation
stands throughout the book — r is carried by the ledger as one persisting unit across every
span beyond its admission cut. By LEM_002_008 (P-reident) r is re-identified across every
such span. By THM_002_014 §4 Step 1 (verbatim): "it must **persist as itself**, which is
**closure**. And closure is **no unresolved remainder** … An *open* interval marks that
something happened; it does not balance *what* happened. So a complete unit of persisting
existence is a **closure** — a static closed bookkeeping object with no unresolved
remainder."

*(Step 2.1 — the universality of the closure-forcing, named-forced.)* THM_002_014 §4
Step 1's derivation is INSTANTIATED at the first distinction, but its two premises are
UNIVERSAL over admitted counted units: LEM_002_008 quantifies "universal over admitted
one-unit (indivisible) distinctions and intervals" (its §3, verbatim), and THM_002_007's
count holds of every admitted support (every admitted token is a counted unit). So the
argument's conclusion — persisting-as-itself is realized as a closure — instantiates at
ANY admitted counted unit r: the widening is carried by the premises' own quantifiers,
not by a prose reading. *(Forcing cards: LEM_002_008 §3 quantifier clause; THM_002_007;
THM_002_014 §4 Step 1's generic closing sentence, which states the instantiated form.)*

*(Step 2.2 — closure-as-object IS realized as book entries; its closing act is a
licensed relation, named-forced.)* The closure of THM_002_014 Step 1 is by its own
verbatim content a BOOKKEEPING object: "every distinction, displacement, or transfer has
its corresponding accounting term — the books balance (the transfer relation, DEF_002_004
/ P3 …; conservation, LEM_002_005 …)" — its balance is constituted of accounting terms
IN THE BOOK. And the act that closes r's OBLIGATION is by type a licensed relation:
LEM_002_013 §3 types "licensed relation (transfer/discharge/closure)" as THE
admission-status-changing operations — closing an accounting obligation is that type's
own content, not a same-word coincidence (the TD-057 object-identity check: same object
by construction — the obligation's closing is defined at LEM_002_013 as the licensed
relation's act). *(Forcing cards: THM_002_014 §4 Step 1's balance clause; LEM_002_013
§2/§3.)*

A persisting r with NO closure anywhere in the book is therefore a complete unit of
persisting existence that is NOT realized as a closure — excluded by THM_002_014 Step 1
(via 2.1); its persistence-as-itself fails, and by LEM_002_008 Step 2 what does not
persist as itself across its spans leaves no record — non-admission — contradicting r
admitted. (And the passage from admitted to non-admitted without a licensed relation is
separately barred: LEM_002_013 Steps 2–6 — a status-change requires a relation; only
licensed relations act; no-entry is not an operation.) The supposition fails: **a
licensed closure of r exists in the book** (an in-book entry, by 2.2). ∎(b)
*(Forcing cards: LEM_002_013 positive face + §3 + Steps 2–6; LEM_002_008 §3 + (P-reident)
+ Step 2; THM_002_014 §4 Step 1 incl. its balance clause; THM_002_007. The bivalence is
P-or-not-P — TD-018.)*

**Step 3 (the survivor — admitted content stands at every state).** The root admits
content: AX_001_001 admits the first distinguishability, THM_002_007 counts it (I = 1) — the book is not empty at the root (P1: there is something; the axiom's ground).
By LEM_002_017 (b)/(c), no licensed entry — nor any finite composition — carries a book
with admitted content to a book with none, and no unlicensed route exists (LEM_002_013).
So at every ledger state of the book, admitted content stands, carried by strictly-positive
holdings; and by AX_001_001 §2 that content is admitted AS distinguishable relation-tokens
— counted units (THM_002_007), each an accounting obligation while unclosed (LEM_002_013).
∎(survivor)
*(Forcing cards: AX_001_001; THM_002_007; LEM_002_017; LEM_002_013.)*

**Step 4 (clause (c) — no maximal element).** Suppose the book's licensed-entry order has
a maximal element ρ* (a last entry). Consider the post-ρ* ledger state.

*(Step 4.1 — standing content exists.)* By Step 3, admitted counted content stands at the
post-ρ* state: the conserved total is carried by SOME strictly-positive holding of that
state. *(Forcing cards: LEM_002_017; AX_001_001 §2's typing via Step 3.)*

*(Step 4.2 — every holding OF the post-ρ* state is unclosed as it stands, named-forced.)*
By LEM_002_013 Step 4, a licensed closing **moves and accounts** the closed quantity —
"every licensed act on r moves it …, discharges it, or closes it — in each case leaving a
paired account of where the unit went." So quantity closed by any entry at-or-before ρ*
was moved onward by that entry and is NOT part of any holding of the post-ρ* state (a
partially-moved holding's standing remainder is, as standing quantity, exactly the part
no entry closed). Hence the holdings OF the post-ρ* state are, as they stand, UNCLOSED
obligations — no re-choice or chain-following is needed: standing-in-the-final-state and
being-unclosed are the same fact, by move-and-account. In particular a closing entry's
own co-end holding is not closed by the act that posts it: the act's object is the closed
obligation at its source end; the co-end holding is the posted account (DEF_002_004's
B-end, through LEM_002_013 Step 4's wording), an admitted counted unit whose OWN
accountability is fresh — LEM_002_013's positive face is universal over admitted counted
units. This is exactly what excludes the all-settled ("everything closed at ρ*") tableau:
settling everything posts accounts, and the posted accounts stand unclosed. *(Forcing
cards: LEM_002_013 Step 4 + positive face (universal); DEF_002_004 via LEM_002_013's
typing; LEM_002_017 Step 1 (the co-end holding is strictly positive).)*

*(Step 4.3 — the contradiction.)* Let x be a standing holding's admitted counted unit
(4.1), unclosed as it stands (4.2). By Step 2, a licensed closure of x exists in the book.
By LEM_002_013 Step 4 a closed obligation does not stand at any state at-or-after its
closing (it was moved and accounted) — since x stands unclosed at the post-ρ* state, no
entry at-or-before ρ* closed it; so the forced closure is an entry AFTER ρ* in book-order.
None exists (ρ* maximal). Contradiction. Therefore the licensed-entry order has no
maximal element. ∎(c)
*(Forcing cards: Steps 4.1/4.2; Step 2 (forced closure); LEM_002_013 Step 4. The
bivalences are P-or-not-P — TD-018.)*
