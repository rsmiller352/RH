## 1) Plain language

We have: existence is a distinguishing (AX_001_001), the first counts as one indivisible unit
(THM_002_007), to stay one it must re-identify, and that re-identification is a **relation** whose
two-endedness is a **second degree of freedom** (LEM_002_008, the relational lift). This card
measures the **extent** of that second degree of freedom, and the measure **is time**.

Here is the heart of it, the thing that makes this not circular. You might object: "to come back
to yourself you have to *turn around*, and turning takes time — so you are using time to define
time." That objection fails. We are **not** assuming a rotation, a motion, or a process that
takes time. We are reading off a property of a **static closed object** — its **total turning**,
the angle it sweeps in one complete closure. Once the persisting distinction is embedded in its
closure plane (the second DoF, LEM_002_008), and that plane has **no preferred direction**
(isotropy — there is nothing else at this layer to pick one out), the family of all the
orientation-preserving ways the distinction can close is represented by **the circle**, and *one
complete orientation-preserving closure has angular extent 2π*. That 2π is not a speed, not a
duration of any motion, and **not a length**: it is the **angular extent** — the total turning —
of one whole closure of the static object. Crucially it is **scale-invariant**: a full turn
subtends 2π for *any* radius, large or small — it never scales the way a circumference (a length)
would. So no radius and no unit of length is fixed; the 2π is **dimensionless and metric-free**,
exactly the kind of licensed geometric constant (like π itself) available at this pre-metric layer
(P8). **Time is DEFINED by this extent**, not the other way around.

(This is the one place to be careful, and it is what was repaired here, D-053(a). The **winding
number** — the *count* of how many times the closure goes around, which is **1** for one complete
closure — is **not** time: it is the **information** unit, I = 1, the *first* degree of freedom
(THM_002_007). Time is the **extent** of that one closure, the angle it sweeps — 2π — the *second*
degree of freedom. Count vs. extent: the count is 1, the extent is 2π. The earlier framing of 2π
as the unit-circle *circumference* — a length with a silently-chosen radius 1 — was a metric
reading the pre-metric layer cannot license; the angular extent is the metric-free reading, and it
is 2π for every radius.)

Three things make the 2π what it is, and each is **forced**, not chosen:
- **A full turn (2π), not a half-flip (π):** because of the **admission-status asymmetry** — A is
  admitted, ¬A is only the non-admission boundary (not an object), so A and ¬A are **not
  interchangeable** (THM_002_007 Steps 0–1, Cor 5). A π-flip would swap A and ¬A (treating them as
  interchangeable); but they are not, so the closure that returns A to A *as A* is the
  **orientation-preserving** full turn, with extent 2π.
- **Isotropy (the circle, not some other closed curve):** because at this layer there is **no
  other structure** to prefer one direction over another (minimality). With every direction
  equivalent, the closure is the circle.
- **Two degrees of freedom:** because the lift to a second DoF is **relational**, never individual
  (LEM_002_008 — the indivisible unit alone furnishes no second DoF; the re-identification
  relation's two-endedness does; the only individual 1-DoF return is a retrace, which erases the
  distinction).

And **closure means: no unresolved remainder** — the books balance. Every distinction,
displacement, or transfer has its corresponding accounting term (this is the transfer relation,
DEF_002_004 / P3 — nothing vanishes, the system must say where it went — plus conservation,
LEM_002_005). An open interval marks that something *happened*; closure balances *what*
happened. **Time is the bookkeeping dimension that lets change be balanced.** Its base measure is
the angular extent of one complete orientation-preserving closure:

$$t_{\text{base}} = 2\pi.$$

And one unit of existence is *serviced* by exactly one such closure — the conjugate support that
lets one unit close with no remainder is the **base mass**, m_base = 1/(2π) (§5).
