# The transfer relation T — what replaces subtraction

## 0) Governance & Scope Lock

Definition card: defines one relation on holdings states and proves it well-defined.
Exact symbolic forms only. The representation contains no signs anywhere: admissibility
gates the relation instead of negativity flagging it. Layer ceiling S01. The S04
escapement language in §9 is a forward POINTER only.

## 1) Plain language

When Bob takes an apple from Alice, no "−1 apple" ever exists: Alice's holding goes from
2 to 1, and Bob — who held nothing and so was not in the book for apples — joins the book
holding 1. The apple is the same apple. That picture IS the framework's subtraction. A
transfer is allowed only if the source actually holds what is to move — there are no
overdrafts, no debts, no anti-apples, and no transfers of nothing (a "transfer of zero"
is not a transfer; nothing happened). What standard mathematics books as subtraction,
this book books as movement. [Plain language re-walked 2026-06-12 with the D-046
amendment — prior wording ("Bob's from 0 to 1") superseded.]

**[Amendment 2026-06-12 — D-043.Q-a + typed-predicate strengthening (RSM), applied
through the Phase-C gauntlet; supersedes the 2026-06-12 Q-a-only proposal]:**

**(B1) Typing.** The transfer quantity is strictly positive **by type**:
$q \in \mathcal{D}_{+}$. There is no zero quantity object; **a zero or
absent-quantity attempt is ILL-TYPED — no $q$ exists to attempt with** ("That is not
a transfer. Nothing happened." — D-043 corrected statement). The Q-a ban is subsumed:
nothing is left to ban, the form cannot be written.

**(B2) Admissibility (partial holdings).** $T(A{\to}B;\, q)$ is admissible at $h$ iff
$$A \in \operatorname{supp}(h) \quad\text{and}\quad h(A) \succeq q.$$
**You cannot transfer from an absent register:** if $A \notin \operatorname{supp}(h)$
(register-absence predicate) no holding exists for the gate to read and the attempt is
inadmissible — no post-state, the no-transfer-occurred predicate.

**(B3) Post-state, support cases explicit.** For admissible $T(A{\to}B;\, q)$ the
post-state $h'$ is:
- **Target** ($B$ always ends in the book): if $B \in \operatorname{supp}(h)$ then
  $h'(B) = h(B) + q$; else $B$ **joins** $\operatorname{supp}(h')$ with $h'(B) = q$.
- **Source, exact case:** if $h(A) = q$ (the order's equality disjunct) then $A$
  **leaves** $\operatorname{supp}(h')$ — everything was transferred out. This is the
  typed form of the old $d = 0_{\mathcal{D}}$ balance readout: the source's outflow
  matches its holding exactly (paired-entries-match) and the register is thereafter
  absent (record-absent). No zero value is written.
- **Source, strict case:** if $h(A) \succ q$ then $A \in \operatorname{supp}(h')$ and
  $h'(A) \in \mathcal{D}_{+}$ is the unique element with $h'(A) + q = h(A)$
  (uniqueness by THM_002_003 [re-pointed 2026-07-11; was AX_S01_006, superseded —
  statement identical, now derived]).
- **Bystanders:** $h'(r) = h(r)$ for $r \in \operatorname{supp}(h) \setminus \{A, B\}$;
  $r \notin \operatorname{supp}(h) \cup \{B\} \Rightarrow r \notin
  \operatorname{supp}(h')$ (present-or-absent alike, unchanged).

Subtraction remains **characterized existentially** in the strict case ($h'(A)$ is the
unique holding whose sum with $q$ restores $h(A)$); in the exact case nothing remains
to characterize — the typed predicates carry the books. Discipline reference:
GUARD_001_008 (reference, not a dependency edge — D-029.3; the guard is draft and a
formal→draft edge is an E-DEP).

- **Totality where admissible, undefined elsewhere:** an inadmissible T has no post-state
  (no partial/penalized fallback) — the gate is the nonlinearity, not a sign.
- **Worked example (P3's own, normative gloss; re-walked 2026-06-12, D-043/D-046):**
  R = {Alice, Bob}, tag apples; supp(h) = {Alice},
  h(Alice) = 2 — Bob is ABSENT (register-absence predicate; the old "h(Bob) = 0 (empty
  holding licensed, IMPORT_001_007 §2)" is superseded). T(Alice→Bob; 1): admissible;
  supp(h′) = {Alice, Bob}, h′(Alice) = 1, h′(Bob) = 1 (Bob joins the book) — and at no
  point does any signed object exist.
  T(Alice→Bob; 2) at the original h: admissible by the equality disjunct; supp(h′) =
  {Bob}, h′(Bob) = 2 — Alice leaves the book (exact case). Inadmissible: T(Alice→Bob; 3)
  (2 ⋡ 3; no post-state). Inadmissible-by-absence: T(Bob→Alice; 1) at the original h
  (Bob ∉ supp(h) — you cannot transfer from an absent register). ILL-TYPED (not
  inadmissible — never reaches the gate): "T(Alice→Bob; 0)" — no q exists to attempt
  with.
- **What replaces "1 − x" forms:** quantity MOVEMENT is T; complement forms are
  share-splitting within a conserved total (precedent: the filled/void share pattern
  D + Ω-void = total at its own layer); signed corpus objects enter only as
  orientation × magnitude (CONV_S01_009). Each later "subtraction" must be one of these
  three or live in a BRIDGE card — that trichotomy is what §8 arms.
