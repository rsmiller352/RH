## 1) Plain language

A thing's "where" is not a slot it sits in — it **is** the set of relations that tell it apart from everything else. There is no background grid or coordinate sitting underneath, at any number of dimensions, holding a "where" independent of relations. And a thing entirely alone, with nothing to relate to, doesn't have a "where" at all — not "the origin," not "zero," but simply **no location-fact**: the question doesn't apply (the same way the Law of Existence treats no-entry as not-a-thing, not as a thing equal to nothing).

This is the same move the Law of Existence makes for existence itself ("a thing exists only as a distinguishable relation") — applied to location: *where* a thing is, is one of the ways it is distinguished, so *where* is relational for exactly the reason *existence* is. (And it's why the geometry that carries the later construction is relational mathematics, P4: a point alone "says almost nothing" — it means something only inside the structure of relations it belongs to.)

This card states only the **general** principle. *How* a particular thing — a cell born from another — gets its relational "where" (next to its origin) is later, cell-level work (S04) that builds on this.

## 2) Statement

> **Premise.** Existence is relational distinguishability (`AX_001_001`): a thing is admitted only as a distinguishable relation-token. Geometry is relational mathematics (`AX_001_002`/P4): a point is meaningful only inside the structure of relations it belongs to.
>
> **Then:**
>
> **(1 — location is a mode of relational distinguishability.)** A thing's **location** — *where* it is — is one of the ways it is told apart from what it is not; it is therefore a form of relational distinguishability.
>
> **(2 — hence location is relational; no background/coordinate location.)** By `AX_001_001`, all distinguishability is relational; so a thing's location **is** its distinguishing relations. There is **no** background / coordinate / absolute location — at **any** dimension (`AX_001_002`/P4: no meaningful point without its relational structure). "No coordinate at any dimension" is asserted as a *prohibition*; no coordinate object is constructed.
>
> **(3 — a relatum-less thing has no location-FACT.)** A single thing alone, with **no** relatum, stands in no distinguishing locational relation — so it has **no location-fact**: an **absence / non-applicability**, **never** a zero-object ("location = 0", an origin, a null coordinate are all forbidden — TD-026).

No glyph introduced; no symbol manipulated.

## 4) Proof (complete)

**Step 1 (location is a mode of relational distinguishability).** A thing's location — *where* it is — distinguishes it: a thing here-rather-than-there is told apart by that very fact. So location is a form of distinguishability. (Not a new primitive: it is distinguishability under the locational aspect.) ∎(1)

**Step 2 (location is relational; no background/coordinate location).** By `AX_001_001`, existence is relational distinguishability — *all* admitted distinguishability is relational; there is no bare distinguishability apart from relation. Location being a mode of distinguishability (Step 1), location is therefore **relational**: a thing's location **is** its distinguishing relations, with no background/coordinate/absolute "where" underneath. This is dimension-independent: the claim concerns location-as-such, and since no coordinate is admitted, any "dimension" that later appears is itself only relational (`AX_001_002`/P4: a point "says almost nothing" alone — meaning lives only in the relational structure). "No coordinate at any dimension" is stated as the card's prohibition (mention), constructing nothing. ∎(2)

**Step 3 (relatum-less ⟹ no location-fact, an absence).** Consider a thing with no relatum (nothing to be distinguished-in-relation-to). By Step 2 its location *is* its distinguishing locational relations — of which it has none. So it has **no location-fact**: location is **not applicable** to it. This is an **absence** (the predicate does not apply), typed exactly as `AX_001_001` types no-entry — **not** a zero-object: not "location = 0", not an origin, not a null coordinate (TD-026). ∎(3)

**What this proof does NOT use / claim:** no coordinate / lattice / point / dimension constructed (Step 2: prohibition only); no zero-object (Step 3: absence); no cell / escapement / observer / σ (the cell-specific adjacency and the observer-dependent reading are forward — S04 / S06–S07); no temporal, no physical (D-060); no restatement of `AX_001_001` (Steps 1–3 add the location corollary the axiom does not itself state). It is **strictly stronger** than `AX_001_001` on the locational aspect (it entails "location is relational"; `AX_001_001` alone does not mention location), and not a duplicate of `AX_001_002` (P4 licenses relational geometry and declines ontology; this card makes the location-ontology claim).
