## 1) Plain language

The previous card says existence is a distinguishing — telling A apart from not-A. This card
counts the very first such distinguishing, and gets exactly one.

Here is why. A single act of distinguishability is a single cut: it splits into the admitted
side A and the not-admitted side, ¬A. Only A enters the book — ¬A is the edge, the boundary
of non-admission, not a thing inside (that was AX_001_001's no-entry). So after the first
cut, exactly one thing has been admitted: the side A. Count what has been admitted, and the
answer is one. That is the meaning of I = 1: the first admitted distinguishability has
existence count one.

The one is FORCED — it is not chosen to be one because "first," and it is not normalized to
one for convenience. It is one because a single cut has a single admitted side. If a single
cut could admit two positive sides, or if the boundary ¬A could itself be admitted as a
thing, the count would not be one — and that is exactly what would have to happen for this
to be wrong (the falsifiers). From the one follow five plain consequences: zero is not an
existence; a fraction is not a primitive existence (you cannot have part of a unit before
you have the unit); a negative is not a primitive existence (a below-nothing has nothing to
be below yet); an actual infinity is not a completed existence-token; and once existence is
counted, the next question is how the sides of a relation are preserved — which is symmetry.
Existence comes first; symmetry follows.

## 4) Proof (complete — the binary-cut argument)

**Step 0 (the cut).** A primitive act of distinguishability is a **binary cut** A | ¬A
(AX_001_001: distinguishability is the telling-apart of A from what it is not). The cut has
two sides: the admitted positive side A, and the complement ¬A.

**Step 1 (only A enters).** A is the admitted positive side: a relation-token, admitted into
the universe of discourse (AX_001_001's admission). ¬A is the **non-admitted** side — it is
the no-entry boundary of AX_001_001, a boundary marker for non-admission, NOT an object of
the theory (TD-001; forbidden_inputs). So ¬A does not enter; only A enters as a
relation-token.

**Step 2 (the admitted support is a singleton).** After the first cut, the admitted support
— the collection of admitted positive relation-tokens — contains exactly the one token A and
nothing else (¬A is excluded by Step 1; no other token has been admitted, this being the
FIRST distinction). The admitted support of the first distinction is the singleton {A}.

**Step 3 (count the singleton).** By singleton-counting (§2 premise), |{A}| = 1.

**Step 4 (the existence count).** I counts admitted positive relation-tokens. For the first
distinction r, the admitted positive support is {A}, so

$$I(r) = |\{A\}| = 1.$$

∎

**Why the "=1" is FORCED, not normalized (the F-182 trap closed).** The coefficient 1 is the
cardinality of the admitted positive support of ONE binary cut. A single cut has a single
admitted side (Step 1), so the support is a singleton (Step 2), so the count is 1 (Step 3) —
this is forced by the binary nature of one distinguishability act. It is NOT obtained by
declaring "the unit is 1" (normalization), and NOT by reading "first" as "one" (a single cut
could a priori admit a non-singleton support — that it does not is PROVED from the
admit-only-the-positive-side structure, not assumed). Pre-stating the conclusion is exactly
the F-182 trap; this proof does not.

**The one named conditional the forcing rests on — (P-token) (F-183, kept visible).** The
verifier graded this derivation VERIFIED-CONDITIONAL (F-183): the "=1" is the COMPUTED
cardinality of the admitted positive support of one binary cut, NOT pre-stated — but the
singleton-ness of that support (Step 2's identification of the positive block A with a
SINGLE token A, rather than a populated region of several tokens) is forced by, and ONLY
by, one named premise supplied by the axiom itself:

> **(P-token).** A single primitive act of distinguishability introduces EXACTLY ONE
> relation-token — the positive side of a binary cut is a single token, not a populated
> region.

This is AX_001_001's own typing of rel_token ("one primitive act of distinguishability =
the minimal admissible unit by which existence can be distinguished"); it is the axiom's
content, NOT a smuggled count (two tokens on the positive side would be two acts / a
non-minimal unit — outside the typing, and exactly falsifier F7). The forcing of I = 1 is
therefore conditional on the axiom's typing of one distinguishability act as exactly one
relation-token — and on nothing else (the housed |{A}| = 1 is premise-legal arithmetic,
"a singleton has one element," NOT the conclusion). This is the new root: the value the
old AX_001_001 asserted is now derived here, conditional on the new axiom's typing.

## 4′) Why this is derived

I = 1 is derived from, in order: **(1)** existence is relational distinguishability
(AX_001_001 — the cut exists because distinguishability is a telling-apart); **(2)** the
primitive binary cut (Step 0 — one act of distinguishability is one cut A | ¬A); **(3)**
admission of only the positive side (Step 1 — ¬A is non-admission boundary, not an object);

and the derivation fails: without (1) there is no cut; without (3) ¬A enters and the support
is not a singleton; without (4) the singleton has no count. The value is the conclusion of
this chain, never its premise.

## 5) Corollaries

**Cor 1 — Zero is not existence.** Zero is not an admitted relation-token, and not an
object, event, payload, transfer, residual, holding, or ledger value. The first existence
count is the positive singleton (Step 4); there is no admitted token of count zero (a count
of zero is no admitted positive support — nothing admitted). A "0" mark only INDICATES
non-admission (the no-entry boundary of AX_001_001), never an existence-token. *(This is the
ground IMPORT_001_007 now cites: zero-exclusion is a consequence of this corollary, not a
premise of the Law of Existence — D-051.)*

**Cor 2 — Decimals are not primitive existence.** A subdivision of the unit presupposes the
unit (you cannot have a fraction of a relation-token before the relation-token is admitted).
Decimals are downstream of I = 1 (D-050 kin: the printed decimal is a unit-relative ratio,
not a primitive existence). No primitive decimal existence-token is admitted.

**Cor 3 — Negative values are not primitive existence.** A negative is not a primitive
admitted magnitude below the unit; it is only an orientation, inverse, conjugacy, direction,
or mapping ON an already-admitted positive unit. There is no below-the-unit existence prior
to the unit (TD-002: subtraction is transfer; no negative object). No primitive negative
existence-token is admitted.

**Cor 4 — Infinity is not primitive existence.** Actual infinity is not an admitted
completed token: a completed existence-token is a finished count (Step 4 gives the first as
one), and an actual infinity is never a completed finite count. "Infinity" marks a boundary,
an unresolved continuation, or a failed finite closure — never an admitted completed
existence-token.

**Cor 5 — Symmetry follows existence.** A relation has distinguishable sides / roles /
readings / orientations (the cut A | ¬A is two-sided). Once existence is counted (this card),
the next question about a relation is the PRESERVATION / pairing / balance of its sides —
which is the symmetry law. Therefore symmetry is downstream of existence: existence is PRIOR
to symmetry. *(This is the bridge to THM_002_012 — the Law of Symmetry Conservation at S01 —
which is thereby explicitly grounded after, not alongside, existence. D-051.)*
