# Orientation × magnitude — the definition of the orientation tag σ and the P3-forced entry representation for signed material (supersedes CONV_S01_009, D-064)

## 0) Governance & Scope Lock

Defines the orientation tag σ and the P3-forced booking representation by which signed
objects (from corpus or conventional notation) are held internally as (orientation,
strictly-positive magnitude). Derives no downstream value; certifies nothing beyond its own
consistency. Tag arithmetic is banned here and everywhere (GUARD_001_005). Layer ceiling
S01. **Supersedes CONV_S01_009** (D-064): retyped CONV→DEF because the representation's form
is forced by P3 and the card types a new object (σ).

## 1) Plain language

When older work hands us a "−3", we do not store a negative three. We store two separate
things: which way the relation is being read (the orientation σ), and how much (three, a
plain strictly-positive amount). A signed zero enters as nothing at all — no direction to
read, no amount to store; nothing enters. The minus sign was never a quantity — it was a
direction worn as a number. Direction goes in a label; amount goes in the ledger. And the
labels are just labels: you cannot multiply them. **This is not a chosen convention: once
there are no negative objects (P3), storing signed material as (direction, positive amount)
is the only option left — the alternatives all smuggle negatives back in.**

## 2) Statement

**σ (defined):** the **orientation tag** — a finite label naming an oriented relation
reading, one of the two distinguished readings of an oriented relation pair
(source→target, target→source). Σ_rel is the finite set of these tags for the relation at
hand. σ carries NO arithmetic (GUARD_001_005).

**Entry representation (P3-forced):** every signed object x entering an internal card from
corpus or conventional sources is held as the pair

$$x \;\mapsto\; (\sigma,\ |x|), \qquad \sigma \in \Sigma_{\text{rel}},\quad |x| \in \mathcal{D}_{+},$$

where |x| is the magnitude, an ordinary strictly-positive holding-typed quantity
(DEF_002_001). A conventional "0" carries no sign and no orientation — it is not a signed
object, falls outside the domain, and **nothing enters** (the no-event-exists predicate,
GUARD_001_008; D-043).

**No tag arithmetic:** Σ_rel carries NO composition. Orientation reversal is re-description
— the swap of the relation's two ends — not an operation on tags; that two successive
swaps restore the original reading is substitution of descriptions, not a multiplication
law (the fence is GUARD_001_005).

## 3) Input contract (GR-1)

Objects: x (external signed object — exists only on the input side); σ (tag, typed: finite
label, no algebra); |x| (𝒟+ element, tag-matched to its register family, TD-009). Domains:
per-relation tag pairs (nonzero signed objects — a signless "0" is out of domain); the
quantity semigroup (DEF_002_001). Quantifiers: universal over entering signed objects.
Limit mode: none.

## 4) Why the form is forced (the retype rationale — DEF, not CONV)

CONV_S01_009 typed itself CONV on the claim that the entry rule is "a choice among named
alternatives." It is not — every listed alternative is rejected by P3, not by taste:

1. **Framework-wide signed quantities** — rejected: contradicts P3 (negatives are not
   objects; DEF_002_004 §1 "no −1 apple ever exists"; DEF_002_016).
2. **Tag algebra with multiplication** — rejected: reimports the sign group under a new
   name; fenced at GUARD_001_005 (F-153b).
3. **BRIDGE-only handling, no internal decomposition** — rejected: internal cards must
   reference oriented transfers natively (S04 escapement; the incidence machinery's
   P3-native restatement, DEF_002_004 §8).

Given P3 (no negative objects) and the internal-oriented-transfer requirement, the
orientation × strictly-positive-magnitude form is the **only** coherent internal
representation — it is forced. The residual free choice is naming only (σ, Σ_rel), which is
definition work. Hence DEF, not CONV.

## 5) Scope & non-overclaim

A booking representation for internal holding + the σ typing. Does not claim every signed
corpus object successfully enters this way (the armed adequacy program, DEF_002_004 §8).
Does not define the BRIDGE map back to signed notation (a separate P7 card, DEF_002_004 §9)
— the entry of EXTERNAL signed material is a P7 import interface, named not built here.
Guard references: GUARD_001_005 (the no-tag-arithmetic fence), GUARD_001_006.

## 7) Verification

Witness (exact-symbolic, QQ; copied/adapted from CONV_S01_009_witness.py per D-064): apply
the entry representation to a deterministic sample of exact signed rationals, asserting for
each: the produced magnitude is in 𝒟+ (strictly positive, exact), the produced tag is one
of the relation's two declared readings, and the pair determines the input (injective on
the sample — no information silently dropped). Domain-exclusion probe: x = 0 → rejected
(nothing booked). Structural assertion: the tag type defines no composition operation
(tag·tag absent/raises — checked, not assumed). Can-fail: corrupt the rule to pass the
signed value through as magnitude → the positivity assertion MUST fail. A pass certifies
internal consistency of the definition, not its adequacy (TD-011).
