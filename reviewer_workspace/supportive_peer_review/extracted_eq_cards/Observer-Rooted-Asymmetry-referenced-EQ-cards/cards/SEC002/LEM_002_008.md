## 1) Plain language

Existence is a distinguishing — telling A apart from not-A (AX_001_001) — and the first such
distinguishing counts as **one** (THM_002_007). That one is **indivisible**: you cannot have a
fraction of it (THM_002_007 Cor 2). This card asks the next question, the one the whole Theorem
of Time rides on: for that one to **stay one** across an interval, what is required — and how does
a single indivisible unit ever become richer than just "one"?

Here is the answer, and it is the heart of the relational lift. **An indivisible unit, taken by
itself, cannot lift to a second degree of freedom.** There is nothing inside a bare "1" to
furnish a second independent direction — it does not divide (it is indivisible), and reading a
"winding" or a "turn" off the lone unit is **empty**: the winding/count of the unit **is** the
information (I = 1, the *first* degree of freedom, THM_002_007), not a second one. So a single
unit cannot lift to two on its own.

What it **can** do is **relate**. To persist as itself across an interval, the unit must be
**re-identified** — you must be able to tell that the distinction at the far end of the interval
is *the same* distinction you started with. And re-identification **is a relation**: a same-as
map across the interval's **two ends** (the reciprocal — "the after-end thing is the before-end
thing"). That relation has **two ends**, and **its two-endedness IS the second degree of
freedom.** The unit never divides; it **relates** to its re-identified self, and the relating —
not the unit — is what supplies the second DoF.

Why re-identification is forced (not optional): suppose the unit persists but is **not**
re-identifiable — there is nothing by which it is the same distinction later. Then it has left
**no record**: at the far end there is no admitted trace of it. But "no record" is exactly the
non-admission boundary (AX_001_001) — the case IMPORT_001_007 fixes as not a thing in the book at
all. So a non-re-identifiable distinction does not persist as one unit; it collapses into
non-admission. To persist as one unit, the unit **must** be re-identified.

And why the lift to two needs the relation specifically (not the unit, not one direction):
picture the unit living in **one** degree of freedom. The only way to "return to itself" along
one axis is to **retrace** — go out and come back along the very same axis — which **un-does** the
distinguishing (walks A back into ¬A) and again leaves **no record**. A retrace is an erasure, not
a re-identification. A genuine same-as relation across the two ends — going out one way and being
re-identified as the same by a *distinct* return — needs a **second** end, a second direction:
the relation's two-endedness. **So re-identification cannot happen individually in one DoF; the
relation furnishes the second.** That second degree of freedom — the plane in which the persisting
unit re-identifies itself — is what the next card (THM_002_014) measures and calls **Time**.

Now the step that makes "the relation furnishes it" into "the relation **is** it" — the part RSM
asked be proven rather than merely stated. Ask: at this layer, where could a *second* degree of
freedom come from **at all**? There are only three possible origins, and they are exhaustive: from
**inside** the unit, from **outside** it, or **in relation**. *Inside* is barred — the unit is
indivisible (you cannot split a "1" into two directions), and the things one might try to wedge
inside it are each forbidden here: a fraction or decimal (subdivision is downstream, not a primitive
at S01), a zero (no-entry, not a thing in the book), a negative (there are no negatives here — a
"sign" is only the orientation *reading* of a relation, which is already the relating itself, not a
new interior), an infinity (no unbounded primitive is admitted). *Outside* is barred — there is no
space, no metric, no ring or loop or coordinate plane to borrow a direction from; that machinery
belongs to much later layers and importing it here would be smuggling. That leaves exactly one
licensed origin: **in relation** — and existence here *is* relational (AX_001_001). So the two-ended
re-identification relation is not merely *a* source of the second degree of freedom; it is the
**only** licensed one. Put that next to what we already showed — that a non-erasing
re-identification *requires* a second degree of freedom — and the conclusion is forced: the thing
required has exactly one place it can come from, and that place is the relation's two-endedness.
**Therefore the relation's two-endedness IS the second degree of freedom** — proven, not asserted.

That is the relational lift, stated crisply: **existence is relational (AX_001_001); persistence
forces re-identification (D-052); re-identification is a relation whose two-endedness is the
second degree of freedom; the indivisible unit lifts to that second DoF only by relating, never
individually.** The higher rungs — a pair forcing a third (c = 3), a triple forcing a non-coplanar
fourth (4 cells) — are the named **forward program** (THM_003_012, THM_004_016), *not proved here*.

## 4) Proof (complete — the relational lift)

**Step 0 (the setup — an indivisible unit, the persistence question).** Let r be a distinction
admitted as one unit of existence: r is a relation-token (AX_001_001) with existence count
I(r) = 1 (THM_002_007), and the unit is **indivisible** (THM_002_007 Cor 2 — no fraction
of a relation-token). Let an **interval** be any before/after span of the book (bookkeeping only —
no metric, no S02+ content). Question: under what condition does r persist as the SAME one unit
across the interval, and how does the lone unit acquire a second degree of freedom at all?

**Step 1 (persist-as-itself requires re-identification — a same-as relation across the interval).**
"r persists as one unit across the interval" means the thing admitted at the far (after) end is
the SAME distinction r as at the near (before) end — one unit, not a fresh, unrelated admission.
By AX_001_001 every admitted thing is a *distinguishable* relation-token; to say the after-end
thing is *the same as* r is to be able to **distinguish it as r** — to **re-identify** it. So
"persists as itself" carries a **same-as relation** across the interval, and that same-as relation
is exactly **re-identification** (the relation by which the after-end distinction is told to be r).

**Step 2 (no re-identification ⟹ no record ⟹ non-admission — re-identification is FORCED).**
Suppose, for contradiction, r is admitted as one unit yet is NOT re-identified across the
interval: there is no relation by which the after-end distinction is told to be r. Then at the
after end there is no admitted trace by which r is present *as r* — **no record of r** across the
interval. (An interval marks before/after, but a bare interval supplies no same-as relation, by
hypothesis.) "No record of r" is precisely the no-entry boundary of AX_001_001 and the
zero-exclusion of IMPORT_001_007 / THM_002_007 Cor 1: a thing with no admitted trace is **not
admitted** — the non-admission boundary, a typed predicate of non-admission (D-043), never an
admitted one-unit existence, never a zero-object. So the supposed "admitted one unit, not
re-identified" does NOT persist as one unit; it has collapsed into non-admission (TD-001, mention
not use). Therefore: **persist-as-one-unit ⟹ re-identified** ((P-reident)).

**Step 3 (the SECOND DoF SOURCE LEMMA — the two-ended re-identification relation is the UNIQUE
S01-licensed source of a second degree of freedom; by exhaustive exclusion of all origins, D-054).**
Where can a *second* degree of freedom for the persisting unit come from at this layer? The origins
**partition exhaustively into three** — and the partition is derived, not asserted, by a bivalence
applied twice. **First bivalence (intrinsic vs extrinsic):** a degree of freedom is either had by the
unit *independent of anything else* (intrinsic — origin (1), **from within**) or *not* (extrinsic);
there is no third truth-value, so this split is complete. **Second bivalence (on the extrinsic
branch: imported-relatum vs grounded-in-the-relation):** an extrinsic degree of freedom is grounded
either in *another object/structure the unit is placed against* (imported — origin (2), **from
outside**) or in *the relating itself* (origin (3), **in relation**); again no third option. So the
origins are exactly **(1) from within / (2) from outside / (3) in relation** — exhaustive because
each split is a genuine bivalence (P-or-not-P), not a list that might be short an item. There is no
fourth *kind* of origin: a distinguishable direction is intrinsic, or extrinsic-imported, or
extrinsic-relational. Take the three in turn.

- **(1) From within (internal subdivision) — BARRED.** The unit is **indivisible** (THM_002_007
  Cor 2): it cannot split into two independent directions — there is no sub-structure inside a bare
  "1" to be a second DoF, and dividing it is forbidden. The candidate interior structures one might
  try to wedge in are each independently non-primitive at S01: a **sub-unit / decimal** (subdivision
  is downstream, not an S01 primitive — P3 / TD-028); a **zero**-object (no-entry, not a thing in
  the book — P1 / IMPORT_001_007 / TD-001); a **negative** primitive (there are no negatives at S01
  — P3 / TD-002; and a "sign" is only the **orientation reading** of a relation, σ in CONV_S01_009
  fenced by GUARD_001_005, which is *already the relating itself* — origin (3), not a new interior);
  an **infinity / unbounded magnitude** (no unbounded primitive is admitted — P6). Also barred by
  this branch: the **lone-unit "winding"** read — that read is **empty**, because the winding/count
  of the unit **is** the information count I = 1 (THM_002_007), the *first* DoF, not a second
  (D-053(a)/(b): "1 is the winding/count; it is the information, not the time"). So the internal
  origin furnishes **no** second DoF.
- **(2) From outside (imported external structure) — BARRED.** Borrowing a direction from a space, a
  metric, a coordinate plane, a ring/loop, a winding-as-spatial-loop, or any topology is **not
  licensed at S01**: this layer is **pre-metric** — no distance, length, or geometry exists yet
  (TD-019), and importing later-layer machinery to ground a base-layer object is forbidden (TD-020 /
  TD-022). So the external origin furnishes no *licensed* second DoF here.
- **(3) In relation (relational two-endedness) — LICENSED, and the only one left.** Existence at
  this layer **is relational** distinguishability (AX_001_001). A relation the persisting unit
  enters has its **two ends** — and that is a structure of the *relating*, not of the unit's
  interior and not borrowed from outside. By Step 2 persistence **forces** exactly one such relation:
  re-identification (the same-as map across the interval's two ends). So the relational origin is
  not only licensed — it is **present and forced**.

Origins (1) and (2) furnish no licensed second DoF; origin (3) does. **Therefore the two-ended
re-identification relation is the UNIQUE S01-licensed source of a second degree of freedom.** (The
exhaustiveness is by the in-itself / from-outside / in-relation trichotomy — a complete partition of
where a degree of freedom can originate — so this is not a flat list that might be missing a case;
TD-018, the load-bearing exhaustiveness, is graded by the verifier per D-054.)

**Step 4 (the IS, by composition — necessity + source-uniqueness; the relational lift).** Combine
two established facts. **(N) Necessity** (Step 5, below): a non-erasing re-identification of the
indivisible unit **requires** a second degree of freedom (the only individual 1-DoF return is the
retrace, which erases r). **(S) Source-uniqueness** (Step 3): the **only** S01-licensed source of a
second degree of freedom is the two-ended re-identification relation. Compose: the second DoF that
re-identification *requires* must come from *somewhere*, and there is **exactly one** licensed place
it can come from — the relation's two-endedness; and that source furnishes **precisely a two-ended
structure** (an outbound end where r is, a return end where r is re-identified as r — Step 5 shows
they cannot coincide without erasing r). The thing **required** and the thing the **unique source
furnishes** are therefore the **same** structure — not merely correlated. **Hence the
re-identification relation's two-endedness IS the second degree of freedom** (not just *supplies*
one — IS it, by source-uniqueness). The lift is supplied by the **relation**, not the unit (Step 3
origin (1) furnishes none): the indivisible unit lifts to a second DoF **only by relating to its
re-identified self**. **This is the relational lift: the lift to the second degree of freedom is
RELATIONAL, never individual, and the relation's two-endedness IS that second DoF** (AX_001_001's
relational ground carried to the persisting case, and made load-bearing — with (1) and (2)
unlicensed, the relational source is the forced one).

**Step 5 (the NECESSITY (N) — a non-erasing re-identification requires ≥ 2 DoF; the two ends cannot
collapse, the 1-DoF retrace is no re-identification).** Could the relation's two ends coincide —
could re-identification happen in a single direction (1 DoF)? No. In one degree of freedom the only
"return to itself" is to **retrace**: go out along the one axis and come back along the very same
axis. But retracing **un-does** the distinguishing — it walks A back into ¬A, **un-distinguishing**
them — and so leaves **no record** (Step 2's non-admission case: a retrace is an erasure, not a
re-identification). For the after-end r to be re-identified as r *without* un-distinguishing it, the
return end must be **distinct** from the outbound end (a distinct return phase, not the outbound run
backwards). Two **distinct** ends are two independent directions — **≥ 2 degrees of freedom**.
Hence a non-erasing re-identification **REQUIRES** a second degree of freedom (the necessity (N)
used in Step 4), and the relation's two ends are genuinely two (they cannot collapse to one without
erasing r).

$$\underbrace{\text{persist-as-one-unit} \Rightarrow \text{re-identified}}_{\text{(P-reident), Step 2}}; \quad
\underbrace{\text{non-erasing re-identification} \Rightarrow \text{requires } \ge 2\text{ DoF}}_{\text{(N), Step 5}}; \quad
\underbrace{\text{unique licensed source of a 2nd DoF} = \text{the two-ended relation}}_{\text{(S), Step 3}}$$
$$\therefore\quad \text{the re-identification relation's two-endedness } \textbf{IS}\text{ the 2nd DoF (relational, never individual) — Step 4.}$$

∎ **(the relational lift; the 1→2 lift proved RELATIONAL — and the IS-identification PROVEN by
necessity (N) + source-uniqueness (S), not asserted.)**

**Why this is FORCED, not a definition of "unit"/"lift" (the TD-011 hazard cleared), and why the IS
is PROVEN not asserted (D-054).** The requirement is not smuggled into "unit" or "lift." The unit
count I = 1 and its indivisibility are THM_002_007's (they say nothing about persistence or a
second DoF). The re-identification requirement is DERIVED (Step 2: a non-re-identified unit leaves no
record, and no record is non-admission — grounded in AX_001_001 + IMPORT_001_007 Cor 1). The
**necessity (N)** is DERIVED (Step 5: the retrace erases, so a non-erasing re-identification needs
≥ 2 DoF). The **source-uniqueness (S)** is DERIVED (Step 3: the exhaustive in-itself / from-outside
/ in-relation trichotomy — internal subdivision and imported topology are unlicensed at S01, leaving
the relational two-endedness the unique licensed source). The **IS** is then DERIVED by **composition**
(Step 4: the required second DoF has exactly one licensed source, which furnishes exactly a two-ended
structure — so the required DoF and the relation's two-endedness are the same). The IS is therefore a
*conclusion*, never a premise or a definition. The claim can lose — falsifiers **F-L0** (an alternate
licensed source defeats source-uniqueness), F-L1, F-L2, F-L3 are executable constructions. Pre-building
the lift into "unit," or asserting the IS by fiat, would be the traps; this proof does neither.

**What is NOT proved here (TD-008/TD-020, the honest scope).** The 2→3 rung (a pair cannot
self-reference/self-balance → c = 3) and the 3→4 rung (coplanarity → a non-coplanar fourth → 4
cells) are NOT proved on this card. They are the **named forward program**: the 2→3 rung is the
candidate that would force c = 3 (the long-reserved THM_003_012 minimality), and the 3→4 rung is
anchored at the promoted THM_004_016 (n = 4 minimal for non-coplanar closure). This card proves the
**relational lift** — the 1→2 lift, the one the Theorem of Time uses — and names the rest as leads
(§9(iii)). The closure MEASURE (2π) is THM_002_014's, not this card's.

## 4′) Why this is derived

The relational lift is derived from, in order: **(1)** existence is **relational**
distinguishability (AX_001_001 — same-as is a distinguishing, hence re-identification is a
relation, and the ground is relational); **(2)** the first distinction is one **indivisible** unit
(THM_002_007 + Cor 2 — the thing that must persist, that cannot divide); **(3)** no record =
non-admission (IMPORT_001_007 / THM_002_007 Cor 1 — the alternative to re-identification collapses
to the boundary); **(4)** the **exhaustive trichotomy of origins** (in-itself / from-outside /
in-relation — a complete partition of where a degree of freedom can arise), with (1)+(2) closing the
internal door and the S01 pre-metric discipline (P4/TD-019/TD-020) closing the external door, leaving
the relational origin the unique licensed source (the Second DoF Source Lemma, Step 3). Remove any
one and the derivation fails: without (1) "same-as" has no meaning, the lift has no relational
ground, and the relational door (3) is not licensed; without (2) there is no indivisible unit to
lift, and a divisible unit could (wrongly) furnish a second DoF by splitting (the internal door
re-opens); without (3) "no record" could stand as an admitted object and the Step-2 contradiction
dissolves; without (4)'s exhaustiveness the "unique source" is only "a source" and the IS falls back
to the necessity-only claim (TD-018). The relational lift — and the IS-identification it carries —
is the conclusion of this chain, never its premise.
