## 1) Plain language

This card is a **gate**, not a construction. We are not yet building the bridge from our discrete
bookkeeping to the smooth, physics-facing "continuous phase." We are writing down — up front and
precisely — **what such a bridge would have to consume, and where it is not allowed to go** without a
proper readout/observer layer (which lives later, at S07). And we build none of it.

Here is the whole content. Our internal order is fixed: **discrete oriented closures → completed
postings → count and rate**. A "continuous phase" is not one of these internal steps; it only ever
appears on a **typed bridge/readout layer**, downstream — there is **no generic "apparent continuous
phase"** inside the lattice. So any future bridge that wants to *represent* our discrete structure as a
continuous mod-2π phase must (RC-1) **take its inputs from the postings and the count**, never invent the
phase first; must (RC-2) **carry the orientation σ as a separate piece** and must **not pretend the
phase gives σ back** (we already proved the phase alone loses σ); may (RC-3) put the continuous phase
**only on the bridge side**; must (RC-4) leave anything that looks like *time-evolution of the phase*
(phase as a function of time, a rate of change) **for later, behind the time/observer gate**; and may
(RC-5) use comparison tools like the Archimedean squeeze or the lattice-π correction **only as a
late-stage comparison to continuous physics**, never as the thing that *creates* the rate, the phase, or
σ. The archive notes that might help that comparison (the staircase and lattice-π material) are named
here as **future candidates only** — to be pulled and verified later, never leaned on now.

Two things this card deliberately does **not** do: it does **not** build the map (that is a later,
separately-gated card), and it does **not** open the static-phase BRIDGE, OB-D2 (what σ *does*), the
conjecture, or any S07/time work. It just fixes the boundary so the next card can't smuggle a continuous
clock or an observer in through the back door.
