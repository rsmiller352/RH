## 1) Plain language

We have called σ an "orientation tag" in two different places, and this card proves those two places are
**the same thing wearing two faces** — so σ is one honest piece of structure, not a word reused.

Face one: the **gap**. When two histories differ in action, σ records **which way** the difference runs —
which history carries the more (DEF_006_005). That is the *orientation of the closure*: when the gap
completes a whole unit and the closure posts, σ says which oriented reading the closure realized.

Face two: the **paired posting**. Every allowed move in the book is written twice — once from each end —
and the bookkeeping law (THM_002_012) guarantees every record sits beside its co-reading, paired by the
orientation swap ι_or. σ is exactly the label that swap flips. So σ says **which side** of the paired
posting a record is on.

Why are these the same σ and not two unrelated labels? Because the completed-closure posting **is the
booking of the gap**: the thing that gets written when the gap completes (THM_006_007) is a record of that
gap, and a record's side (its ι_or-reading) is just the gap's orientation read off again. One orientation,
read on the gap or read on the posting — same σ. That equivalence is the theorem.

Three careful limits. σ stays a **discrete label** — you cannot do arithmetic on it; the "swap" ι_or is a
re-description (swap twice and you are back where you started — by *saying it again*, not by multiplying
σ by itself). σ is **not** something you can read back out of the smooth "continuous phase" — we already
proved the phase alone loses σ, which is exactly *why* σ has to live here, on the discrete side. And this
card stops at the orientation/side role: it does **not** yet say σ chooses the *next* move, or which side a
*bridge readout* sees, or anything about an observer — those are open, gated, or later.

## 4) Proof (the forced equivalence — σ's two facings)

**Step 1 — σ orients the gap/closure (DEF_006_005, DEF_002_002).** DEF_006_005 types the gap as
g_Φ = (σ, |g_Φ|): when two histories' actions differ, σ ∈ Σ_rel (DEF_002_002) is the orientation — which
history carries the larger action, a finite reading-label (source→target vs target→source), with **no tag
arithmetic** (GUARD_001_005). So σ is the orientation of the gap. By THM_006_007 the completed-closure
posting is the forced record written at unit completion of |g_Φ| (ΔI = 1 FOLLOWS), and it **reads g_Φ** —
so the posting carries σ as the **orientation of the closure**.

**Step 2 — σ determines the side of the paired posting (THM_002_012, DEF_002_010).** THM_002_012 (the Law
of Symmetry Conservation at S01) proves that licensed transfers generate ι_or-**balanced** record
multisets: every record sits beside its co-reading under the orientation involution ι_or. DEF_002_010
types ι_or as the involution that **swaps the relation's two readings** (entering as re-description, not a
tag operation — DEF_002_010 §4(e) / GUARD_001_005). The completed-closure posting is a licensed record
(THM_006_007's forced posting), hence ι_or-paired; ι_or swaps it to its co-reading (the opposite
orientation). σ names **which** of the two ι_or-related sides the posting occupies — the **side of the
paired posting**.

**Step 3 — the booking identity forces the coincidence.** The completed-closure posting is the **booking
of the gap**: by THM_006_007 the record the escapement writes at completion is precisely a record of
g_Φ. DEF_002_002 fixes ONE orientation tag σ ∈ Σ_rel per oriented relation (the reading-label). Evaluated
on the gap, that tag is the gap's orientation (Step 1); evaluated on the gap's booking posting, the same
tag is the posting's ι_or-reading (Step 2) — because the posting **is** the gap booked, its reading is the
gap's orientation read off again. There is no second, independent orientation label in play (DEF_002_002:
one tag, one meaning; GUARD_001_005: no composition that could manufacture a second). Hence the posting's
ι_or-side **equals** the gap's orientation σ.

**Step 4 — ∴ two facings of one structure.** By Steps 1–3, *orientation of the closure* (i) and *side of
the paired posting* (ii) are the **same σ**, evaluated on the gap or on its booking posting. They are
**two facings of one discrete orientation structure**, not two unrelated meanings. The equivalence is
**forced** by the booking identity (THM_006_007) + the single-reading-tag typing (DEF_002_002) + the
ι_or-pairing (THM_002_012 / DEF_002_010); break any of these and the coincidence fails (F-S06sig-1). ∎

**(No arithmetic — ι_or is re-description.)** ι_or∘ι_or restores the original reading by **substitution of
descriptions** (swap the two ends, swap back), NOT by σ·σ = e; no product/sum/exponent/inverse on σ is
formed anywhere (DEF_002_002 / GUARD_001_005). The equivalence is a coincidence of **labels on the same
object**, not a group identity.

**(Not recovered from phase — RC-2; THM_S06_003.)** σ's role is wholly discrete. The continuous phase
φ_rel = 2π·|g_Φ| (mod 2π) is magnitude-only; for the same-magnitude-opposite-orientation pair
g_a = (σ₊, m), g_b = (σ₋, m): φ_rel(g_a) = φ_rel(g_b) but σ(g_a) ≠ σ(g_b) — the phase does **not** determine
σ. So σ is **not** recovered from the phase (GATE_007_004 RC-2; THM_S06_003); its content lives exactly
here, on the discrete posting side — which is **why** the orientation/side role is the right home for it.
