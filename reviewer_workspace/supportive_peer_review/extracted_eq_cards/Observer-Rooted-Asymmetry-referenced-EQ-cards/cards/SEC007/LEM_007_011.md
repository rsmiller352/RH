## 1) Plain language

The substrate's escapement, at the moment a leftover fills to a whole unit, is **forced** to record
one completed unit of existence — it has no choice (THM_005_009; the Law of Existence). That forced
record is one strictly-positive thing, and like every record in this framework it is read from its
oriented sides (DEF_002_010; THM_005_010). The aperture, at that same moment, registers one full 2π
turn — one completed closure (ROLEDEF_006_008). OB-A (THM_007_007) ties the closure to **that very forced
output** — the same completion, not a separate event.

Now count and pay. **Count:** OB-B (THM_007_008) proves the completed-unit count and the closure count
are **one** count — there are not two. **Pay:** OB-C (DEF_007_009) gives **one** service burden per
completed unit — there are not two. So if there were two genuinely independent processes here — one
"substrate" process completing its own unit, one "aperture" process completing its own — we would see
**two** counts and **two** services. We see one of each. So **there are not two independent processes**:
the completion is a single forced unit, counted once, serviced once, with the completed-unit-side
reading and the closure-side reading as its two oriented sides; σ rides through as the orientation
(THM_007_005), never dropped.

That is all this card claims — and it stops exactly there, honestly. "Counted once, serviced once" rules
out two *independent* processes, but it does **not** rule out two processes that always finish *together*
as one count and split one service. So the question "is this **one** relation read two ways, or **two**
relations locked to co-complete?" stays open. Closing it is the next card's job (THM_007_012). This card
narrows the field; it does not declare the winner.

## 4) Proof (complete)

**Step 1 (one forced completed unit).** By THM_005_009, while the residual ratio is below the unit
account no unit has completed — the open-account / record-absent predicate, **no event** (never a stored
zero). Reaching the unit account **forces** exactly one completed unit, ΔI = 1 (the Law of Existence:
completed existence must be recorded). By THM_002_007 existence is counted in whole units (the first
I = 1) and is admitted as a distinguishable relation-token (AX_001_001). So each completion is **one
forced completed unit of existence** — not contingent, not fractional, not multiple.

**Step 2 (the closure is the closure-side reading of that same forced output).** By OB-A (THM_007_007
§2) the tick events are the forced ΔI = 1 postings (one per completed unit-account, THM_005_009), and
`tick_closure_map` sends the tick of each completed unit-account to the 2π closure of **that same
completion** (ROLEDEF_006_008). So the 2π closure is keyed to the forced completed-unit output itself — it
is the **closure-side reading of the same completion**, not of a separate event.

**Step 3 (one count — independent completion excluded).** By OB-B (THM_007_008), over every window of
the discrete cycle index ΔN_tick = ΔI_closure (forced by OB-A's bijection restricted to the window). So
the completed-unit-side count and the closure-side count are **one** count per completion. Suppose, for
contradiction, the completion were **two independently-completing** processes (a substrate process
completing its own unit and an aperture process completing its own). Each independent completion posts
its **own** unit-count increment (Step 1: a completed unit is forced to be recorded), so two independent
completions over a window would post **two** unit-records against the closure side's **one** — so
ΔN_tick would **exceed** ΔI_closure over the window, contradicting OB-B's count identity ΔN_tick =
ΔI_closure. Hence the completion is **not two independently-completing processes**: it is
**singly-counted**. (The counts are strictly-positive count readouts ∈ ℤ≥1; the contradiction is a
count inequality, no signed quantity — TD-034.)

**Step 4 (one service — independent servicing excluded).** By OB-C (DEF_007_009), each completed
closure carries **one** per-unit service/completion burden cost[·] ∈ 𝒟+ (one service per completed unit,
σ-blind-in-value, measured against u_Φ; the no-completion case the open-account predicate, never a
stored zero). Suppose, for contradiction, the completion were **two independently-serviced** processes;
each independent process completing a unit carries its **own** service burden (every completed unit is
serviced — DEF_007_009), so the completion would carry **two** service burdens — contradicting OB-C's
single per-completion burden. Hence the completion is **not two independently-serviced processes**: it is
**singly-serviced**.

**Step 5 (two oriented readings of one completion; σ carried).** By DEF_002_010 a record is an oriented
reading, and one strictly-positive transfer is read from two oriented sides (THM_005_010: the runtime
completion is one strictly-positive unit read from two oriented sides — orientation × strictly-positive
magnitude, no signed object). The forced completed-unit output is the **completed-unit-side oriented
reading** (Step 1); the 2π closure is the **closure-side oriented reading** of the **same** completion
(Step 2). σ (THM_007_005; GATE_007_006: σ = which side of the paired posting) rides through both
readings as orientation/readout — **carried**, never erased, never recovered from cost (DEF_007_009
σ-blind-in-value) or from the continuous phase (the same-magnitude-opposite-orientation lossiness, RC-2),
never collapsed into a scalar.

**Step 6 (the narrowing, and the honest boundary).** Combining: the completion is **one** forced

completed-unit-side output and the 2π closure are two oriented readings (Step 5). Therefore there are
