## 1) Plain language

The previous card (OB-A) proved the substrate's **ticks** and the phase's **closures** are the same
heartbeat counted twice — one tick per completion, one closure per completion, paired one-for-one. This
card draws the immediate consequence the junction needs: **the two clocks run at the same rate.**

Count the ticks over any stretch of cycles, and count the closures over the **same** stretch: because they
are paired one-for-one (OB-A), the two counts are **equal**. Divide each by the same number of cycles, and
the two **rates** are equal too: the completion-rate r_int (ticks per cycle) equals the closure-rate
r_closure (closures per cycle). That is the whole theorem — and it is exact at every finite stretch, not
just in the long run (the long-run value, Ω, is just the shared number both rates settle to).

The careful part is what this is **not**. It is **not** a statement about physical time — both rates are
counted over the substrate's own **cycle index**, never against a clock; there is no "rate of change in
time," no dφ/dt. It is **not** the bridge rate İ = E/ħ — no ħ, no energy. It does **not** need the
calibration constant κ: because OB-A already pairs the events one-for-one **inside** the substrate, the
two internal rates are simply equal — κ (and physical time, and relativity) only matter later, at the
bridge, which is not this card. And the orientation σ rides **alongside**: an oriented tick pairs with the
oriented closure of the same σ; σ is never dropped and never read out of the phase.

Finally, what it sets up but does **not** claim. This is the rate equality the big conjecture
(update-rate = phase-rate) will need — but the conjecture is the *unification*, with its physics meaning
and its bridge, and that comes **last**. This card proves only the internal equality, prices no energy
(that is OB-C), and asserts no conjecture.

## 4) Proof (the internal rate equality)

**Step 1 — the two rates are difference quotients over the same cycle index (DEF_007_001 / ROLEDEF_006_008).**
r_int := ΔN_tick/Δx (DEF_007_001): N_tick the tick count (completions per cycle), the discrete difference
quotient over the update/cycle index Δx (k ≠ n ≠ t — DEF_005_012; no continuous t, no derivative d/dt).
r_closure := ΔI_closure/Δx: I_closure = ⌊|g_Φ|⌋ the closure count (the 2π closures, ROLEDEF_006_008), the
discrete difference quotient over the **same** cycle index Δx. Both are dimensionless ratios of two
strictly-positive counts per cycle; the no-completion window is the open-account predicate (never a 0-rate
/ I = 0).

**Step 2 — the per-window count identity (OB-A, THM_007_007).** By OB-A, tick_closure_map: {tick events} →
{closure events} is a **bijection** (each completed unit-account is exactly one 2π closure, one tick ↔ one
closure). Restricted to a window [x₁, x₂] of the cycle index, the bijection pairs the ticks whose
completion falls in the window with the closures whose completion falls in the window, **1-to-1** — so the
**number of ticks in the window equals the number of closures in the window**:
$$\Delta N_{\text{tick}} \;=\; N_{\text{tick}}(x_2) - N_{\text{tick}}(x_1) \;=\; I_{\text{closure}}(x_2) - I_{\text{closure}}(x_1) \;=\; \Delta I_{\text{closure}}.$$
(The cumulative counts coincide, N_tick(x) = I_closure(x), because each completed unit up to cycle x has
exactly one tick and one closure — OB-A; hence the window differences are equal.)

**Step 3 — the rate equality.** Dividing the equal counts by the same Δx (the readout-level ratio,
DEF_007_001 discipline):
$$r_{\text{int}} \;=\; \frac{\Delta N_{\text{tick}}}{\Delta x} \;=\; \frac{\Delta I_{\text{closure}}}{\Delta x} \;=\; r_{\text{closure}}.$$
The equality holds at **every finite window** (the count identity is exact, not a limit), and a fortiori in
the long run (both rates → Ω, the shared value, THM_005_019, readout-only). ∎

**(Definition / theorem — the OB-B obligation.)** r_int (DEF_007_001) and r_closure (the closure-count
difference quotient over ROLEDEF_006_008's count) are the **definitions**; the **equality** r_int = r_closure
is the **theorem**, forced by OB-A's count identity (Step 2) — **not** a claim about Ω's value (Ω is the
shared readout). 

**(Can-fail, P0 — the risky claim.)** Falsifiable: a window where ΔN_tick ≠ ΔI_closure (the per-window
tick count ≠ closure count — a completed tick with no closure, or a closure with no tick, contradicting
OB-A) gives r_int ≠ r_closure. The promoted bijection (OB-A) forbids the mismatch; a counterexample within
the licensed machinery refutes the theorem (F-S06rate-eq-1).

**(Internal only — no physical time, no İ, no κ.)** Both r_int and r_closure are difference quotients over
the substrate's **own cycle index** Δx — never against a physical clock; there is **no** d/dt over physical
time, **no** phase(t), **no** dφ/dt. The bridge rate İ = E/ħ stays **bridge-side** (DEF_007_001-named,
deferred), never internal. **No κ** is needed: OB-A pairs the events **1:1 inside** the substrate, so the
internal rates are equal κ-free; the physical-time / κ / relativistic calibration belongs to the **later
bridge** (the conjecture / S07), not here.

**(σ carried; sets up not asserts.)** Each completion's tick and closure carry the same σ (the orientation
