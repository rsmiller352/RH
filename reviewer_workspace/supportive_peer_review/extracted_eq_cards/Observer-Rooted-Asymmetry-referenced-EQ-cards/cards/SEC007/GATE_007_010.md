κ needed. So RSM **split** OB-B in two: the **κ-free internal equality** (which OB-B's card, THM_007_008,
just proved), and a **later κ calibration** that only matters when we bridge to physical time.

go back and edit it; instead this new card records: the contract used κ-language; the ruling split OB-B;
THM_007_008 discharged only the κ-free internal half; κ is **set aside for later** (deferred, not denied);
and the internal identity is now discharged by THM_007_012, while bridge/P0 obligations remain deferred under GATE_007_013. It performs no calibration, builds no time bridge, and opens none of the closed work — it just
keeps the books straight before the next card.
