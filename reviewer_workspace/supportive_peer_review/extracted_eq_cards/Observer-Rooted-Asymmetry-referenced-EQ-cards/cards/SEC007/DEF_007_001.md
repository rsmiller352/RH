## 1) Plain language

The substrate ticks: it completes one unit-account at a time, and each completion is **forced** by the
Law of Existence (when the running residue reaches one whole unit, a posting must happen — that is the
escapement, THM_005_009). Counting those completions up to cycle x gives N_tick(x). The substrate has
one **verified** long-run rate: completions per cycle settle to Ω (THM_005_019).

This card does one small, careful thing: it **types the internal rate** — "how many completions per
cycle" — as a **difference quotient** of that count, ΔN_tick/Δx. Three things make it honest. First,
it is **discrete**: there is no continuous clock inside the substrate (the update index, the event
count, and physical time are three different things — DEF_005_012), so this is a *difference* over
whole cycles, **not** a derivative d/dt. Second, it is **ours, not physics**: the bridge has a rate
İ = E/ħ (information per unit physical time), but that lives on the bridge and carries ħ; **our**
internal rate carries no ħ and no t — the two are *counterparts* the junction may later try to
equate, but this card does **not** equate them (that equality is the conjecture, and it comes last).
Third, a window with **no completion** is **"the account is still open, residue carried"** — a typed
state, **never** a rate of zero or an I = 0 object.

So this card gives the junction a clean internal rate to talk about, while keeping every fence: no
continuous time, no internal ħ, no zero-object, and — crucially — **no claim yet that this rate equals
the phase rate.**
