## 1) Plain language

S05 and S06 already build their objects in a definite order, and this card just **proves what that
order is** — narrowly, inside our own formal stack, without any grand metaphysical claim and without
assuming anything about what an observer sees.

Here is the internal order, read straight off the cards we already promoted. First there is the **gap**
between two histories, g_Φ — and it comes in two pieces: an **orientation** (the σ tag — which way) and
a **magnitude** (how big). It is built from S01 and S05 ingredients only; **no "continuous phase" goes
into making it.** Next, when the magnitude completes a whole unit, a **posting is forced** — the Law of
Existence says a completed unit must be accounted, so the count ticks up by one; we then **count** those
completed postings, and any **rate** is just a reading of that count. So the internal sequence is:
**discrete oriented closures → completed postings → count / rate structure.**

What about *phase*? In the stack, the only phase that appears at this level is a **mod-2π reading of the
gap** — literally 2π times g_Φ, wrapped to a full turn (ROLEDEF_006_008). It is **defined from** the gap;
it is a **downstream reading**, not an input. And a **genuinely continuous** phase — the smooth,
physics-facing thing — is something else again: it belongs to a **later bridge-side / relativistic /
macroscopic readout** (the complex e^{iτ} form, S05.10, deferred). It is **not** automatically what any
observer sees, and it is **not** the next internal step after discrete closure. So this card is careful:
a continuous-phase *representation* is **downstream** of the discrete posting/count structure, whichever
representation you mean — the S05 mod-2π reading (defined over the gap) or the later relativistic readout
(not built here).

That is the whole claim: in our stack, **discrete oriented closure comes first; any continuous-phase
representation is a readout of it, not its source.** Three guard-rails. (1) This is a statement about
*our formal stack* — the dependency order and the card bodies — **not** a claim that "the discrete is
metaphysically primary." (2) It says **nothing about what an observer sees**: there is no generic
"apparent continuous phase" at the internal level. (3) It does **not** finish OB-D. It only sets the
*direction* the constructive work runs in. The next card (OB-D1) asks the sharper question — *what
discrete oriented closure structure must be in place before a relativistic/physics-facing readout can
represent it as continuous mod-2π phase?* — and if answering it needs S07/readout machinery, OB-D1 stops
short and registers the bridge boundary rather than forcing a map. It also fits the warning we already
recorded (the held diagnostic): because a continuous-phase reading is only a magnitude-readout, it
**loses** the orientation σ — which is exactly why σ has to live on the discrete side (OB-D2).
