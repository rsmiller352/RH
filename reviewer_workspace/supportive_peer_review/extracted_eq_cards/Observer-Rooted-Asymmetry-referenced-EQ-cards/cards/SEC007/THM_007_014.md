## 1) Plain language

`DEF_007_002` said *what* complete closure is: the system has no unpaired residue left — the G-operation has
nothing left to book (G-silence). This card asks the dynamics question: **does the system, while it is running,
ever get there?** The answer is **no — not by running.**

Here is why, in one breath. Positive existence is **counted** (`THM_002_007`) and **non-erasable**
(`LEM_002_013`): once a unit of existence is admitted, the books cannot simply make it go away. The residual it
leaves is a **strictly-positive holding** while it is on the books (`GUARD_005_008`) — and the source is **never
cleared to zero** (`DEF_005_012`); when a unit completes, the entry **discharges** — it *leaves the book* (becomes
absent), forced by the Law of Existence (`THM_005_009`). So far so good: completions do clear entries. But the
system is **driven** — every cycle it produces another strictly-positive residual. So the moment one entry
discharges and the register goes empty, the next cycle **re-fills** it. The empty (closed) state is only ever a
**flicker** between a discharge and the next feed. It never *lasts*.

So while the system runs, it **never settles into "nothing left to book."** Complete closure — the *sustained*
empty state — is simply not where a driven system goes. It is reached only if the **driving stops** (no more
cycles feeding residual) — the **forcing-ceased / self-coupled** limit (G-silence). And note the careful part:
"empty" never means a *zero value* on the books. The framework forbids that (`GUARD_005_008`/`DEF_005_012`). "No
residual" means the register has **left the book** (register-absence), and the existents that were there are
still there — **paired**, conserved, just no longer an open account. Closing the books is not erasing anything.

Two things this card is scrupulous about. **It is a non-erasure result, not a geometry result.** The reason the
system keeps producing residual is that **existence is non-erasable** — not because of any "pressure" or lattice.
The geometry (the void fraction, the packing) sets *how big* each residual is, but the no-go needs only *that*
there is a strictly-positive one each cycle, which is a **type** fact. Γ ("pressure") is nowhere in it — and its
pressure-reading isn't even an established meaning. **And it stops at the no-go.** It says driven dynamics don't
reach closure, and that reaching closure *requires* the driving to cease. It does **not** say *how* the driving
ceases — that mechanism is later (and may be a genuinely new piece).

## 4) Proof (complete)

**Step 1 (residual-present is a strictly-positive typed holding; "no residual" = register-absence, never zero).**
By `GUARD_005_008` §2(3) (instancing `GUARD_001_008`), the residual register `S_res` is **strictly-positive-by-type
when present**; "no unpaired residue remains" is the **typed predicate**; an emptied register **leaves the book**
(register-absence) — **never `S_res(k) = 0`**. By `DEF_005_012` the source is **not cleared to a zero value**. So
the residual is either a strictly-positive holding (present) or absent (left the book) — never zero. Complete
closure (`DEF_007_002`: no unpaired residue) is the **register-absence** predicate, not a zero value. *(No Ω/D/ω_cell
value used — positivity is the type.)*

**Step 2 (positive existence is non-erasable — the driver).** By `THM_002_007` an admitted distinguishability is
a **counted** unit (positive existence). By `LEM_002_013` (the persistence corollary) admitted existence is
**conserved under the spine's operations — its admission-status can change only through a licensed relation**;
the books **cannot erase** it. So a completed unit's residual is **non-erasable**: it does not vanish on its own.
*(The driver is non-erasure — `THM_002_007`+`LEM_002_013` — not Γ/pressure; Γ is absent.)*

**Step 3 (discharge clears an entry but does not clear the source; the driven feed re-populates).** By
`THM_005_009` the discharge is **FORCED** at unit-completion (the Law of Existence): a completed unit posts and the
entry **leaves the book** (Step 1). But by `DEF_005_012` the **source is not cleared** — and in the **driven/cycling
regime** a **strictly-positive residual feed is produced each cycle** (the cycling advances; the feed's positivity
is the `GUARD_005_008` type). So immediately after a discharge empties the register, the next cycle **re-feeds**
it. The register therefore oscillates: present (strictly positive) → discharges (leaves the book, momentarily
absent) → re-fed (present) → … *(The feed's existence each cycle is non-erasure producing a fresh residual; its
size is geometry, not used.)*

**Step 4 (the no-go).** Complete system closure is **sustained** register-absence (Step 1; `DEF_007_002`). By
Step 3, under driving the register is **perpetually re-populated** — register-absence occurs only **transiently**
(between a discharge and the next feed) and is **never sustained**. Therefore **complete system closure is NOT
reached by driven dynamics.** ∎(1)

**Step 5 (the necessary condition).** Suppose, toward the contrary necessity claim, that complete closure
(sustained register-absence) holds. By Step 3, while the per-cycle feed continues, the register is re-populated —
so sustained absence is impossible. Hence sustained register-absence requires the **strictly-positive per-cycle
feed to CEASE** — the **forcing-ceased / G-silence** limit. So **complete closure requires forcing-cessation.**
It is **not** reached by **residual reduction to `S_res = 0`** — that is architecturally forbidden
(`GUARD_005_008`/`DEF_005_012`: never a zero value). ∎(2)

**Step 6 (non-erasure carried).** Throughout, completions **pair** the existents (`THM_002_012` via `LEM_002_013`);
discharge is a register **leaving the book** (register-absence), not a deletion of the existent — existence stays
**counted** (`THM_002_007`). So "no residual" is the matched-balance / register-absence **predicate**, never an
erasure, annihilation, or zero. ∎(3)

**What this proof does NOT use:** no **Γ** / pressure (the driver is non-erasure); no **D/Ω/ω_cell value** (the
feed's positivity is the type, not its magnitude); no **`S_res=0`** / erasure / annihilation (register-absence
predicate, existents persist); no **physical** time / energy / gravity / horizon; no **positive-reaching**
derivation (how the forcing ceases — deferred); no **G value / the /4**.
