## 1) Plain language

We have two internal readouts in hand: `Ψ_support` (a support-payload number, `= 2/(3π)`, built from counting the four occupied cells over the three observer-paired contact facets — `LEM_011_016`) and `N_{Λ,O}=9` (a count of the nine σ-carried Λ/G closure records — `LEM_012_011`). The next step (still open, `OI-074`) is to render these counts into physical magnitudes `|Ψ|`, `|Λ|` through an aperture, a smooth scalar, and eventually SI units.

This card is the **doorway rule** for that step. It says: you may walk through (the destination — aperture, scalar, SI — is *allowed*), **but** you must not lose the count on the way. Concretely, whatever smooth value you produce, the original count must still be **recoverable** — not by trying to invert the smooth number on its own, but by reading it together with the **construction record** the readout already carries (for `Ψ_support`: that it came from `4` cells over `|C_O|=3`; for the Λ side: that it came from `9` σ-carried records via `ReadCount`). The count is never allowed to silently vanish into a bare scalar (`LEM_002_013` non-erasure, applied at the moment of rendering).

The rule also fixes the *posture*: the observer doing the rendering is **internal** (one of the four cells, never an outside spectator on a background grid), and a smooth scalar is always a *presentation of* a recoverable count, never a new primitive that replaces it. The gate **denies the shortcut** (treating a scalar/SI number as primitive and forgetting where the count came from); it **does not deny the destination** (a card or verifier that tries to use this gate to block SI rendering altogether is itself misusing it — `F-S07G70-8`).

## Claim

Any lawful downstream measurement, aperture, scalar, or SI rendering must remain observer-local and must preserve the originating distinguishability count as an accounted source.

Distinguishability is the relational admission ground.

Count is the index readout of admitted distinguishability.

Scale is a measurement/rendering of count through an observer-local aperture or comparison relation.

Thus a downstream rendering is admissible only if the originating count index remains recoverable through the rendered value together with the promoted construction record cited by the rendering.

\[
\operatorname{Dist}_O(R)

\[

This is recorded-construction recovery, not unique inversion of the scalar value alone.

Here \(\mathcal A\) is the later aperture / measurement context. This card does not define \(\mathcal A\), does not compute a scalar, and does not produce SI units.

It gates the bridge.

## Gate Condition

A later aperture, scalar, or SI card passes this gate only if it supplies all of the following.

1. **Observer-local source.**

   The rendered object is a promoted observer-local readout/count object.

2. **Internal observer.**

   The observer is the internal Now-observer/readout relation, not an external spectator.

3. **Distinguishability source.**

   The card identifies the admitted distinguishability domain being rendered.

4. **Count readout.**

   The card identifies the count/index readout:

   \[
   \operatorname{Count}_O(R)
   =
   |\operatorname{Dist}_O(R)|.
   \]

5. **Rendering map.**

   The card defines or cites the aperture / measurement rendering map:

   \[
   \operatorname{Render}_O(-;\mathcal A).
   \]

6. **Recorded-construction recovery.**

   The card defines or cites a recovery map under which the count index is recoverable from the rendered output together with the promoted construction record cited by the rendering:

   \[
   \operatorname{Recover}_O
   \left(
   \operatorname{Render}_O(\operatorname{Count}_O(R);\mathcal A),
   \operatorname{Rec}_O(R)
   \right)
   =
   \operatorname{Count}_O(R).
   \]

   The rendering must account for the count it consumes. The count is not annihilated into an unaccounted output.

   This need not be a unique mathematical inverse of the rendered scalar value alone.

7. **No shortcut.**

   The card does not read a coefficient, scalar, or SI value as primitive.

8. **No external grid.**

   The card does not introduce a background coordinate grid, external observer, or outside universe of records.
