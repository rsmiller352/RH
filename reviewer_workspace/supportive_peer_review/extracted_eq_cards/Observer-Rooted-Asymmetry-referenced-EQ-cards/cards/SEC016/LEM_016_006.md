# One-Event Two-Face Preservation and Count-Recovery Record

## 0. Governance and one lemma claim

This governed formal lemma is the narrow successor required by
`SRC_000227:L42-L44`. It carries exactly one claim:

> The active closure/readout ground supplies one event with two distinct,
> preserved faces. A tagged construction record retains the event identity,
> both faces, and their distinct counts, so exact projection maps recover all
> five fields without combining the counts or assigning a value.

This lemma supplies only the event/face/recovery contract used by the later
\(409\) Bridge.

## 1. Active one-event and two-face ground

`DEF_011_001:L31-L36` supplies one event \(\gamma_\Psi\), with:

\[
n_{\rm post}=2,
\qquad
n_{\rm route}=1,
\qquad
n_{\rm post}\ne n_{\rm route}.
\]

The two counts describe different roles within that same event. They are not
identified, multiplied, added, averaged, or otherwise combined.

`LEM_011_003:L33-L36` supplies the typed separation

\[
\gamma_\Psi
\longmapsto
\left(
\gamma_\Psi^{\rm post},
\gamma_\Psi^{\rm route}
\right),
\]

where the posting face and routing face remain distinct. That active lemma
types the two faces; the preservation claim below is the new carried-record
step and is not attributed retrospectively to the active lemma.

## 2. Tagged preservation record

Let

\[
\iota_{\rm post}:
\{\gamma_\Psi^{\rm post}\}
\longrightarrow
\{\mathrm{post}\}\times\{\gamma_\Psi^{\rm post}\},
\]

\[
\iota_{\rm route}:
\{\gamma_\Psi^{\rm route}\}
\longrightarrow
\{\mathrm{route}\}\times\{\gamma_\Psi^{\rm route}\}
\]

be the canonical injections into the tagged disjoint union. Define

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Rec}_{2f}(\gamma_\Psi)
:=
\left(
\gamma_\Psi,\,
\iota_{\rm post}(\gamma_\Psi^{\rm post}),\,
\iota_{\rm route}(\gamma_\Psi^{\rm route}),\,
n_{\rm post},\,
n_{\rm route}
\right).
}
\]

Because the tags are different,

\[
\iota_{\rm post}(\gamma_\Psi^{\rm post})
\ne
\iota_{\rm route}(\gamma_\Psi^{\rm route}).
\]

Thus the record contains two distinguishable faces of one event, not two
events and not one merged face.

## 3. Exact recovery

Define the projection maps

\[
\operatorname{Recover}_{\rm event}
\left(\operatorname{Rec}_{2f}(\gamma_\Psi)\right)
:=
\gamma_\Psi,
\]

\[
\operatorname{Recover}_{\rm faces}
\left(\operatorname{Rec}_{2f}(\gamma_\Psi)\right)
:=
\left(
\gamma_\Psi^{\rm post},
\gamma_\Psi^{\rm route}
\right),
\]

and

\[
\operatorname{Render}_{2f}
\left(n_{\rm post},n_{\rm route}\right)
:=
\left\langle n_{\rm post},n_{\rm route}\right\rangle.
\]

The recorded-construction recovery map is

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Recover}_{\rm counts}
\left(
\operatorname{Render}_{2f}
\left(n_{\rm post},n_{\rm route}\right),
\operatorname{Rec}_{2f}(\gamma_\Psi)
\right)
:=
\left(n_{\rm post},n_{\rm route}\right)
=
(2,1).
}
\]

These are exact tuple projections. They prove recovery from the rendered
count pair together with the carried construction record; no inversion of a
bare scalar is asserted. This instantiates the record discipline required by
active `GATE_009_001:L446-L460`.

## 6. Falsifiers

The lemma fails if:

1. two events replace the one event;
2. either face is absent;
3. the face tags are identified;
4. the two counts are combined;
5. either count cannot be recovered;
6. the event identity cannot be recovered;
7. a numerical magnitude or particle identity is assigned; or
8. observer output is written back into source data.

The independent witness must reject all eight corrupt modes.
