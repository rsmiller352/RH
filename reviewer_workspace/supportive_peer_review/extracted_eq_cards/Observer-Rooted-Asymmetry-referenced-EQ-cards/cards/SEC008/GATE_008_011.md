## 1) Plain language

The framework borrowed a checklist from the archive (DEF_TIME_003): a real time bridge must pin down nine things —
what it starts from, what time-variable it targets, its units, its calibration, what clock it's reading, where it's
valid, how it behaves relativistically, when it fails, and which way the dependence runs. The worry was that some
of these are still missing. They aren't. The promoted stack already answers all nine — but **at the structural,
unit-free level**: the start is the closure structure, the target is the closure-readout time reading, the units and
calibration are the observer-scale bridge, the clock is the clockhood *role*, the domain is the resolved-closure
regime, the failure is dropping below the resolution floor, and the dependence runs one way (internal → reading).

The ninth-of-the-tricky-ones is the seventh field, **relativistic status**. Read as a physics demand ("prove you
match relativity") it looks open. But this theory is meant to be **underneath** relativity, not measured against it
from outside — so the honest status is: *self-contained; nothing relativistic imported; comparison waits until we
derive it ourselves.* That **is** a status, and stating it **fills the field**. So the checklist is **structurally
complete**.

This card writes that down — and draws one hard line. Filling the field with "deferred" completes the
**specification**; it does **not** flip the switch that unlocks **measured** physics. Lorentz, time dilation,
measured rates, predictions, E=mc², gravity — all stay locked, exactly because the relativistic status is
*deferred, not derived*. The card certifies the structure is finished and **refuses** to claim any measured result.
It changes no other card; it predicts nothing; it opens nothing.
