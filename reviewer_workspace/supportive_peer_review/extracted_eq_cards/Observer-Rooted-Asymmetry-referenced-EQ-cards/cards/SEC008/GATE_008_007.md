## 1) Plain language

A bookkeeping correction to the **order of work**, exactly like the gate before it — but one rung lower.

When we built RO-D we gave the readout side its first real physical quantity: **time**. Time, here, is the
observer-system reading its completed closures and counting how much *turning* each one is worth — the
`2π` extent per closure. That reads one side of the closure: the **cycle/extent** side.

But the books have always had a second, equal-and-opposite side already written down. A closure has an
**extent** (that became time) *and* a **support** — the thing that lets one unit of existence close with **no
remainder**. Formal ground calls that support **mass**: `m_base = 1/(2π)`, the *conjugate* of the
time-extent, with `m_base·t_base = I`. Mass is not new and not undefined; it is time's reciprocal, sitting in
the same matrix row. And it matters next, because **energy** in this book is literally *mass spread across the
channels* (`E = m·Cap`), and the rule for building the energy bridge (OB-G) insists the time side and the
mass side be **kept separate** — you cannot define energy from the time chart alone and then "discover" mass.
So the mass side needs its own reading **before** we calibrate to units and **before** energy.

So RSM re-scoped: **read the support/mass side next, then calibrate, then do energy.** This card inserts one
new job — **RO-Dm**, the κ-free mass/support reading, the mirror of RO-D — between RO-D and the unit-bridge
RO-E, and fixes the order to `RO-C → RO-D → RO-Dm → RO-E → RO-F → RO-G`. It also widens RO-E's job: when we
finally attach units, we attach them to **both** readings (time *and* mass/support), not time alone.

Three cares, same as last time. The existing register cards are formal, so this gate does **not** edit them;
this new card records the change and supersedes **only** the one RO-D→RO-E step. This card **defines nothing**
— not the mass reading, not the unit bridge, not mass, energy, gravity, or any calibration constant; it only
fixes the order and writes down the grounded reasons. Two things it deliberately leaves alone: **gravity** — which the books pair with mass in the
bottom row `[m, G]`, so it is *related*, but where exactly it sits relative to energy is **left open**, with no
gravity job created here; and **units** — *seconds and the human/instrument way of reporting* are a **later,
non-fundamental convention**, not the physical quantity itself; physical time and the mass/support reading
exist before anyone reports them in seconds, and "human-scale" means *a relationship at a scale*, never a mind.
