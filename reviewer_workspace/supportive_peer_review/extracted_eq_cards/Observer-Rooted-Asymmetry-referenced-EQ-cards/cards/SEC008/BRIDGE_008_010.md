## 1) Plain language

RO-D gave the reader a **time** reading and RO-Dm the conjugate **support** reading; RO-E **calibrated** the two
together — one coupled, observer-scale way to report the pair, with their product (the reciprocal identity
`m_base·t_base = I`) preserved. This card is the **doorway to energy**.

It says: the **energy** of a closure is what its **support** looks like when you distribute it across the
channels — `E = m·Cap`, the support spread over the nine ordered routes (the "channel-distribution image"). That
is a pure book number (`9/(2π)`), carrying no `ħ` and no laboratory unit. To **report** it as physical energy you
cross a bridge: you name the bridge-side physical energy `E_phys`, and you relate it to the **rate** side of the
reading through Planck's constant — `İ = E_phys/ħ`, "information per unit physical time." The support side hands
over the magnitude `E`; the time/rate side hands over `İ`; **`ħ` is the bridge between them**. This is the **one**
place `ħ` is allowed in — and only on the bridge, never inside the book.

Three cares. **It consumes the whole calibrated pair, not half of it.** Energy is not "just the support" — the
support reading only means anything paired with its reciprocal time reading (that is what RO-E locked). So RO-F
sits on RO-E and takes the coupled pair as one object; the identity `m_base·t_base = I` survives. **The two sides
must come from genuinely different places (OB-G).** The rate side traces back to the *time* chart (RO-D), the
energy side to the *support/mass* chart (RO-Dm); if they secretly shared inputs, `İ = E_phys/ħ` would be circular.
They don't — RO-D and RO-Dm have disjoint roots — so the bridge is honest. One subtlety guards this: the energy
uses the **active** mass `m` (a value in its own right), **not** the base `m_base`, which is *defined* as
`I/t_base` and would smuggle the time extent back into the support side. Using `m_base` there would quietly make
the two charts share a root and break the honesty — so the card forbids that substitution outright (D-054). **It stops at the correspondence.** It
does **not** say `E = mc²` (that is a separate, later, carefully-guarded bridge), does **not** touch gravity, and
makes **no** measured prediction — no clock rate, no Lorentz, no dilation. Those wait. This card delivers exactly
one thing: *energy is the channel-distribution image of the calibrated support, related to the time/rate side by
`ħ` on the bridge.*
