## 1) Plain language

DEF_006_010 said *what kind of object* the coherence aperture A_Φ is — a two-faced object (an internal
floor below, a bridge-conditional ceiling above) with the middle left open. It did **not** prove the
edges fit together cleanly. This theorem does exactly that one thing: it proves the **three regimes
don't overlap** — every action-gap lands in **at most one** of "too small to resolve" (the floor),
"resolved, in the window" (the middle), or "so large the bridge says it washes out" (the ceiling).

Two honesties, both required by RSM. **First**, the bottom split is **sharp and entirely ours**: below
one unit there is no resolved distinction, at or above one unit there is — the boundary sits exactly at
|g_Φ| = 1 (the floor), with no physics in it. The **top** split is **not a sharp number** — we do
**not** invent a value where coherence ends. Instead the middle is **defined** as "resolved **and not**
in the bridge's washed-out regime," so it cannot overlap the ceiling **by construction** — no cutoff
needed, and the ceiling stays a borrowed, conditional clause (ħ never comes inside). **Second**, we do
**not** claim the regimes are each non-empty as a theorem; we only **exhibit examples** of each (a
half-unit gap below, a one-unit gap in the middle, a very-large symbolic gap above) and check them — an
example, not a hidden claim. And the middle stays **just a typed region**: we prove it doesn't overlap
its neighbours, nothing more — no coherence law lives inside it here.

## 4) Proof (the partition)

**Step 1 — the sharp internal floor split (R₁ | resolved), UNCONDITIONAL.** By THM_006_007 the
resolution floor is sharp at the unit: for |g_Φ| < 1 the action-gap carries **no resolved distinction**
(the record-absent / open-account predicate — never I = 0), and for |g_Φ| ≥ 1 the gap is **resolved**
with count I = ⌊|g_Φ|⌋ ≥ 1 (each completed unit one 2π closure, ROLEDEF_006_008). The two cases are
mutually exclusive and exhaust {|g_Φ| ≥ 0} by the trichotomy at the threshold 1 (|g_Φ| < 1 versus
|g_Φ| ≥ 1) — a **sharp, internal, unconditional** split. Write **resolved := {|g_Φ| ≥ 1}**, so
R₁ ∩ resolved = ∅ and R₁ ∪ resolved = {|g_Φ| ≥ 0}. (The boundary |g_Φ| = 1 is **consumed** from
THM_006_007, not invented here.)

**Step 2 — the bridge-conditional upper split (R₂ | R₃) by TYPED SET-DIFFERENCE.** By BRIDGE_006_009,
under (T) the declared u_Φ↔ħ translation and (I) the F-211-verified oscillatory estimate, a
far-supra-unit nonstationary gap lies **outside the coherent regime** — the suppression regime R₃,
entered via the **symbolic** marker K ≫ 1 (qualitative; **no numeric value**). Since a far-supra-unit
gap is in particular resolved (|g_Φ| ≫ 1 ⟹ |g_Φ| ≥ 1), **R₃ ⊆ resolved**. Define the **middle**
$$R_2 \;:=\; \text{resolved} \setminus R_3 \;=\; \{\,|g_\Phi| \ge 1\ \text{and}\ |g_\Phi| \notin R_3\,\}.$$
Then **R₂ ∩ R₃ = ∅ by the set-difference construction** — disjointness holds **without** any numeric
cutoff between them (the far-supra onset stays the inherited qualitative marker). This split is
**bridge-conditional**: R₃ is the inherited BRIDGE_006_009 regime, **never re-derived internally** and
**never makes ħ internal** (the u_Φ↔ħ correspondence stays the declared hypothesis).

**Step 3 — pairwise disjointness + exhaustiveness.** R₁ ∩ R₂ = ∅ since R₂ ⊆ resolved = ¬R₁ (Step 1,
sharp at 1). R₁ ∩ R₃ = ∅ since R₃ ⊆ resolved = ¬R₁. R₂ ∩ R₃ = ∅ by Step 2. Exhaustive:
R₁ ∪ R₂ ∪ R₃ = R₁ ∪ (resolved \ R₃) ∪ R₃ = R₁ ∪ resolved = {|g_Φ| ≥ 0}. Hence the three typed
regimes are **pairwise disjoint and exhaustive** — A_Φ is **well-formed** as a typed three-regime
object. **Disjointness is the typed mutual-exclusion predicate** (no |g_Φ| in two regimes — the
no-shared-member predicate, **never an empty-set-AS-object / I = 0**). ∎ (Step 1 unconditional; Steps
2–3, where R₃ enters, conditional on (T)+(I) of BRIDGE_006_009.)

**Non-degeneracy — WITNESSED at example-level, NOT a theorem claim (RSM tightening).** The proof above
establishes disjointness + exhaustiveness; it does **not** claim each regime is inhabited. Representative
members — R₁: |g_Φ| = 1/2 (sub-unit); R₂: |g_Φ| = 1 (resolved, and 1 < K so not far-supra under K ≫ 1);
R₃: |g_Φ| = K (the symbolic far-supra marker) — are **exhibited and checked at the witness/verifier
level** under the symbolic assumptions (§7), as **example-level non-emptiness**, never folded into the
theorem's load-bearing claim.

## 9) Notes

**(i) Why a THM, and the DEF/THM split (RSM Decision 1).** DEF_006_010 typed A_Φ (the two faces + the
open middle); RSM ruled the **disjoint-regime partition** is proof work and must not be hidden in the

**(ii) The RSM tightening — disjointness claimed, non-degeneracy witnessed.** The theorem's load-bearing
claim is **pairwise-disjoint typed regimes** (well-formed). **Non-degeneracy is NOT claimed** as a
theorem; representative members per regime are exhibited and proved at the **witness/verifier level**
under the symbolic assumptions (§7 check 2). This keeps the theorem from overclaiming inhabitation it
does not prove structurally — exactly the TD-010/TD-011 discipline (scope the claim; don't inflate).

**(iii) No sharp cutoff — the set-difference is the whole point.** The middle/far-supra disjointness is
**definitional** (R₂ := resolved \ R₃), so it needs **no** numeric boundary between coherence and
suppression. The far-supra onset stays the inherited **qualitative** marker K ≫ 1 (BRIDGE_006_009); a
sharp ≲O(1) value would need the u_Φ↔ħ bridge **built** (later-gated) and would breach the fence. The
paper's eq-9 "≲O(1)" is the **bridge-side target** (P11), never an internal cutoff.

and u_Φ-only**. The three-regime partition (introducing R₃) is **conditional on (T)+(I) of
BRIDGE_006_009** — R₃ is the inherited bridge clause, pointer-cited, never an internal theorem; ħ never
enters the internal split; u_Φ↔ħ stays declared. The type boundary between the internal floor split and
the bridge ceiling is preserved (DEF_006_010's mixed-typing, now proved disjoint).

**(v) Forward pointers (TD-020 — pointers only).** The **focusing map** (A#, ħ_eff = ħ/A#,
