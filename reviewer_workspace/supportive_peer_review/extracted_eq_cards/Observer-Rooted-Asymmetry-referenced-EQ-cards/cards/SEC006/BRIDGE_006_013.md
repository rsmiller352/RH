## 1) Plain language

The coherence aperture A_Φ is the *object* (the window of resolvable distinctness). This card builds
the *operation* that changes how finely you resolve through it: **focusing**. Focusing has one knob,
a number **A#** between 0 and 1: A# = 1 is full resolution, A# < 1 is coarser. The whole content is
this: focusing changes only the **resolution** — physically, it replaces the action quantum ħ by a
larger effective one, ħ_eff = ħ/A# — and it does **not** move anything. The events (the staircase
transitions, where closures register) stay exactly where they were; the action-difference ΔS between
two histories is **unchanged**. Only how sharply you read the phase rescales.

That "nothing moves" is the load-bearing claim, and we **prove** it rather than assume it. The proof
is a small, standard distributional fact (from the paper's §10.2): if you multiply a signal by a
constant, you change its *heights* but never the *locations* of its spikes. Focusing is exactly such
a constant multiplication (by A#), so the event locations are invariant — hence ΔS is invariant. Once
ΔS doesn't move and only the quantum rescales, the screen reading factors cleanly: **I_screen =
A#·I_bulk**. Because A# ≤ 1, this only ever **shrinks** the reading — focusing compresses, it never
grows anything (no new action, no new events). Two fences matter: ħ and ħ_eff live **only** on the
bridge to ordinary physics — our internal unit stays u_Φ and our internal count stays I = ⌊|g_Φ|⌋,
which doesn't care about resolution at all; and A# is a **secondary** knob — A_Φ remains the primary
object. The discrete-vs-continuous question (the ±1 phase step) is **not** here; that is S06.

**(The no-shift proof — the load-bearing NEW-WORK, RSM Decision 2; the §10.2 distributional fact.)**
Let the phase observable Φ have distributional derivative Φ̇ = Σ_n c_n δ(t − t_n) + r(t), with r
locally integrable and **singular support {t_n}** (the event/closure locations). Focusing by A# is
multiplication by the nonzero constant A#: Φ_screen = A#·Φ. Then, in the sense of distributions,
$$\dot\Phi_{\text{screen}} \;=\; A\#\,\dot\Phi \;=\; \sum_n (A\#\,c_n)\,\delta(t - t_n) \;+\; A\#\,r(t).$$
Scalar multiplication by A# ≠ 0 **rescales the coefficients** c_n → A#·c_n **but leaves the support of
each δ(t − t_n) unchanged** — the singular support is again **{t_n}** (a nonzero scalar multiple of a
distribution has the *same* singular support: it cannot create or remove a singularity, only rescale
its strength). Hence the **event locations are INVARIANT** under focusing (Δt = 0). With the dynamics
fixed (§2.5: focusing is a resolution change, "no modification of equations of motion"), the
action-difference **ΔS between the fixed event configurations is INVARIANT**. ∎ (bridge-side
distribution theory; no S06, no internal ħ, no built bridge — RSM Decision 2 satisfied.)

**(The factoring — Δ-linearity, PROVED from the no-shift.)** Δ is a linear difference operator on
action differences; ΔS is invariant (just proved); ħ_eff = ħ/A#. Hence
$$I_{\text{screen}} = \Delta(S/\hbar_{\text{eff}}) = \Delta(A\#\cdot S/\hbar) = A\#\cdot \Delta(S/\hbar) = A\#\cdot I_{\text{bulk}},$$
the aperture parameter factoring out because A# is a constant scalar and Δ is linear (eq 12→13). The
F-210 CONDITIONAL (Δ-linearity asserted) is thereby **DISCHARGED**: the factoring is **proved**, resting
on the no-shift (ΔS invariant) + the linearity of Δ, not assumed.

**(Compression — P6.)** 0 < A# ≤ 1 ⟹ I_screen = A#·I_bulk ≤ I_bulk: focusing **compresses** the
readout (no scalar growth; no new action/events/distinguishability — RSM P6 directive). eq 14
(ΔS = (ħ/A#)·I_screen) and eq 15 (𝒜⁻¹ = (1/A#)·I_screen) follow as the inverse readouts.
