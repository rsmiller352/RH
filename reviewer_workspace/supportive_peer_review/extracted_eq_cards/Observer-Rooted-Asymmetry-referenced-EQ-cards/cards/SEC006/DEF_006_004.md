## 1) Plain language

The previous card built an action 𝒮_Φ that accumulates along a history. To turn that action into
**information** — into a count of how distinguishable two histories are — we need a **unit**: the
smallest action difference that actually counts as *one* step of distinguishability. That unit is
**u_Φ**. The rule that fixes it is simple and not a free choice: **one unit of distinguishability
is one u_Φ of action difference.** Existence is counted in whole units, and the first is I = 1
(the existence count, THM_002_007); u_Φ is exactly the action gap that moves the count by one. So
u_Φ is not a knob we tune to fit anything — it is *defined by its job*: the minimal action
difference that can be told apart, the action-amount per one unit of distinguishability.

Its unit size is **strictly positive**, but its absolute display magnitude is a
**unit choice** — like deciding to measure in one ruler or a finer one. If you rescale the action
and u_Φ together, the **count** I = (action difference)/u_Φ does not change: you changed the ruler,
not the distinguishability. What is real is the **threshold** — one unit — not the decimal. (That
is the same unit-relativity the runtime account uses for its residual, DEF_005_005.) And u_Φ is
**ours, internal**: it is deliberately **not** Planck's ħ — connecting the two is a separate,
explicit bridge, never assumed (TD-019). This card fixes u_Φ's value content — strictly positive,
role-forced, unit-relative, internal — and nothing more.

role. u_Φ obeys it: its value is fixed by being the minimal admissibly-distinguishable action
