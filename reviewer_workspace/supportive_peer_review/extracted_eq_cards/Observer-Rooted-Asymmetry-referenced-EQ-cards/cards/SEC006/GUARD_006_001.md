## 1) Plain language

Two different things have been called "phase": a **continuous** phase (an action read in
aperture units, wrapping every 2π) and a **discrete ±1** orientation (a sign, σ). They are
**not the same object.** S05 only ever uses the **continuous** one. Whether the discrete sign
is somehow a coarse-grained shadow of the continuous phase — a quotient, a limit, a reduction
— is a real and interesting question, but it is **S06's** question (it needs the seam machinery
S06 has and S05 lacks), and **S05 must not quietly answer it in either direction.** The discrete
±1 reuses the existing orientation tag **σ** when S06 needs it; S05 mints no new sign glyph. This
card is the fence that keeps the two apart inside S05 and defers their reconciliation to S06.

## 2) Statement (the prohibition, stated once)

1. **Distinctness.** The **continuous coherence-phase** (the action read in aperture units,
   defined **mod 2π**) and the **discrete ±1 orientation/parity** (σ, DEF_002_002) are
   **distinct typed roles** with distinct symbols.
2. **S05 uses only the continuous phase.** Every S05 card's working phase is the continuous
   coherence-phase; the discrete ±1 is **not** an S05 working object (it is realized by σ at S06).
3. **No S05 reduction (the fence).** S05 makes **no claim** that the discrete ±1 is a
   **quotient, limit, reduction, or derivation** of the continuous phase. That reconciliation is
   an **S06 obligation (OB-D)**; S05 holds **no license** to decide it in either direction
   (TD-023). A reduction claim presented at S05 is rejected; the same claim is admissible only at
   S06, where the seam license lives.

## 5) Scope & non-overclaim

Fences the discrete↔continuous relationship inside S05 and defers its reconciliation to S06;

OB-D (S06); mint any glyph; read "phase" as physical phase (TD-019). Guard references: P9 (symbol

GUARD_001_006 (layer discipline). Referenced by: the S05 phase consumers (ROLEDEF_006_008, DEF_006_005)
and any downstream card reading the continuous phase.
