# Relational histories 𝓗(A,B) — the typed set of admissible relational walks from distinguishability event A to event B, instantiating the DEF_002_009 schematic walk step-type with a distinguishability step; the substrate the S05 action functional ranges over; the empty history is the empty-walk ε (a walk, never a zero-object)

## 1) Plain language

S05 is going to talk about "histories" — the ways one distinguishable thing relates to
another — and then measure them. Before measuring anything we have to say cleanly what a
history *is*. The framework already has the right object: a **walk** (DEF_002_009), a finite
chained sequence of relational steps. A history from A to B is just such a walk whose two ends
are distinguishability events A and B. So we do not invent a new kind of object; we **reuse the
walk** and call the set of all admissible walks from A to B the history set **𝓗(A,B)**. Two
things matter for what comes next. First, histories **chain**: a history from A to B followed
by one from B to C is a history from A to C — that is what lets the action functional add up
along a history (the next card). Second, the **empty history** — going nowhere — is the empty
walk **ε**. It is a perfectly good walk; it is **not** "zero," not an absence of existence,
not an I=0 object. That distinction is the very first brick of S05's floor discipline: in this
section nothing is ever a zero-object. This card lays down the history set and those two facts,
and nothing more.

## 2) Statement

> **(Histories as walks.)** For distinguishability events A, B (relation-tokens admitted by
> AX_001_001), the **relational history set**
> $$\mathcal{H}(A,B) \;:=\; \{\, w : w \text{ is an admissible relational walk from } A \text{ to } B \,\}$$
> where *walk* is the DEF_002_009 object instantiated with the **distinguishability step**
> (a relation-token traversal carrying an involutive reverse, chaining by concatenation —
> DEF_002_009 §2 scope clause; the THM_002_011 register-walk instantiation is the precedent).
> A *history* is an element γ ∈ 𝓗(A,B).
>
> **(Concatenation.)** For histories γ₁ ∈ 𝓗(A,B) and γ₂ ∈ 𝓗(B,C), the concatenation
> γ₁ ∘ γ₂ ∈ 𝓗(A,C) is the walk concatenation of DEF_002_009. 𝓗 is **closed under
> concatenation** at matching endpoints (the composability the action functional 𝒮_Φ consumes
> at DEF_006_003).
>
> **(The empty history.)** The empty history is the **empty walk ε** (DEF_002_009), the
> identity of concatenation: ε ∘ γ = γ = γ ∘ ε at matching endpoints. **ε is a walk, NOT a
> zero-object** — never the excluded zero state of IMPORT_001_007, never an I=0 existence value
> (P1, TD-026).

𝓗 is the S05 history-family glyph (registered here; well-formedness inherited from the walk

## 4) Well-formedness (DEF obligation)

- **Admissibility is the instantiated walk admissibility (GATE-2).** A history is an admissible
  walk under DEF_002_009's schematic step-type, instantiated with the distinguishability step
  (a relation-token traversal with an involutive reverse, chaining by concatenation). The step
  type carries no structure beyond reversal and chaining (DEF_002_009 §2), so the
  instantiation is licensed exactly as THM_002_011's register-walk instantiation is — no new
  well-formedness obligation beyond the walk's. Endpoints are distinguishability events
  (AX_001_001); a candidate whose endpoints are not such events, or that is not an admissible
  walk, is not in 𝓗(A,B).
- **Concatenation is well-defined and closed at matching endpoints.** Walk concatenation
  (DEF_002_009) is associative with identity ε; restricting to matching endpoints (B = the
  shared event) gives γ₁ ∘ γ₂ ∈ 𝓗(A,C). This is the composability DEF_006_003's
  concatenation-additivity consumes; it is a property of the walk structure, inherited.
- **The empty history is ε, a typed walk — never a zero-object.** ε is the empty step
  sequence (DEF_002_009 §3): the identity of concatenation, a walk. It is explicitly NOT the
  excluded zero state (IMPORT_001_007) and NOT an I=0 existence value (P1). This is the base
  case of the S05 floor-zero discipline (D-055.C): "no history" is the empty walk (a record of
  a trivial walk), not an absence-of-existence object. (The *resolution* sense of "no resolved
  distinction" — the floor — is THM_006_007's, expressed as a record-absent predicate, also
  never a zero-object; pointer only.)
- **No content beyond the typing.** No action value (DEF_006_003), no aperture unit u_Φ
  (DEF_006_004), no gap Δ (DEF_006_005), no metric/distance. Reading any of those off this card
  is out of scope (TD-020 forward pointers only).
