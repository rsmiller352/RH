## 1) Plain language

Everything S05 declares is strictly positive, and the aperture layer owns no zero to declare. The
action of a history is a strictly-positive amount — or, for the empty history, simply "no increment"
(a typed fact, not a stored zero). The resolution unit u_Φ is strictly positive. The gap between two
histories is a strictly-positive magnitude with an orientation — or, when the two actions match,
"they balance, no excess" (a typed fact, not g_Φ = 0). And where the old work read the resolution
floor as "I = 0" — two histories too close to tell apart — this book reads "no resolved distinction
on record; the record is absent." This card is the aperture-layer fence that makes the ground rule
(stated once at S01) bite here, so a stray zero anywhere in S05 is caught. "I = 0" may appear only as
external readout-absence language describing what is NOT on the books — never as a thing inside them.
