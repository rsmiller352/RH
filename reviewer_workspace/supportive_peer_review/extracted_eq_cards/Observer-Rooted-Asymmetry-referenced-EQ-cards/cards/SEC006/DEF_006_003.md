## 1) Plain language

S05 measures histories. The thing it measures with is an **action** — a positive amount that
accumulates as a history is traversed, the way a tally grows step by step. We write the action
of a history γ as **𝒮_Φ[γ]**. Three facts pin it down. First, action **adds along
concatenation**: the action of "do γ₁, then γ₂" is the action of γ₁ combined with the action of
γ₂ — that is what makes it a faithful running tally. Second, action is a **strictly positive**
quantity and it only ever **adds** (the framework has no negative numbers — P3); what standard
physics writes as an action *difference* between two histories is a transfer, handled later (the
gap card), never a minus sign here. Third, the **empty history** has **no action increment** —
and "no increment" is a typed fact (the identity of the adding operation), **not** a stored
"zero." Just as the empty history itself was the empty walk ε and not a zero-object
(DEF_006_002), its action is the no-increment predicate, not the number 0.

Two careful namings. The unit 𝒮_Φ is measured against is the **internal** resolution unit
**u_Φ** — the smallest action increment that can be told apart — reserved earlier (its actual
size is the next card's job). And 𝒮_Φ is **our internal action**, deliberately **not** the
physicist's classical action S and **not** built from Planck's ħ: those live only on the
bridge, and connecting our 𝒮_Φ to them is an explicit, separately-gated step — never an
assumption (TD-019). This card lays down the action functional, its adding rule, its
positivity, and the empty-history predicate, and nothing more.

## 2) Statement

> (DEF_006_002),
> $$\mathcal{S}_\Phi : \mathcal{H}(A,B) \;\longrightarrow\; \mathcal{D}^{+}_{\text{act}} \qquad (\text{strictly-positive action quantity, } \mathcal{D}^{+}\text{ action-tagged, DEF\_S01\_005; TD-009}),$$
> assigning to each **non-empty** history γ a strictly-positive action holding 𝒮_Φ[γ]. The
> output is a **single accumulated internal-action amount** (a scalar READOUT in the
> COMMUTATIVE 𝒟+, matching DEF_002_001) — **not** an order-tracking action log. 𝒮_Φ[γ] **may
> depend on** the ordered structure of γ, but the order and relational structure are **carried
> by the input history** 𝓗 (the order-bearing object, DEF_006_002), **not erased** by, and
> **not re-exposed in**, the scalar output value (RSM gate 2026-06-14). "Scalar" means scalar
> OUTPUT, **never** scalar GROWTH (the P6 guard, §5 / §9(iv)).
>
> **(Concatenation-additivity.)** For concatenable histories γ₁ ∈ 𝓗(A,B), γ₂ ∈ 𝓗(B,C),
> $$\mathcal{S}_\Phi[\gamma_1 \circ \gamma_2] \;=\; \mathcal{S}_\Phi[\gamma_1] \,\oplus\, \mathcal{S}_\Phi[\gamma_2],$$
> where ⊕ is the licensed **positive composition** of 𝒟+ (DEF_002_001) — a strictly-positive,
> **commutative**, associative, identity-free combination (the accumulated total is
> order-independent **as a value**; the order lives in the input history, not the readout).
> **No signed term, no subtraction** (P3).
>
> **(The empty-history action — a typed predicate, never a 0 value.)** For the empty history
> ε (the empty walk, DEF_006_002), 𝒮_Φ[ε] is the **no-increment predicate** — the typed
> identity of ⊕ in the sense that 𝒮_Φ[ε ∘ γ] = 𝒮_Φ[γ] = 𝒮_Φ[γ ∘ ε] holds **as a predicate
> over the composition**, NOT by storing a value 0 for 𝒮_Φ[ε]. (𝒟+ is identity-free by
> signature, DEF_002_001; "no action increment" is the absence/identity predicate, the
> floor-zero discipline carried from DEF_006_002 — D-055.C, P1, TD-026.)
>
> **(The resolution unit — typed, not valued here.)** 𝒮_Φ's increments are measured against the
> **internal resolution unit u_Φ** (DEF_006_004) — the minimal admissibly-distinguishable
> action increment. u_Φ's VALUE CONTENT (strictly positive, role-forced, unit-relative) is DEF_006_004 and the typed gap Δ in u_Φ units
> is DEF_006_005; here u_Φ enters only as the typed comparison unit (TD-020/TD-009).
>
> **(Internal — fenced from bridge S and ħ.)** 𝒮_Φ is the **internal** action functional. The
> registry's plain **S** ("Classical action along a history") is **bridge-side** and is NOT
> re-typed; physical **ħ** is bridge-side; **ℏ/ℏ_eff are never used internally**. Any 𝒮_Φ↔S or
> u_Φ↔ħ identification is admitted ONLY across an explicit, later-gated BRIDGE card (P7;
> TD-019, the D-055.A3 u_Φ↔ħ fence in parallel).

𝒮_Φ is introduced as a new registry glyph (D-056.GATE-3, RSM-chosen; ASCII fallback

## 4) Well-formedness (DEF obligation)

- **The codomain is the strictly-positive action quantity (P3/TD-009).** 𝒮_Φ takes a non-empty
  history to a 𝒟+ element tagged "action" (DEF_002_001's strictly-positive, identity-free
  semigroup; the tag per TD-009). Strict positivity is by type: a non-empty history carries a
  genuine, strictly-positive action increment. No signed value is admissible (P3).
- **Concatenation-additivity is well-defined and uses the licensed positive composition.**
  ⊕ is 𝒟+'s associative, **commutative** positive composition (DEF_002_001); additivity over
  concatenable histories (matching endpoints, DEF_006_002's concatenation closure) is therefore
  well-typed, associative, and commutative — the accumulated action total is order-independent
  **as a value** (the order lives in the input history 𝓗, not the scalar readout; RSM gate
  2026-06-14). This is the composability the gap (DEF_006_005) and the floor (THM_006_007)
  consume.
- **The empty-history action is the no-increment predicate, never a 0 value.** 𝒟+ is
  identity-free by signature (DEF_002_001): there is no 0 element to assign. 𝒮_Φ[ε] is the
  typed predicate "no action increment" — it satisfies the identity LAW over ⊕ (𝒮_Φ[ε∘γ] =
  𝒮_Φ[γ]) as a predicate on the composition, not by storing a value. This is the floor-zero
  discipline (D-055.C) carried up from DEF_006_002's empty-history-is-ε-not-zero; it is the same
  discipline as DEF_002_001's identity-free 𝒟+ (an emptied register leaves the book; here, the
  empty history contributes no increment, as a predicate).
- **u_Φ enters as a typed unit, not a value (TD-020/TD-009).** 𝒮_Φ's increments are typed
  against u_Φ (the minimal distinguishable increment, DEF_006_004-reserved); the card uses the
  unit's TYPE/role, not its value (DEF_006_004) — well-typed because the unit is reserved and
  the increment is u_Φ-comparable; the actual value and the gap-in-u_Φ-units are later cards.
- **The internal/bridge fence is by typed role (TD-019).** 𝒮_Φ is internal
  (distinguishability-typed); bridge-S and ħ are distinct typed roles (bridge). The fence is
  role-driven: 𝒮_Φ ≠ S and u_Φ ≠ ħ as typed roles, independent of any value coincidence; only
  an explicit BRIDGE card licenses an identification (P7). (The DEF_006_004 u_Φ↔ħ fence pattern,
  verified value-independent there at F-201.)
- **No content beyond the typing.** No u_Φ value (DEF_006_004), no gap Δ (DEF_006_005), no floor
  (THM_006_007), no metric. Reading any of those off this card is out of scope (TD-020).
