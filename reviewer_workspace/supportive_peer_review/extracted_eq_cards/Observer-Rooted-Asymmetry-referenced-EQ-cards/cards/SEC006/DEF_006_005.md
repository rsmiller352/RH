## 1) Plain language

Information is about how far apart two histories are *in action*. So we need the **gap** between
two histories' actions — and we have to build it without ever writing a minus sign, because the
framework has no negative numbers (P3). Here is the honest way. Take the two action amounts
𝒮_Φ[γ₁] and 𝒮_Φ[γ₂]. One is at least as large as the other; the amount by which the larger
**exceeds** the smaller is a genuine, strictly-positive quantity — the **excess** (that is what
the cone order ⪰ already gives us, DEF_002_001). That excess is the gap's **magnitude**. Which
one was larger is the gap's **orientation** — a tag (σ), not a sign. So the gap is **orientation
× magnitude**, never a subtraction. We then measure the magnitude against the resolution unit
u_Φ: divide (only at the final readout) to get a plain dimensionless number, **g_Φ**. That number
is the raw distinguishability between the two histories; the next cards floor it to a whole count
(the information I) and decide that below one unit nothing is resolved (the floor).

One more careful point, the same one that runs through all of S05: if the two actions are **equal**,
there is **no gap** — and "no gap" is a **typed fact** (the two balance, with no excess left over),
**not** the number zero. We prove balance by **pairing** the two, not by subtracting them to get 0
(TD-026). Nothing in this section is ever a zero-object.

## 4) Well-formedness (DEF obligation)

- **Orientation × magnitude, the P3 transfer-form (no subtraction).** When the two actions differ,
  the cone order ⪰ (DEF_002_001) supplies a UNIQUE strictly-positive excess e (the amount the
  larger exceeds the smaller: (smaller) ⊕ e = (larger)); this is the magnitude source, NOT a
  subtraction (DEF_002_001's order is defined by the existence of e, the P3-licensed replacement
  for "−"). The orientation σ (DEF_002_002) tags which history is larger — a relational reading
  tag, NOT a sign (no tag arithmetic, GUARD_001_005). Well-formed: the gap is (σ, e/u_Φ), built
  with no minus.
- **The no-difference case is the paired-balance predicate, never g_Φ = 0.** When the two actions
  match, there is no excess (e does not exist as a strictly-positive holding — 𝒟+ is identity-free,
  DEF_002_001); "the two actions balance" is the typed PAIRED-BALANCE predicate, proved by pairing
  the holdings (TD-026/F-179: pairing, never Σ = 0), NOT a stored g_Φ = 0. This is the floor-zero
  discipline (D-055.C/D-043) at the gap layer, carried from DEF_006_002 (empty history = ε) and
  DEF_006_003 (empty-history action = no-increment predicate).
- **The u_Φ-ratio is at coefficient-readout level (the 01a_004 discipline).** |g_Φ| = e/u_Φ is
  formed at the final readout, NOT inside the typed quantity construction (no division of typed
  quantities mid-construction — TD-009; the archive's own discipline, "No division occurs inside
  the typed construction). The result is a dimensionless magnitude.
- **Dimensionless + unit-relative (inherited from u_Φ).** Under 𝒮_Φ ↦ c·𝒮_Φ, u_Φ ↦ c·u_Φ (c > 0),
  the excess e ↦ c·e and |g_Φ| = e/u_Φ is invariant — the gap is the unit-relative readout, not the
  scale (DEF_006_004 / DEF_005_005 / TD-028).
- **No content beyond the gap.** No floor (THM_006_007), no count I = ⌊|g_Φ|⌋ (THM_S05_007/008), no
  aperture object (S05.9), no metric, no phase. Reading any off this card is out of scope (TD-020).

## 9) Notes

**(i) The g_Φ glyph — the Δ-family avoidance (P9/TD-019), flagged for the gate.** The archive and
the assembly write the gap as **Δ** (Δ := π_S(𝒮[γ₁]−𝒮[γ₂])/π_S(ℏ)). But the registry already holds
a Δ-family — **ΔS** (action difference, BRIDGE — physical), **Δφ** (relative phase, BRIDGE),
**ΔI** (the S04 unit posting) — and the ΔI registration note records "no bare Δ is registered."
A Δ-prefixed internal gap glyph would visually near-collide with **Δφ** (a phase difference) and
risk a TD-019 confusion with the **bridge** action-difference ΔS — a one-symbol-near-two-meanings
hazard (P9). So the internal action-gap is registered under the clean, distinct glyph **g_Φ**
("gap", house-style subscript Φ, matching u_Φ / 𝒮_Φ / A_Φ; no prior g in the registry). This is a
builder substitution forced by the registry state (the Γ → 𝓗 pattern, DEF_006_002); surfaced for
RSM to confirm g_Φ (or pick another glyph) at the per-card gate. The framework's "Δ(𝒮_Φ/u_Φ)"
notation maps to g_Φ.

**(ii) The GAP-S05-5 P3 repair.** The best-graded archive gap piece (01a_004) defines the gap
through a signed subtraction of action readouts — the read-campaign itself flagged it as needing
transfer-form restatement (the P3 hazard, scope package §6). This card is that repair: the gap
magnitude is the cone-order EXCESS (DEF_002_001's ⪰ — a strictly-positive 𝒟+ holding, the
P3-licensed replacement for "−"), the orientation a σ-tag (DEF_002_002); no subtraction is
performed. The absolute value the old floor used (⌊|Δ|⌋) already discarded sign — direct evidence
the relational content is magnitude + orientation, so the transfer-form restatement is faithful,
not an imposition.

resolution floor (sub-unit |g_Φ| < 1 ⟹ no resolved distinction, a record-absent predicate never
