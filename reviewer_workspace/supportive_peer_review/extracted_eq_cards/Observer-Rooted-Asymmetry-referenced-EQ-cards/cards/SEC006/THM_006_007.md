## 1) Plain language

Two histories are told apart by how far their actions differ — the gap g_Φ, measured in resolution
units u_Φ. The floor is the simple, riskily-falsifiable claim at the heart of the aperture: **if the
gap is less than one unit, the two histories carry no resolved distinction.** They are not yet
"one thing apart"; nothing is posted to the books. Crucially — and this is the whole point of the
rebuild — "nothing posted" does **not** mean "the count is zero." There is no zero on the books. The
account is simply **open**: no completed unit yet, the record absent. (The old archive cards wrote
this as "I = 0"; that is exactly the defect this card repairs — F-178.)

And when the gap **does** reach a whole unit? Then one unit of distinguishability has **completed** —
and completed existence cannot go unaccounted (that is the persistence law, LEM_002_013). So the
posting is not a choice; it is **forced**. Exactly one completed unit-token posts (the paired
bookkeeping, THM_002_012), and because existence is counted in whole units of one (THM_002_007), that
posting is **ΔI = 1** — never declared, always forced by the completion. The total count is just how
many whole units the gap has crossed. This is the very same escapement the runtime layer (S04)
already proved; here it is rebuilt from the same first principles at the aperture layer, where the
"ticks" are units of distinguishability rather than runtime cycles.

## 4) Proof / Construction

The two claims are the floor (I) and the forcing (II); the count (III) reads them off.

**(I — the floor: sub-unit ⟹ no resolved distinction, record-absent never I = 0.)** A resolved unit
of distinguishability is, by definition of the unit u_Φ (DEF_006_004; the minimal
admisibly-distinguishable action difference, forced as the action-quantum per ΔI = 1), one whole u_Φ
of action-gap. A sub-unit gap |g_Φ| < 1 has **not** completed one unit: there is no completed-unit
token to post. By GUARD_006_006 (the aperture-layer strict-positivity / no-floor-zero fence,
instancing GUARD_001_008), the absence of a completed unit is the **record-absent / open-account
predicate** — NOT an internal I = 0 value (a zero booking would assert the existence of nothing, a
Law-of-Existence violation, IMPORT_001_007 via GUARD_006_006). Hence below the unit no resolved
distinction is posted, and the state is the open account, not a zero. (The risky content, F1: this is
a falsifiable claim — a persistent operational readout that distinguishes a sub-unit pair refutes it.)

**(II — the forcing: completion ⟹ ΔI = 1, by account completion, NOT declared.)** Suppose the gap
reaches a whole unit (a completed crossing). Then one unit of distinguishability has **completed** —
a completed existence. By **LEM_002_013** (the Persistence Corollary: existence persists; admitted
account-status is conserved under licensed operations — non-erasure), a completed unit **cannot vanish
unaccounted**: it must be posted. So the posting is **forced** (not optional — the escapement cannot
"choose" not to post once the unit completes; TD-028). By **THM_002_012** (the Law of Symmetry
Conservation: every licensed operation balances — the forced paired posting / double-entry), the
forced posting admits **exactly one** completed unit-token. By **THM_002_007** (the First Existence
Count: existence is counted in completed units, I = 1 the first), the magnitude of that one
completed-unit posting is **ΔI = 1**. The value is **not pre-stated** ("the quantum must be 1") and
**not declared** (a codomain) — it **FOLLOWS** from the completion of one unit being accounted
(non-circular: completion + non-erasure + paired posting + the unit, each independent S01 content).
This is the **THM_005_009 account-completion forcing re-grounded at the aperture layer** on the same
S01 roots (α, D-056.GATE-1) — DEF_S04_023/THM_S04_019/GUARD_S04_016 are the proven runtime
**precedent / equivalence-check**, named, not consumed as a premise.

**(III — the count reads off the completions.)** The count I(γ₁,γ₂) is the number of completed
unit-crossings of the gap as it accumulates from the open account: each completed crossing forces a
ΔI = 1 posting (II), so I = the number of completed crossings = ⌊|g_Φ|⌋ as the **external**
completed-crossing readout (D-050: ⌊·⌋ counts whole posted units; the internal object is the gap
accumulator |g_Φ|, which may be irrational and never "reads 0" — a sub-unit gap is the record-absent
predicate of the count, not I = 0). I ∈ ℤ≥1 once ≥ 1 unit completes. Unit-relativity is inherited
