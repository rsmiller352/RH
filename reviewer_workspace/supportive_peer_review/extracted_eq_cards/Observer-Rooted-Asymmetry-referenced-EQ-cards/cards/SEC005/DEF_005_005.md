# The unit account and the residual ratio — the runtime residual is a UNITIZED RATIO measured against the unit account I = 1 (R_acc = current residual / unit threshold); the account states R<1 (positive residual holding, not completed existence) / R=1 (one completed accountable unit) / R>1 (≥1 completed unit must post + carry); the five-point account-completion doctrine (D-049) given a load-bearing card home

## 0) Governance & Scope Lock

R_acc (the residual measured against the unit threshold), and the three account states

## 1) Plain language

Existence is counted in whole, completed units, and the first such unit is what the book
calls I = 1 — there is one completed thing rather than nothing. A leftover that has not yet
reached a whole unit is not a little independent piece of existence sitting on its own. It is
a balance in an account, and it only means anything when you measure it against the unit that
completes — against one. So this page does not talk about "a residual of 0.3" as if 0.3 were
a thing. It talks about a ratio: how far the leftover has filled toward one whole unit. Write
that ratio R_acc. If you switched to a smaller measuring unit the decimal would move — 0.3
might read as 30 of some finer tick — but nothing about existence would have changed; you
changed the ruler, not the world. What is real is the threshold and where the account stands
relative to it. Three standings: below one, the account holds a positive leftover but has not
completed a unit of existence yet (R<1); exactly at one, the account has completed one whole
accountable unit (R=1); above one, at least one whole unit has completed and must be posted,
with whatever is left carried forward (R>1). The next page (THM_005_009) shows that reaching
one FORCES the book to post — because completed existence cannot go unaccounted. This page
just lays down the account, the ratio, and the three standings the forcing rests on.

## 2) Statement

**(a) The unit account (P1, consumed — not declared).** The unit of the runtime account is
the first completed accountable existence,

$$I = 1 \qquad (\text{AX\_S01\_001; the unit threshold}),$$

consumed from S01 as the GROUND. Zero is not existence (IMPORT_001_007): there is no account
state below the boundary; the account begins at the unit. (Doctrine points 1 and 3.)

**(b) The residual ratio (the residual is a UNITIZED RATIO).** For a runtime residual held in
the register S_res (DEF_005_003, strictly positive when present), the **residual ratio** is

$$R_{\text{acc}} \;=\; \frac{\text{current residual}}{\text{unit threshold}} \;=\; \frac{S_{\text{res}}}{I} \;=\; S_{\text{res}} \quad (I = 1),$$

a unit-relative quantity, NOT a free-floating real decimal. The printed decimal of S_res is
**scale-dependent display** (choosing a smaller unit moves the decimal point without changing
existence — D-049); the **invariant** is R_acc, the leftover relative to the unit that
completes. (Doctrine point 4: residuals are relational leftovers measured against one.)

**(c) The three account states (the completion partition).** The account stands in exactly one
of three states, decided by R_acc against the unit threshold:

- **R_acc < 1 — positive residual holding, NOT completed existence.** A strictly-positive
  leftover (R_acc > 0, register present, DEF_005_003) that has not reached a whole unit. This
  is not a zero-inclusive interval on a declared quantity: the lower end is the
  register-absence predicate (no residual), the upper end the completion threshold. (Doctrine
  point 2: less-than-one is not completed existence.)
- **R_acc = 1 — one completed accountable unit.** The residual has reached the unit threshold:
  one whole unit of existence has completed. (Doctrine point 3 / point 5 trigger.)
- **R_acc > 1 — at least one completed unit must post, leftover carried forward.** The residual
  has crossed the threshold: ≥ 1 whole completed unit must be posted and the remainder (itself
  a strictly-positive sub-unit residual, R_acc − ⌊R_acc⌋ when present) is carried forward. (Here
  ⌊R_acc⌋ is the EXTERNAL completed-crossing count of DEF_005_006 — the number of whole units
  posted — NOT an internal floor that can read zero; the internal object is the irrational residual
  accumulator, and "when present" guards the no-residue case as register-absence, never a stored
  R_acc − ⌊R_acc⌋ = 0 — D-050 / TD-028.)
  (Doctrine point 5: when residual reaches one, the book must close by posting an event-token —
  the FORCING is THM_005_009's.)

**(d) Completion is counted in whole units (D-043 deepened).** Existence is counted in
completed units of I = 1 (P1); the unit/integer structure of the count is grounded in
persistence/non-erasure (a completed unit must be accounted — LEM_002_013, the Persistence
Corollary), NOT declared. A completed unit is
exactly one whole unit; the count of completed units is the integer part of R_acc. (This is
the ground THM_005_009 forces ΔI = 1 from; F-182's "integrality is fiat" is DISSOLVED here —
integrality follows from existence being counted in completed units, not from a declared
codomain.)

## 4) Well-formedness (DEF obligation)

- **The unit is the ground, not a declaration.** I = 1 enters as AX_001_001's existence
  ground (P1), consumed upstream — this card declares no value; it types the account around
  the consumed unit. Zero is excluded by IMPORT_001_007 (the account has no sub-boundary
  state). Well-formed: the threshold is the consumed unit, the least completed existence.
- **R_acc is a unit-relative ratio, not a real decimal.** R_acc = (current residual)/(unit
  threshold) is dimensionless relative to I; with I = 1 it equals the held residual S_res
  numerically, but its TYPE is the ratio (the unit-relative leftover), not a standalone real.
  The unit-relativity is a well-formedness property: under a change of the measuring unit by
  a positive factor c (residual ↦ c·residual, threshold ↦ c·threshold), R_acc is invariant —
  the decimal display of S_res moves, the account state (R<1/=1/>1) does not. (TD-028; D-049
  "you changed the measuring unit," not existence.)
- **The account states partition the ratio exactly.** R_acc < 1, R_acc = 1, R_acc > 1 are
  mutually exclusive and exhaustive against the unit threshold (an exact comparison, the
  completion gate). R<1 is strictly positive (register present) or the register-absence
  predicate (no residual) — never a held zero (DEF_005_003 / GUARD_001_008). The threshold
  predicate R_acc ⪰ 1 is the boundary comparison (scope-convention item 2), not a declared
  zero codomain.
- **No value, no dynamics, no forcing.** This card types the account and the ratio; it assigns
  the per-cycle residual its value at DEF_005_007 (ω_cell = Ω_pack), runs the update at
  DEF_005_011, and FORCES the posting at THM_005_009. Reading any of those off this card is
  out of scope.
