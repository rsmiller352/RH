# The source ↔ +1-cell bookkeeping relation — the +1 co-end cell is the OPPOSITE-SIDE ι_or co-reading of the ONE NON-ERASED symmetry-paired entry whose source-side reading is the unit OUT of S_res; the cell CONTINUES that entry (it IS the entry, read from the co-end), NOT a newly detached object. The count-side (event count, THM_005_010) and the cell-side (THM_005_020) of the co-end are ONE entry read two ways. Typed residual HISTORY (reading b) DEFERRED

## 0) Governance & Scope Lock

Theorem card; types the bookkeeping relation **forced** between the source (the unit OUT of the
residual register `S_res`) and the **+1 co-end cell**. The escapement discharge is **one** transfer
instance — one ι_or-pair (`THM_005_010`): the unit OUT of `S_res` is the **source-side reading**, and
the **+1 cell is the opposite-side (co-end) reading of that SAME instance** (`DEF_002_010`: ι_or
swaps the reading, the instance datum is untouched). **Non-erasure** is what makes this a
**continuation** and not a destroy-then-create: the −Δ is the re-grounded non-erasing transfer
(`THM_005_010` §2b; `DEF_002_004` "no zero value is written"). So the +1 cell **is not a newly
detached object** — it is the same non-erased, symmetry-paired entry read from the co-end. This
**resolves the seam**: the count-side ("the event count (ΔI=1)", `THM_005_010`) and the cell-side
("= one cell", `THM_005_020`) of the co-end are **one entry read two ways**, not two objects.
Banned: any DETACHED +1 cell (a distinct instance); any ERASURE (a zero written / the datum changed
across the swap); any typed residual-HISTORY (reading b, DEFERRED); any cross-cell / multi-cell
composition (`LEM_010_003` OPEN) or Ω↔D (D-030.Q6); any location / coordinate (`THM_005_020`); any
temporal language; any "conservation" framing (RSM 2026-06-18); any physical bridge (D-060); editing
any promoted card. Layer ceiling S04. Cited as the source↔+1-cell co-reading relation and for
nothing else (TD-011).

## 1) Plain language

At an escapement, the running residual completes a unit and that unit appears as a new **cell**.
This page asks the bookkeeping question: *what is that cell, relative to where it came from?*

It is **one event written down twice**. The discharge is a single transfer: one strictly-positive
unit leaves `S_res` (the source-side reading), and the very same transfer, read from the other side,
**is** the +1 cell (the co-end reading). Reading it from the co-end does not make a new thing —
*"the reading swaps; the instance datum is untouched."* And nothing is rubbed out: no "−1" is ever
written as an erasure; the unit that left the source is the same unit the cell now reads. So the +1
cell is **not a fresh, detached object that merely happened after** the residual completed — it is
that completion's entry, read from the opposite side, **continued, never erased**.

That settles a small puzzle. One card calls the co-end "the event count going up by one"; another
calls it "one cell." Both are right, and they are not two things to reconcile: they are the **same
single entry read from two sides** — the count-side and the cell-side of one co-end.

*(What this page does **not** say: that the cell stores a record of how the residual built up — that
"history" question is left open; and nothing here about how cells' books combine, or where the cell
is, or any time. And it speaks of no "conservation" — only the symmetry-paired, non-erased reading.)*

## 4) Proof (complete)

**Step 1 (one instance, two readings).** By `THM_005_010` §2b the escapement discharge posts **one**
oriented transfer — "ONE oriented ι_or-pair — the source-end reading and the target-end reading of a
single instance". By `DEF_002_010` §2c, ι_or maps the source-side reading to the co-end reading of
the **same** instance: `ι_or⟨σ_src,A,B,q⟩ = ⟨σ_tgt,A,B,q⟩`. So the source-side (unit OUT of `S_res`)
and the co-end are the two oriented readings of **one** instance `t = ⟨A,B,q⟩`, not two instances
(`THM_002_012`: nothing unpaired; `DEF_002_002`: two readings per oriented relation pair). ∎(1)

**Step 2 (non-erasure).** `DEF_002_010` §2c: across the swap "the reading swaps; **the instance
datum is untouched**." `DEF_002_004` §2: at the source, "**no zero value is written**" — and the
−Δ is re-grounded as the non-erasing transfer (`THM_005_010` §2b; the apple-form gloss "taken from
Alice and given to Bob", `THM_005_010` §1). Hence the source entry is **not erased and re-created**;
the one datum `⟨A,B,q⟩` persists, read from two sides. ∎(2)

**Step 3 (the +1 cell is the co-end reading, not a detached object).** By `ROLEDEF_005_018` /
`DEF_002_004` §9 / `THM_005_020`, the +1 cell is the unit-of-existence completed at the discharge —
i.e. the **co-end (INTO) side** of the one transfer (`THM_005_010` §2c names "the +1 cell as
co-end"). Combining Steps 1–2: the +1 cell is the **opposite-side ι_or co-reading of the same
non-erased instance** whose source-side reading is the unit OUT of `S_res`. It therefore **continues**
that entry — a co-reading of one non-erased datum — and is **not** a separately-created, detached
object. (A +1 cell carrying a *different* instance than the source-side reading would be an unpaired
/ mismatched record, a symmetry-pairing violation under `THM_002_012` — `THM_005_010`'s falsifier.) ∎(3)

**Step 4 (the seam resolved).** `THM_005_010` types the INTO-end as "the event count (ΔI=1)";
`THM_005_020` types the completed unit as "= one cell." By Steps 1–3 these are the **count-side and
cell-side of the one co-end reading** of the single instance — one entry read two ways, **not** two
objects and **not** a double posting. The forward pointer `DEF_002_004` §9 carried ("the +1 cell as
co-end … not grounded by S04, TD-020") is hereby grounded at S04. ∎(4)

**What this proof does NOT use / claim:** no typed residual-HISTORY (reading b deferred — `S_res` is
the current partial holding); no cross-cell / multi-cell composition (`LEM_010_003` OPEN); no Ω↔D
(D-030.Q6); no location / coordinate (`THM_005_020`); no temporal object / no "rate"; no
"conservation" framing (RSM); no physical (D-060); no signed arithmetic (the +1 cell is the P3
transfer co-end, `DEF_002_004` §9; TD-034).

## 8) Falsifiers

Mode A), **F-sb2** (erasure: a zero written / datum changed — load-bearing, Mode B), **F-sb3**

content is asserted (F-sb4). Loss handling per the D-042 protocol: the consumed promoted facts
(`DEF_002_010`, `THM_002_012`, `THM_005_010`, `DEF_002_004`, `THM_005_020`) held; a violating
construction (a detached cell, an erased entry, an unpaired record) is the suspect.
