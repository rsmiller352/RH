## 1) Plain language

*(This section carries the RSM-ruled EXTERNAL ANALOGY — explicitly marked, per TD-034;
every accounting term is translated in place. The claims and proof are §2/§4's, in the
internal idiom.)*

**[MARKED EXTERNAL ANALOGY — accounting; each term translated.]** In double-entry
accounting, almost every number lives inside one statement: the P&L records the period's
activity *(translation: the flow view — the cycle's closure activity)*, the balance sheet
records what stands *(translation: the stock view — the ledger's holdings)*, and each
entry pairs with its counterpart on its own statement — different sides of the same
thing. But ONE number crosses: **net income**, computed on the P&L and posted into the
balance sheet — the same fact appearing in both statements. The statements are said to
**articulate** through it *(translation: two statement-positions of one fact)*. The
crossing has its own unique entry type — the **closing entry** *(translation: the
feed-admission — the per-tick arrival of the share)* — and passes through a special
account, **Income Summary**, which exists only to carry the crossing and holds nothing
afterward *(translation: the shortfall position, which clears every cycle)*.

The runtime's Ω is that number. Each cycle, the cell's closure comes up short by exactly
the void share — the part of the cell the sphere never fills. That shortfall is the
cycle's "net income": a fact produced by the flow (this cycle's closure under-filled by
Ω) and posted into what stands (the register receives ω_cell — the same quantity, by the
promoted identity that binds the runtime valuation to the geometric share). The
admission's booking — the question G3 asked — is exactly this crossing record: not a
transfer (nothing moved from anywhere; no account was debited), but one fact written
from its two statement-positions: geometry fell short; the book grew. And the shortfall
side keeps nothing: next cycle's closure produces its own share, cleanly. That is why
the book can grow without anything being created from nothing and without conservation
being touched: conservation governs transfers, and this is not a transfer — it is the
way existence's own under-fill enters the ledger, the same entry mode by which the very
first content was admitted.
