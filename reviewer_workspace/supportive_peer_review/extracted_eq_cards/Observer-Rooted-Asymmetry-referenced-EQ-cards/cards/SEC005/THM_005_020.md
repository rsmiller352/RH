## 1) Plain language

Two corrections to the earlier version, which lumped a forbidden thing with a forced thing — and one re-housing.

**Cells each keep their own leftover, and that's fine (O1).** When an escapement fires, one cell completes; each cell carries its own running leftover (it carries over, never resets). Many leftovers is **not** many existences — the cells are **one** existence because they're **connected**: every child is born as the other end of its parent's own transfer, so it's tied to the parent, and through the parent to the whole. The only thing that *would* be a second existence is a cell floating free, connected to nothing. (So "one existence" never meant "only one leftover"; it meant "all connected, nothing free-floating.")

**That connection is what binds them — not "gravity," not "one register" (O2).** The binding is just the chain of child-tied-to-parent relations. (Gravity, which will later settle all this leftover at the whole-system level, is *not* here — we haven't gotten there.) And it **is** a pairwise tie (child to parent) — but a *relational* one, not the forbidden "everything sits touching on a grid" the earlier draft rightly killed.

**A child has a place — relative to its parent (O3).** We now have a separate foundational page (`LEM_002_006`) that says *where* anything is, is just its relations — no background grid, ever. A lone thing has no "where"; a child has its parent to relate to, so the child is **next to its parent, relationally**. Forced. But "next to" means the relation, not a spot on a grid — no coordinate, no 3D. (A 3D picture is a later, optional readout.)
