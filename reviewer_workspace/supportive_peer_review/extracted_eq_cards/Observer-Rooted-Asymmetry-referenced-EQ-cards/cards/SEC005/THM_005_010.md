## 1) Plain language

A tick fires exactly when the running residual, plus this cycle's share Ω, reaches a whole unit
of existence — and when that happens the book has NO CHOICE but to fire, because a completed
unit of existence must be recorded (the Law of Existence; the previous page, THM_005_009, proves
the forcing). So the discharge is not just "defined to happen at a threshold"; it is FORCED at
completion. When it fires, one strictly-positive unit leaves the residual register and one
strictly-positive unit enters the event count: one event, written down twice, from the two
ends — the apple taken from Alice and given to Bob. That double-entry is exactly one balanced
pair, and this page proves it balances — not by saying "balanced" as a wordplay, but by showing
the discharge is a single instance of the ground symmetry law (every allowed move posts a paired
record). If anyone ever proposed a discharge that took a unit out without putting one in (an
unpaired record), the law would not bend: the proposed move would be the suspect — wrong, or
missing its partner entry (the new cell), or old-math drift — never the law.
