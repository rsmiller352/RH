## 1) Plain language

There is one escapement, told two ways: as a step-by-step recurrence (each cycle adds a share and
maybe discharges) and as a one-shot operator (the floor of a running product). This page proves the
two tellings agree when the run starts from nothing held — the tick count and the residue come out
the same either way. It also carries, with its proof obligation named openly, the link to the
scheduler's phase-step count (the number of scheduler steps after K updates is K plus the tick
count) — the corpus's one bridge between update rate and phase rate, which the later sections will
use. The general starting-residue version and that phase link are honest open obligations, not
claims dressed as proofs.

## 2) Statement

(Native runtime scale; ω_cell = Ω, strictly positive; N_tick(x) = ⌊x·Ω⌋ from DEF_005_007.)

**Clause 1 (recurrence ≡ operator, empty-history start — PROVED).** At the empty-history start
(S_res(0) the empty-history predicate — no residue held; the re-typed S(0), no zero):

$$T_{\text{rt}}(K) = N_{\text{tick}}(K) = \lfloor K\cdot\Omega \rfloor, \qquad S_{\text{res}}(K) = \operatorname{frac}(K\cdot\Omega),$$

i.e. the recurrence's tick count and held residual EQUAL the operator twin's. The two presentations
(recurrence form and operator form) are the same (count, residual) type — ONE escapement.

**Clause 2 (general-S(0) phase clause — CARRIED, NAMED obligation GAP-S04-A).** For a general
strictly-positive start residue S_res(0) below the unit, T_rt(K) = ⌊S_res(0) + K·Ω⌋ and
S_res(K) = frac(S_res(0) + K·Ω) (the phase-shifted operator form, from #10). The proof that this
coincides with the recurrence under the general start is the named obligation (GAP-S04-A
(ii)) — carried, not asserted as discharged here.

**Clause 3 (the PhaseSteps linkage, PC-013 — CARRIED, NAMED obligation GAP-S04-A).** The scheduler
phase-step count after K updates is

$$\text{PhaseSteps}(K) = K + T_{\text{rt}}(K) = K + \lfloor S_{\text{res}}(0) + K\cdot\Omega\rfloor,$$

the same (count, residual) type — the corpus's only update-count↔phase-step linkage (PC-013),
carried HERE as the operator identity (S06-FIRST: S04 proves the operator identity, S06 consumes it
as the phase-rate seam). Its general proof is the named obligation (GAP-S04-A (iii)).

## 4) Proof (clause 1 complete; clauses 2–3 carried conditional)

**Clause 1 (PROVED).** At the empty-history start, S_res(0) is absent (no residue held); the
accumulated input over K cycles is K·Ω (DEF_005_007's u_tick(K) = K·Ω). By the floor-sum keystone
(THM_005_015, with S_res(0) the empty-history start): T_rt(K) = ⌊K·Ω⌋ and S_res(K) = frac(K·Ω).
By DEF_005_007, N_tick(K) = ⌊K·Ω⌋. Hence T_rt(K) = N_tick(K) and S_res(K) = frac(K·Ω) — the
recurrence and operator presentations agree (same count, same residual). ∎(1)

**Clause 2 (carried — GAP-S04-A (ii)).** For a general start S_res(0), THM_005_015 already gives
T_rt(K) = ⌊S_res(0) + K·Ω⌋ and S_res(K) = frac(S_res(0) + K·Ω). The remaining obligation is the
induction showing the recurrence's step-by-step output coincides with this phase-shifted operator
form for every K under the general start — a small finite induction (GAP-S04-A; the
ESCAPEMENT_RESIDUAL_THEOREM 10-line induction is the seed). Carried, not discharged here.

**Clause 3 (carried — GAP-S04-A (iii)).** PhaseSteps(K) = K + T_rt(K) is the exact form (PC-013);
the obligation is to show it composes as Q∘u_tick with the step count (the phase-rate seam). Carried
as the operator identity (S06-FIRST); the general proof is the named obligation.
