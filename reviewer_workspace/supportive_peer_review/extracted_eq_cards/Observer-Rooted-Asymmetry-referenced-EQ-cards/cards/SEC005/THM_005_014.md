# Accounting identity as a symmetry-balance instance (W-008 kin) — S_res(0) ⊕ k·ω_cell = S_res(k) ⊕ Σⱼ[posting j]; accumulated input PAIRS with held-residual + completed-discharge readings (a THM_002_012 instance, proved by PAIRING never subtraction); F-102 INLINE

## 1) Plain language

Over any run of cycles, the books balance — and they balance by pairing, not by subtracting to
zero. What went in (the start residue plus all the per-cycle shares) is matched, reading for
reading, by what is held now plus every unit that has been discharged. Nothing is left
unpartnered. This is not a fresh conservation law: it is the ground symmetry law (every allowed
move posts a paired record) read over the runtime history. The old work wrote it as a
telescoping subtraction that cancels to zero; that aggregate form is weaker — it would allow
hidden pairwise violations that still summed to zero. Pairing is the honest, stronger statement.

## 2) Statement

(Native runtime scale; ω_cell = Ω, strictly positive, DEF_005_007; all readings strictly
positive.)

**The accounting identity (a symmetry-balance instance of THM_002_012).** For every cycle history
of length k from a start state S_res(0):

$$S_{\text{res}}(0) \,\oplus\, k\cdot\omega_{\text{cell}} \;=\; S_{\text{res}}(k) \,\oplus\, \bigoplus_{j=1}^{k} [\text{posting } j],$$

where every term is a strictly-positive reading: the **accumulated input** (start residue ⊕ k
per-cycle shares) PAIRS with (**held residual** S_res(k) ⊕ **completed-discharge** readings
Σⱼ[posting j]). Balance is proved by PAIRING — each accumulated-input reading has its co-reading
in (held residual ⊕ completed discharges) — a single instance of THM_002_012's reachability
balance over the runtime history; NOT a standalone telescoping, NOT a subtraction to zero
(F-179 note A: aggregate-zero is provably weaker — it admits pairwise-violating assignments).

## 4) Proof (complete, INLINE — F-102, re-expressed PAIRING-first)

**Step 1 (the history is a licensed generation).** The cycle history (S_res(0); cycle 1, …,
cycle k) is a finite sequence of licensed operations: each cycle accumulates ω_cell (a
strictly-positive holding, DEF_002_001) and, when the threshold is crossed, executes the
discharge transfer (THM_005_010 — a single admissible transfer, one ι_or-pair). So the runtime
history is a licensed generation in the sense of THM_002_012 §3.

**Step 2 (balance by pairing — the THM_002_012 instance).** By THM_002_012 (b), the record state
of a licensed generation is ι_or-balanced: every posted reading sits beside its co-reading. Read
over this history: each accumulated-input reading (a per-cycle share ω_cell added to the running
residual) is paired with its co-reading in (the held residual S_res(k) carried forward ⊕ the
completed-discharge readings Σⱼ[posting j]). Conservation of the pooled total (LEM_002_005,
consumed through THM_005_010's discharge transfers) gives the equality of totals; the symmetry
law (THM_002_012) gives that the equality is realized by PAIRING, reading-for-reading, not by
aggregate cancellation. The bounded residual (LEM_005_013) guarantees S_res(k) is a strictly-positive
held reading (when present) or register-absent (on exact discharge) — never a zero term.

**Step 3 (the identity).** Summing the per-cycle accumulations: the start residue plus k shares
equals (by the conserved-total balance, paired) the currently-held residue plus the units that
have left via discharges. In transfer form (no subtraction): S_res(0) ⊕ k·ω_cell and
S_res(k) ⊕ Σⱼ[posting j] are the two paired readings of the same conserved total. ∎

**Why pairing, not telescoping (F-179 note A).** A telescoping "S(0) + kω − S(k) − ΣΔ = 0" is an
aggregate-zero form, which admits pairwise-violating assignments summing to zero (provably weaker).
The pairing form (each reading beside its co-reading) excludes those — it is the THM_002_012 content,
and it carries no subtraction and no signed term.

**What this proof does NOT use:** no standalone telescoping; no subtraction to zero; no signed
object; no new symmetry card (THM_002_012 is instanced, not re-proved); no PC-008/010 as ground
(the L05-family sweep GAP-S04-N is OWED — those are motivation only); no Noether-physical reading
(Route B's W-008 reserved).
