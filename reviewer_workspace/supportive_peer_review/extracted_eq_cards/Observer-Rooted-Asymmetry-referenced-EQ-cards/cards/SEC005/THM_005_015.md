# Floor-sum keystone — T_rt(K) = ⌊S_res(0) + K·ω_cell⌋ and S_res(K) = frac(S_res(0) + K·ω_cell); proof INLINE from the accounting identity (#9) + the bounded residual (#8); T_rt(K) a count READOUT

## 1) Plain language

Here is the runtime in one clean formula. After K cycles, the count of whole units of existence
that have COMPLETED is just the whole-unit count of (the starting leftover plus K times the
per-cycle share), and the leftover still held is the unit-relative ratio of that same running
account. This is not real-number arithmetic on a free-floating decimal (D-049): the whole part
is a COUNT — how many whole unit-accounts have completed by cycle K (counted against the unit
I = 1) — and the leftover is a RATIO — how far the account has filled toward the next whole unit,
measured against one. You do not have to step through the recurrence cycle by cycle — the closed
form gives both at once. "No tick by K" means no whole unit-account has completed on record yet
(the empty-history reading), not "a count of zero."

## 2) Statement

(Native runtime scale; ω_cell = Ω, strictly positive; S_res(0) strictly positive below the unit
or the empty-history start.)

**The floor-sum keystone.** For every cycle count K:

$$T_{\text{rt}}(K) \;=\; \big\lfloor\, S_{\text{res}}(0) + K\cdot\omega_{\text{cell}} \,\big\rfloor, \qquad S_{\text{res}}(K) \;=\; \operatorname{frac}\!\big(\, S_{\text{res}}(0) + K\cdot\omega_{\text{cell}} \,\big),$$

where:
- **T_rt(K)** is the **COUNT OF COMPLETED UNIT-ACCOUNTS by cycle K** — how many whole units of
  existence have completed (counted in units of I = 1, P1 — DEF_005_005); a COUNT READOUT (∈ ℤ≥1
  when a unit-account has completed; "no tick by K" is the record-absent predicate, never
  T_rt(K) = 0). This is account completion, not the floor of a real number (D-049/TD-028);
- **S_res(K)** is the **UNITIZED RESIDUAL RATIO held at cycle K** — the leftover relative to the
  next unit, R_acc against the unit account I = 1 (DEF_005_005); strictly positive when present
  (frac > 0) or register-absent on exact crossing (DEF_005_003), never S_res(K) = 0, and never a
  free-floating real decimal (the printed decimal is scale-dependent display, the ratio the
  invariant).

## 4) Proof (complete, INLINE)

By the accounting identity (THM_005_014), the accumulated input S_res(0) ⊕ K·ω_cell equals (by
PAIRING) the held residual S_res(K) ⊕ the completed discharges Σⱼ[posting j]. Each discharge posts
exactly one completed unit-account (THM_005_010), so Σⱼ[posting j] is exactly T_rt(K) completed
unit-accounts (units of I = 1). Therefore

$$S_{\text{res}}(0) + K\cdot\omega_{\text{cell}} = S_{\text{res}}(K) + T_{\text{rt}}(K)\cdot 1.$$

By the bounded residual (LEM_005_013), the held residual ratio S_res(K) is strictly between the
completed-unit boundary and the next unit (present case) — i.e. frac(S_res(0) + K·ω_cell) lies in
(0,1) as the unitized ratio against I = 1 — or the register is absent (exact crossing, the
integer-valued case where the account closes on a whole unit). Since T_rt(K) is the integer COUNT
of completed unit-accounts and the residual reading is either the strictly-positive unitized ratio
in (0,1) (present case) or absent (exact crossing — the whole sum closes on an integer count of
completed units, the ratio is the no-residue predicate, not a stored 0), the decomposition
S_res(0) + K·ω_cell = (completed-unit count) + (unitized residual ratio) is the UNIQUE
completed-unit/residual-ratio decomposition (DEF_005_006; the unit account / ratio typing,
DEF_005_005). Hence
T_rt(K) = ⌊S_res(0) + K·ω_cell⌋ and S_res(K) = frac(S_res(0) + K·ω_cell). ∎

The proof uses only #9 (the pairing identity), #8 (the bound), and DEF_005_006's uniqueness — no
decimal, no signed object, no telescoping-into-zero. The exact arithmetic and the displayed
identity are unchanged from the floor-math statement; only the GROUNDING is re-described as
account completion (D-049/TD-028): ⌊·⌋ counts completed unit-accounts, frac is the unitized ratio.
