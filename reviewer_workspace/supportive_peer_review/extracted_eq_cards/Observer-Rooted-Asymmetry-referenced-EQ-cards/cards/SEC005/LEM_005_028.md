## 1) Plain language

The runtime's entire state is one number: the sliver currently held in the register. This
page proves that number is a complete fingerprint of the past. Because the share Ω is
irrational, no two distinct cycle counts ever leave the same sliver — if they did, their
difference times Ω would be a whole number, which the previous page ruled out. So reading
the sliver exactly determines exactly how many cycles have been serviced and how many
ticks have posted. Nothing about the past was written down anywhere, and nothing needed to
be: the books satisfy "the past must be accounted for" by POSITION, not by archive. That
also explains, after the fact, why the runtime's state was honestly designed as a single
number — the design is viable precisely because the sliver never repeats. The contrast
makes the point sharp: were the share a plain fraction, the slivers would cycle and the
count would be recoverable only up to the period — history genuinely lost. One irrational
number keeps existence running (the previous pages) and remembering (this one).

## 4) Proof (complete)

**Step 1 (clause (i)).** Suppose frac(K·Ω) = frac(J·Ω) with K > J ≥ 1. Equal fractional
parts mean the two accumulated totals K·Ω and J·Ω lie a whole number apart — i.e.
(K−J)·Ω ∈ ℤ with K−J ≥ 1. By LEM_005_027(ii) no positive integer multiple of Ω is a
whole number. Contradiction. ∎(i)

**Step 2 (clause (ii)).** By THM_005_016 the native state at cycle K is State_rt(K) =
(frac(K·Ω)); by Step 1 the map K ↦ frac(K·Ω) is injective, so the state value determines
K uniquely; N_tick(K) = ⌊K·Ω⌋ (DEF_005_007) and the completed-unit record follow as
functions of K. The determination is a readout of the standing state (DEF_005_012's
ledger → state → record → readout direction respected — nothing flows backward from a
past-object, because none exists); no archive object is constructed. ∎(ii)

**Step 3 (clause (iii)).** For a rational share p/q (gcd(p,q)=1): frac((K+q)·p/q) =
frac(K·p/q + p) = frac(K·p/q) — the sequence is periodic with period dividing q, so the
state value determines K only mod that period. (An exact-arithmetic fact about the
surrogate; the witness exhibits it — the contrast is the lemma's own can-fail surface.)
∎(iii)

**What this proof does NOT use:** no property of Ω beyond LEM_005_027's clauses; no
general start; no observer/aperture content; no stored history object; no temporal
premise (K is book-order).
