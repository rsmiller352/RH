## 1) Plain language

We already know — proved, at the bookkeeping layer — that nothing *closes and persists* unless at least
**three** places are involved: with one or two, every closed trip is an exact out-and-back and the book
erases it (`THM_002_011`). This card takes that fact and names it: **connectivity is just the count of
the points a persisting closure needs**, and that count is **three**. The trips between the points
(the arrows) are how the closure *shows up* — they witness it — but the thing we *count* is the
**points**, and closure forces three of them.

Two things make this the honest derivation the earlier attempts never were. **First, no space is
assumed.** Every previous "why three" secretly leaned on already living in 3D (a 2D plane "has a
normal", so you "need a third axis") — which assumes the answer. Here the three is forced by a pure
counting fact about returning trips (an out-and-back has even length; the smallest genuine loop,
A→B→C→A, has length three and cannot be an out-and-back), so it owes nothing to space. **Second, the
"fourth" is honestly later.** Three points close the loop; a **fourth** point is what lifts it out of
the plane into 3D and is what an **observer** reads — and existence only *means* anything once there is
a relationship that reads it. That fourth belongs to the readout layer; we point at it, we never use it
to argue the three. (And "two places joined by two different arrows" is not a thing the theory allows —
the ground model has exactly one connection per pair of places; the closure proof already settled the
two-place case.)

And the reason we count the **persisting** loop, not just any loop, is **non-erasure** (the
2026-06-20 unification): existence has to leave a mark that **cannot vanish** — an exact out-and-back
is booked as if it never happened, so it realizes no persisting existence. So "must persist" is the
*requirement* (non-erasure), and "the smallest loop that persists needs three places" is the *count*
(the closure floor) — the same shape as mass, where non-erasure forces the value and a structural fact
gives the form. Connectivity, mass, and time all bottom out in the one fact that existence persists.
