# M_exist — the fundamental object: the 3×1 existence column [I, τ, m]

## 0) Governance & Scope Lock

Definition card: assembles the first column of the book from already-defined entries.
Exact symbolic forms only. Every entry's value carries its provenance card — nothing is
asserted as derived where it is not. No geometry, no later columns. Layer ceiling S02.

## 1) Plain language

The book grows one column at a time, and this is the first column — the existence column.
It holds the three quantities the previous cards put on the table: the unit of existence
I, the closure cycle τ, and the servicing density m. It is a column, not a matrix: the
first matrix appears only when the second column arrives. Nothing new is computed here;
this card is the act of binding the three entries into one object.

## 2) Statement

$$M_{\text{exist}} = \begin{pmatrix} I \\ \tau \\ m \end{pmatrix}, \qquad
I = 1,\quad \tau = 2\pi,\quad m = \frac{1}{\tau} = \frac{1}{2\pi}$$

**The FUNDAMENTAL object — a 3×1 column, not a matrix** (D-025.R7 revised). Entry
provenance (designations/imports, exact): I = 1 ← AX_001_001 (axiom); τ = 2π ←
DEF_003_002 (forced-inherited from THM_002_014's t_base, D-064; formerly a declared convention); m = 1/(2π) ← DEF_003_003 (definitional identity,
derived clause exact). Cross-entry identity carried: m·τ = I (DEF_003_003).

Objects: M_exist (3×1 column of typed entries — typed before any arithmetic, TD-009);

- **Non-emptiness:** the column is exhibited with all three entries bound to existing
  formal objects; no entry is open.
- **Entry-status honesty:** the column ASSEMBLES; it does not upgrade. I remains
  axiom-grade, τ forced-inherited-grade (THM_002_014 t_base, D-064; formerly convention-grade), m definition-grade with exact derived value — each
  status readable from the provenance list in §2 (registry statuses live on the entry

- **Shape:** 3×1 by D-025.R7 revised; the one-column-at-a-time law (D-023.1) makes the
  next object 3×2 (DEF_003_015), never this card's concern beyond the pointer.
- **Why three rows:** the row count is the count of the three booked quantities of the
