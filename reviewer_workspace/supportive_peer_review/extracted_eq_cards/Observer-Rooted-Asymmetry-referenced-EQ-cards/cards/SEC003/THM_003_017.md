# The M_base top row is the invariants — booked-TO, distinguishing nothing (FORCED). I is the neutral existence count/balance: I=1 is forced (THM_002_007), and service τ and support m book TO it (m·τ = I, DEF_003_003). c is the fair channel count: c=3 is forced (THM_003_012), with no channel invariantly distinguished (LEM_003_010). The quantities beneath the top row (τ, m, and the composites) are the service and support that book to the invariants — the two oriented sides of each. The SAME holds for η (S03) and S_flow (S07, read via the clockhood role), carded at their layers.

## 0) Governance & Scope Lock

States, positively, what the top row of `M_base` (`DEF_003_015`) **is**: the invariants —
booked-**to**, distinguishing nothing. `I` is the neutral existence count/balance (`THM_002_007`;
`DEF_003_003`, `m·τ = I`); `c` is the fair channel count (`THM_003_012`; `LEM_003_010`, no channel
invariantly distinguished). The quantities beneath the top row are the service (`τ`) and support
(`m`) that book to the invariants. Grounded only on the five forced sources
(`THM_002_007`, `DEF_003_003`, `THM_003_012`, `LEM_003_010`, `DEF_003_015`). Computes no quantity. Layer S02 (η, S_flow are same-characterization forward
instances, carded at S03/S07).

## 1) Plain language

The top row of the book's first matrix (`DEF_003_015`, `[I, c]`) are the **invariants**. The
quantities underneath — `τ` (service), `m` (support), and the composites they build — are the
**two oriented sides** that book **to** those invariants. Read each top entry:

- `I` is the **existence count/balance**. `THM_002_007` fixes `I = 1` (the forced count). And `I`
  is what service and support book *to*: `DEF_003_003` books `m·τ = I` — mass, carried over one
  full closure, services exactly one unit of existence. So `I` is booked-**to**, the balance the
  double entry meets; it is not itself one of the booked quantities. It singles out nothing.
- `c` is the **fair channel count**. `THM_003_012` fixes `c = 3`. And the channels are *fair*:
  `LEM_003_010` proves no channel is invariantly distinguished by the rules — rename the three
  channels any of the 48 ways and flip their reading directions, and the rules (which use only the
  count three and symmetric distribution) say the same thing about all three. A count that singles
  out no channel distinguishes nothing.

So the top row are the invariants: the neutral count/balance `I`, the fair count `c`. The service
and support beneath them are what get booked; the invariants are what those bookings *balance to*.
And this is *why it matters*: because no channel is distinguished, the downstream readout
`(c−1)/c = 2/3` comes out the same no matter which cell observes — it is forced and
observer-independent (`THM_010_013`, `LEM_010_011`), and the Koide target `K_c = 2/3` stands
(`GATE_015_002`). A distinction on a top invariant would single out a channel and break that.
(That S07 chain is *why* fairness matters — motivation, **not** a dependency of this card.) The
same reading of `η` (S03) and S_flow (S07, read via the clockhood role) gives the same answer — so the characterization is
uniform.

  distinguished by the rules (`LEM_003_010`). `c` singles out no channel.

## 4) Proof

**Step 1 (what an invariant is here).** A top-row entry is an **invariant** iff its own forced
card fixes it as a value the rules single nothing out about, and the service/support quantities
book **to** it (it is booked-to, not booked). Show this of `I` and `c`. ∎(1)

**Step 2 (`I` — the neutral existence count/balance).** `THM_002_007 §2`: "`I = 1` … the '=1'
coefficient is the **FORCED content of this card**" — `I` is the forced existence **count**. And
`I` is booked-**to**: `DEF_003_003 §2` books the servicing identity `m·τ = I` (defining sentence:
"Mass is the density that, carried over one full closure, services exactly one unit of existence"),
so service `τ` and support `m` are the two oriented sides that meet at `I`. `I` is the balance the
double entry books to; it is not itself a booked/distinguished quantity, and it singles out
nothing. So `I` is an invariant (Step 1). ∎(2)

**Step 3 (MAKE-OR-BREAK — `c` — the fair channel count).** `THM_003_012` derives `c = 3` (the
minimal persisting-closure register count, `THM_002_011`). And the channels are fair:
`LEM_003_010 §2(c)` proves "no channel is invariantly distinguished by the rules" — the S02 rule
set is `G₃`-equivariant (order 48; "any channel can be carried to any other"); its statements
"use only the count c and symmetric distribution, never an individual channel label" (§2(c)). So the fair count `c`
singles out no channel; it distinguishes nothing. So `c` is an invariant (Step 1). ∎(3)

**Step 4 (∴ the top row is the invariants).** By `DEF_003_015` the top row is `[I, c]`; by
Steps 2–3 each is a forced value the rules single nothing out about, booked-**to** by the
service/support beneath. So the top row is the invariants; the quantities beneath (`τ, m`, and
their composites) are the service and support that book to them — the two oriented sides of each.
The same characterization (Step 1) — a fair/neutral invariant distinguishing nothing — applies to
`η` (fair 2-of-3 edge) and S_flow (read via the clockhood role) at their layers. ∎(4)

**Why it matters (named-forward, non-load-bearing).** Because no channel is distinguished, the
readout `(c−1)/c = 2/3` is the same for any observing cell — forced and observer-independent
(`THM_010_013`; `LEM_010_011 §2(5)`: "either twin as the reference gives the same (c−1)/c"), and
the Koide target `K_c = (c−1)/c = 2/3` stands (`GATE_015_002`). A distinction on a top invariant
would single out a channel and break that observer-independence. Cited as motivation, **not**
consumed (S07 is downstream; forward edge barred).
