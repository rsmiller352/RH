# Mass-time-units readout rule — book quantities reach units-bearing claims only through declared BRIDGE maps

## 0) Governance & Scope Lock

Prohibition card (GUARD): states the readout firewall, once; referenced, never restated
(TD-013). Never load-bearing.

## 1) Plain language

The book's m and τ are bookkeeping values — a density and a cycle label on the book's own
scales. The moment anyone wants to say what they amount to in kilograms, seconds, or any
measured unit, that sentence is a translation — and translations happen only on bridge
cards that state their map and what the map loses. There is no shortcut from the book to
the laboratory.

## 2) Statement (the prohibition, stated once)

The values of m and τ (and every quantity built from them: E, the M_base entries, the
future bottom-row residual content) are **book quantities** on the native M_base row-role scales
(service/support/invariant, THM_003_018). Any claim attaching measured units, laboratory magnitudes, or empirical
calibration to them must route through a **declared BRIDGE map** (P7): an explicit
translation card stating which book object maps to which conventional object and what is
lost or approximated. No internal card states a units-bearing readout.

**Exemption:** none needed — the rule IS the routing; BRIDGE cards are its licensed exit.

## 5) Scope & non-overclaim

Fences readout claims only. Does not prohibit the S08-deferred empirical program (it
defines its doorway); does not adjudicate which BRIDGE maps will exist. Referenced by:
DEF_003_003 §5, DEF_003_014 §5, ROLEDEF_003_011 §5.

## 6) Per-symbol accounting

Local: none. Imported: m, τ ← DEF_003_003 / CONV_S02_002 (the fenced objects).
Undeclared: none.

## 7) Verification

Not applicable — structural prohibition. Mechanical support: validator conventional-
notation and TD-019 scans; promotion checklist item 6 (notation discipline).

## 9) Notes

Empirical execution is deferred (D-023.9: "the theory needs to ride on its own first");
falsifiers still ride on cards per P0 — this guard keeps the eventual empirical contact
honest by making every readout pass through a map that can be audited.
