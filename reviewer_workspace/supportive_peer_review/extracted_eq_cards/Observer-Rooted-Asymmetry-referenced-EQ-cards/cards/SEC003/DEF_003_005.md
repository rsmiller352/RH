# c := the count of connectivity channels = 3 (exact premise; minimality open, fairness the official framing)

## 0) Governance & Scope Lock

Definition card: stipulates one count and types it. Pre-metric layer: no distance,
length, metric, or speed notion may appear in any positive role. The fairness DERIVATION
is not on this card (sufficiency: LEM_003_010; minimality: reserved THM_003_012). Layer
ceiling S02.

## 1) Plain language

The book's quantity can be distributed along exactly three channels of connectivity —
that is what c = 3 says, and it is ALL it says here. The channels are not directions in a
space (there is no space yet), and c is not a speed (there is no distance to be fast
across). What is proved, and where: that three channels can be perfectly fair — no
channel singled out by the rules — is the next card's small theorem; that three is the
SMALLEST fair count is an open target, reserved until it is actually proved.

## 2) Statement

$$c := \#\{\text{connectivity channels}\} = 3$$

— the channel labels are written x, y, z as NAMES (card-local labels, not metric axes;
nothing about them is geometric at this layer).

**Pre-metric typing (TD-019):** no distance exists at this layer, so c cannot be a speed
by construction. **Exponent notation:** c², c³ and the like abbreviate **iterated
channel-distribution** — apply "distribute across the 3 channels" two (three) times, a
count of applications, never a power of a velocity.

**Status:** exact premise — stipulated, with the fairness framing official (D-030.Q2):
sufficiency proved at LEM_003_010; minimality OPEN, reserved as THM_003_012 (D-026.Q4:
the gap closes only when that THM exists).
