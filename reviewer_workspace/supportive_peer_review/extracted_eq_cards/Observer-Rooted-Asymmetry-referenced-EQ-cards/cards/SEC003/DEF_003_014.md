# E := m·Cap = 3·3·m = 9/(2π) — mass distributed across the channels, twice

## 0) Governance & Scope Lock

Definition card: stipulates the E-form and carries its exact value clause. Exact symbolic
forms only — π symbolic (P8). Channel-distribution semantics ONLY: no velocity language,
no physics identification anywhere on this card. Layer ceiling S02.

## 1) Plain language

Take the servicing density m and distribute it across the three channels — then do it
again. Every ordered route of the two distributions counts once: nine routes, so
E = 9·m = 9/(2π). That is all E is at this layer: a booked distribution total. It is
written E because the later BRIDGE work will ask whether this object relates to what
physics calls energy — but that is a question for the bridge section to earn, not a
reading this card makes. And the "squared" in the familiar-looking shape is two
APPLICATIONS of "across three channels," not any velocity multiplied by itself.
