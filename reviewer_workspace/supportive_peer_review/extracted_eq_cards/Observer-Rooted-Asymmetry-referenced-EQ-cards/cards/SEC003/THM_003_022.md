# Capacity-account instantiation — pointwise paired multiplicity realizes G as the closure-side completion

or reproving, the promoted pointwise symmetry law, the paired relation

`j in J`. Instantiate the promoted two-sided co-reading on

\[
\mathcal V_J=J\times\{m\text{-side},\tau\text{-side}\}

\[
\iota_J(j,m)=(j,\tau),
\qquad
\iota_J(j,\tau)=(j,m).
\]

\[

\qquad
\operatorname{mult}_{m}(j)
=\operatorname{mult}_{\tau}(j)
=1
\quad(j\in J).
\]

Let `T_tau` denote that complete matched `tau`-side family. Then

\[
\operatorname{supp}(T_\tau)=J,
\qquad
\operatorname{mult}_{\tau}(j)=1.
\]

`ROLEDEF_003_011` identifies this unique remaining closure-side booking with
the already-defined `G` office; this is not a new definition of `G`. Hence

\[
\boxed{G=T_\tau=\operatorname{Cap}\tau.}

\[

\qquad
