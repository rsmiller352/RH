# Count discipline (D-030.Q2) — channel-count ≠ type-count ≠ rank-count ≠ spatial dimension ≠ speed

## 0) Governance & Scope Lock

Prohibition card (GUARD): states the count-discipline firewall, once. Other cards
REFERENCE this card; they never restate it (TD-013). Never load-bearing for any
computation.

## 1) Plain language

Several different things in and around this book happen to involve the number three —
and several more will. Three connectivity channels. Three registers needed for a
persistent closure record. Three rows. Later, perhaps, three of something geometric.
This card is the standing rule that a shared NUMBER is never a shared MEANING: each count
counts its own things, and gluing two counts together because both read "3" is exactly
the mistake this spine's history caught twice and named. If two counts are ever to be
identified, a theorem at the right layer must do it — explicitly, as output.

## 2) Statement (the prohibition, stated once — D-030.Q2)

**Five count-like notions are never automatically identical:**

1. **channel-count** — c = 3, the count of connectivity channels (DEF_003_005);
2. **type-count** — the count of quantity-types/tracked categories (DEF_003_001's list;
   the EQ_CT21 defect identified this with channel-count silently — refused);
3. **rank-count** — counts of rank-tag composites (CONV_S02_004 (retired D-064 → native split THM_003_018; rank tags non-load-bearing)'s typing) and, distinctly,
   the M_rank ladder index (S04's name — CONV_S02_013);
4. **spatial dimension** — no spatial dimension EXISTS at S02; "three-dimensional" is a
   later-layer notion that flows forward only (D-025.R10);
5. **speed** — c carries no rate semantics of any kind (pre-metric layer; TD-019).

**c = 3 means three connectivity channels at S02** — not physical speed, not 3D space.

**Register-threshold clause (seam S-4):** THM_002_011's |R| ≥ 3 persistence threshold is
a REGISTER count at S01; its numeric agreement with c = 3 is a coincidence of values
until a forward-reading theorem makes it a consequence — no identification is licensed
in either direction (the promoted card's §5 non-license, enforceable here).

**Identification route (the only legal one):** any identification among these counts must
arrive as the OUTPUT of a theorem at the lowest layer where both sides exist, read
forward (TD-020) — never as a reading, a convenience, or an axiom.

## 5) Scope & non-overclaim

Fences automatic identifications only; it does not assert the counts are DIFFERENT in
some deep sense (TD-018 discipline: non-identification is scoped non-licensing, not a
denial — a future theorem may legitimately relate two counts, by the route above).
