(`τ`) and **support** (`m`) that book TO the invariant `I` — the two oriented sides that meet at `I`.

**Step 3 (the role is uniform across each row).** A lower row of `M_base` carries a single role, the

- `E` is in row 2 `[τ, E]` → **service**. This matches its content: `DEF_003_015`'s row-2 reading is

**Step 5 (I, c not sides).** By `THM_003_017`, the top row `[I, c]` are the invariants — booked-TO,
distinguishing nothing. So `I` and `c` are on **neither** side; the split is over rows 2 and 3 only.
∎(5)

**∴** `M_base`'s rows are the split: **service `{τ, E}`**, **support `{m, G}`**, **invariants
