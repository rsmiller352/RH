# Tracked categories of the base book — the Corollary 2 handoff receiver (honest DEF, D-030.Q5 fallback exercised)

## 0) Governance & Scope Lock

Definition card: receives THM_002_011 Corollary 2's handoff and stipulates what the base
book tracks. Exact symbolic forms only. The stipulation is labeled as such everywhere — no
sentence on this card may read as a derivation of the list. The residual CATEGORY is named;
the residual RULE is not stated (theorem target, D-030.Q6). Layer ceiling S02.

## 1) Plain language

The previous section proved that when a chain of transfers closes without exactly retracing
itself, the book keeps a permanent record — and that keeping records at all requires
accounting structure. This card says what kind of entries this book's accounting keeps:
the unit being distinguished, which closure records are held, what a closure leaves open
for continued booking, and which bookings closed out exactly. We tried to PROVE this is
the unique necessary list and could not — so the list is honestly declared as a design,
with the alternatives written next to it, not dressed up as a theorem.

## 2) Statement

**(a) Accounting requirement (consumed, formal).** THM_002_011 Corollary 2: persistence of
ledger-closure records requires accounting structure — registers and records, over
DEF_002_001's register set R.

**(b) Tracked-category list (STIPULATED — defined, not proven; D-030.Q5).** The base book
tracks, per register family:

- **T1 — unit distinction:** the existence-typed unit (I = 1, AX_001_001) whose admissible
  counts the book's holdings are holdings OF.
- **T2 — retained closure state:** which persistent ledger-closure records (THM_002_011
  §2(b)'s persisting candidates) the book currently holds.
- **T3 — remainder/residual:** the slot for what a closure leaves open for continued
  booking. The category is named here; the RULE governing it (the bottom-row residual
  rule, D-023.1's reading) is a theorem target (D-030.Q6) and is not stated on this card.
- **T4 — completed discharge:** bookings identified with ε (closed, exactly retraced —
  CONV_S01_003's erasure class, as consumed by THM_002_011).

**(c) Status clause.** The list (b) is a stipulation. §4 records which fragments are
genuinely forced by S01 results and exactly where forcing stops; the closed-list claim
(these four, jointly sufficient, none mergeable) carries no derivation. Alternatives are
named in §4.

## 4) Justification (DEF obligation: well-formedness + the recorded derivation attempt)

**The attempt (Q5: derivation first).** Can the list (b) be derived from S01 formal inputs
(P2's realization requirement + the Corollary 2 seam)? Outcome per category:

- **T1 — forced, degenerately.** All booked quantity is typed holdings of distinctions
  (DEF_002_001 over AX_001_001): a book tracking nothing unit-typed tracks nothing. This
  is forcing only in the sense that the category restates the ground.
- **T2 — forced relative to the seam.** THM_002_011 proves persisting candidates exist
  (|R| ≥ 3) and Corollary 2 hands their records to S02: an accounting structure with no
  category retaining closure state cannot hold what the seam delivers. Forced GIVEN that
  the base book is the seam's receiver — which is this section's design commitment, not an
  S01 theorem.
- **T4 — capability forced; tracked status stipulated.** To book w·reverse(w) as if it never
  happened (CONV_S01_003), the booking process must recognize the decomposition. S01
  forces the RECOGNITION; nothing in S01 decides whether recognition is reified as a
  tracked category rather than process structure. Tracked status is design.
- **T3 — not derivable here.** The only candidate ground for a residual category is the
  bottom-row residual rule ("continuous booking of residual", D-023.1) — itself a theorem
  target (D-030.Q6) whose drafting awaits the librarian corpus search. Stipulated now;
  its derivation obligation rides with that future THM.
- **Closed list — not derivable.** S01 fixes WHAT closes and persists (a classification of
  walks) but is silent on the REPRESENTATION the book uses; no S01 result excludes a fifth
  category or forces non-mergeability.

**Why the attempt fails as a derivation (one sentence):** S01's theorems pin categories
only relative to representational obligations (receive Corollary 2's records; implement
the erasure identification), and nothing at S01 forces the book to reify those obligations
as tracked categories — so necessity holds per-fragment, the list does not.

**Fallback exercised (D-030.Q5).** Alternatives considered and not adopted:

1. **Three-category list** (drop T3 until the residual-rule THM lands) — not adopted: the
   bottom row's booking (DEF_003_003, ROLEDEF_003_011, DEF_003_015) needs the SLOT named even
   while the rule is open; dropping T3 would smuggle the slot in unnamed (the F-020 shape).
2. **Two-state merge** (collapse T2/T4 into one binary classification flag) — not adopted:
   it keeps the classification but discards the record content the seam delivers.
3. **Five-category list** (add per-channel allocation) — not adopted at S02: channels
   arrive at DEF_003_005, but allocation rules are later-layer content (GUARD_001_006).

**Well-formedness:** the four categories are pairwise disjoint in type (ground type;
held records; open slot; discharged class) and each is anchored to a named S01 object;
the definition class is non-empty (the base book of this section instantiates it).
