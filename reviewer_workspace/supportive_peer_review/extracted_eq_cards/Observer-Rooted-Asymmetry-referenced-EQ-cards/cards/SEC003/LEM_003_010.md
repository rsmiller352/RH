# Fairness sufficiency at c = 3 — no channel invariantly distinguished by the rules

## 0) Governance & Scope Lock

Proved subsidiary claim; complete proof on the card. Exact finite combinatorics only.
The group acts on channel LABELS and orientation TAGS — no spatial object, no signed
matrix, no metric. Minimality is banned content here (reserved THM_003_012). Layer
ceiling S02.

## 1) Plain language

Three channels can be perfectly fair. Take every way of renaming the channels among
themselves and flipping each channel's reading direction: that is a set of 48 relabelings,
and any channel can be carried to any other by one of them. So if a property of channels
survives all the relabelings — if it is written into the rules rather than into some
particular state — it must say the same thing about all three channels. The book's own
rules only ever use the COUNT three and symmetric distribution, so they single out no
channel. That is fairness — and this card proves only that three channels CAN be fair,
not that fewer cannot.

## 2) Statement

Let C = {x, y, z} be the channel label set (DEF_003_005) and let **G₃** be the channel
relabeling group generated by (i) the permutations of C and (ii) the per-channel
orientation reversals (re-descriptions of each channel's two oriented readings,
DEF_002_002). Then:

**(a) Order.** |G₃| = 3! · 2³ = 48.

**(b) Transitivity.** G₃ acts transitively on C (already its permutation subgroup does).

**(c) Fairness sufficiency (D-025.R5).** Every channel property invariant under G₃ is
constant across the three channels; and the S02 rule set is G₃-equivariant (its
statements use only the count c and symmetric distribution, never an individual channel
label). Hence **no channel is invariantly distinguished by the rules** — a fair
realization of three channels EXISTS, exhibited by the S02 book itself.

**Not claimed (the split, D-026.Q4):** minimality — that no n < 3 admits a fair
realization — is reserved as THM_003_012 (GAP-S02-B).

## 3) Input contract (GR-1)

Objects: C (finite label set, |C| = 3, DEF_003_005); orientation tags σ per channel (two
readings per channel, DEF_002_002 — tags carry NO arithmetic, GUARD_001_005); G₃
(card-local group: pairs (p, t) with p a permutation of C and t a reversal-tag triple;
composition = composition of relabelings). A **channel property** is a map P : C → X (any
codomain); P is invariant under g ∈ G₃ iff P(g·ch) = P(ch) for all ch ∈ C, where g acts
on channels through its permutation part. Domains: finite. Quantifiers: universal over
G₃ and over properties (the latter discharged by proof, not enumeration). Finite-N
surface: complete enumeration of G₃ and orbits. Limit mode: none.

## 4) Proof (complete)

**Step 1 (order).** A relabeling is determined by (p, t): p one of the 3! = 6
permutations of C; t one of the 2³ = 8 assignments of a reading orientation to each
channel. Composition is componentwise (permutations compose; tags relabel along p and
toggle), distinct pairs give distinct relabelings, so |G₃| = 6 · 8 = 48. The orientation
toggles are re-descriptions (DEF_002_002 §2): no tag arithmetic is used or needed —
"two toggles restore the reading" is substitution of descriptions, not a composition law
on tags (GUARD_001_005 respected; the group's law lives on relabelings, not on tags).

**Step 2 (transitivity).** For any ch_i, ch_j ∈ C the transposition (ch_i ch_j) ∈ Sym(C)
⊆ G₃ (with all tags unchanged) carries ch_i to ch_j. So the action is transitive.

**Step 3 (invariance ⇒ constancy).** Let P be invariant under G₃ and ch_i, ch_j ∈ C. By
Step 2 pick g with g·ch_i = ch_j. Then P(ch_j) = P(g·ch_i) = P(ch_i). Hence P is constant
on C.

**Step 4 (the S02 rule set is equivariant — the exhibit).** Inspection of the section's
statements: **no S02 rule names an individual channel** x, y, or z — the channel labels
appear only as names (DEF_003_005's typing) or in label-symmetric contexts that survive
any relabeling; every S02 occurrence of channel structure enters only through the **count**
c = 3 and **symmetric distribution across ALL channels**. (This is a structural property of
the S02 rule FORM — the rules are channel-label-free by construction — established without
citing any individual downstream rule; see the AMENDMENT note, D-064.) A statement built
from the count and all-channel distribution is preserved by every relabeling of C, i.e.
G₃-equivariant. By Step 3, any channel property the RULES
define is constant across channels: no channel is invariantly distinguished by the rules.
With D-025.R5's fairness this is sufficiency: a fair 3-channel realization exists. ∎

**What this proof does NOT use:** no 3D ambient, no spatial axes (the F-108 circularity
stays out); no signed matrices (reversals are tag re-descriptions); no minimality
reasoning of any kind.
