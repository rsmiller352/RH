# Exact derived-representation ontological separation

## 0) Governance and scope lock

This gate determines whether the nonprimitive downstream status of an **already
scientifically established typed quantity** is adequately grounded. It does not
establish the quantity, derive its value, admit a mathematical domain, or
replace the quantity's owning scientific card.

The symbols `q`, `GatePass`, and \(\mathcal P_0\) below are metanotation for the
gate contract. They are not theory-native quantity symbols, matrix entries, or
new primitive objects. Here \(\mathcal P_0\) abbreviates the class of objects
admitted as primitive `rel_token` objects by `AX_001_001`.

## 1) Gate conditions

A registered typed quantity \(q\) passes this gate only when all of the
following hold:

1. an authoritative owning card scientifically establishes \(q\);
2. the owning card derives or defines \(q\) from identified upstream relational
   ground within its declared claim tier;
3. \(q\) has a registered downstream role and is not typed as a primitive
   relation-token;
4. the owning card or a separately promoted verifier supplies an exact,
   independently specified, nonvacuous verification obligation appropriate to
   the declared role of \(q\);
5. the invariant tested by that obligation is established independently and
   upstream of \(q\);
6. every auxiliary verification input is upstream of \(q\);
7. no verification input contains \(q\), depends on \(q\), or contains a
   descendant of \(q\);
8. the verification operation is independently licensed and is not defined
   merely to reproduce the desired result;
9. \(q\) contributes substantively to discharging the verification obligation;
10. the active dependency path is acyclic;
11. no current authoritative record admits \(q\) as a primitive relation-token;
12. all existing domain, sign, orientation, phase, typing, provenance, bridge,
    comparison, and empirical guards remain satisfied.

The verification obligation is role-specific. This gate does not require all
quantities to share one algebraic identity and does not require reconstruction
of an entire relational object when the declared role represents only a named
invariant of that object.

## 2) Gate result

When the twelve conditions hold, the repository may record:

\[
\operatorname{GatePass}(q)
\Longrightarrow
\operatorname{OntologicalSeparationEstablished}(q),
\]

and, under the primitive-admission rule of `AX_001_001`, conclude that \(q\)
is not a primitive relation-token:

\[
q\notin\mathcal P_0.
\]

Passing this gate does **not** establish scientific admissibility. Scientific
admissibility, value, scope, and interpretation remain grounded in the owning
scientific card.

Failure to pass does not prove that \(q\) is primitive:

\[
\neg\operatorname{GatePass}(q)
\centernot\Longrightarrow
q\in\mathcal P_0.
\]

Failure returns:

`ONTOLOGICAL_SEPARATION_UNESTABLISHED`

and blocks the claimed downstream use until the specific provenance, typing,
domain, verification, or dependency defect is resolved.

## 3) Representation fence

Ontological admission attaches to registered typed objects. It does not attach
automatically to constants, operators, or subexpressions appearing in their
exact value representations.

Thus an equality such as

\[
t_{\mathrm{base}}=2\pi
\]

does not independently register \(\pi\), \(2\pi\), or the displayed expression
as a primitive relation-token.

Number-theoretic classification alone neither admits nor prohibits a
representation. Every representation remains subject to its owning scientific
card and its existing domain and typing guards.

## 4) Forced-step account

This is a gate contract, not a value theorem. Its result follows by authority
classification:

1. `AX_001_001` fixes primitive admission at the relation-token level.
2. Conditions 1–10 establish that \(q\) is an accountable downstream typed
   quantity rather than self-grounding or circular input.
3. Condition 11 confirms that current authority contains no separate primitive
   admission for \(q\).
4. Condition 12 preserves every narrower scientific fence.
5. Therefore the already-established downstream use of \(q\) is ontologically
   separated from primitive admission.

The gate creates no new scientific object and does not promote the owning
scientific claim.

## 5) Scope and nonclaims

This gate does not assert that exact recovery is necessary for every possible
derived quantity. It does not admit arbitrary fractional, irrational,
transcendental, signed, imaginary, complex, continuous, or smooth quantities.
It does not convert a writable expression into scientific evidence. It does not
permit a downstream result to ground its own origin. It does not reinterpret a
matrix-generating invariant as an ontological primitive, or vice versa.

## 6) Verification

The witness checks the twelve-condition contract, upstream independence,
nonvacuity, acyclicity, primitive-admission exclusion, asymmetric failure
meaning, and all five corruption modes. It certifies the gate logic only; it
does not certify any future quantity presented to the gate.
