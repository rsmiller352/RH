# Norm-free generation: the two-active (a,a,0)-orbit spans Λ_FCC — span_ℤ of the signed coordinate permutations of (1,1,0) equals the even-coordinate-sum lattice; a parity/linear-algebra identity, no minimal-norm, no superlattice exclusion

## 0) Governance & Scope Lock

**LEM card (forced-shell cleanup) — the norm-free generation half.** Once the forced two-active / one-idle orbit is presented as the coordinate orbit of `(1,1,0)` (`LEM_004_006`, at `a=1`), it already generates the FCC lattice by integer span. This is a **parity + linear-algebra** calculation only. It does NOT use minimal norm, nearest-neighbor selection, packing, density, or superlattice exclusion. PRE-METRIC (TD-019). The coordinate `0` is display absence (an idle channel, `DEF_004_001`), never a zero quantity.

## 1) Plain language

The FCC lattice `Λ_FCC` has two equal descriptions (`DEF_004_003`): the integer span of `(0,1,1),(1,0,1),(1,1,0)`, and the set of integer triples whose coordinate sum is even. The three generators are themselves signed permutations of `(1,1,0)` — members of the forced size-12 orbit. And every member of that orbit has coordinate sum `∈ {−2,0,2}`, hence even. So the forced orbit both **lies inside** the even-sum lattice and **contains generators of it** — therefore its integer span is exactly `Λ_FCC`. No shortest-vector or minimal-norm argument is needed.

## 2) Statement

Let `S_2act := { signed coordinate permutations of (1,1,0) }` be the size-12 `(a,a,0)`-orbit at `a=1` (`LEM_004_006`; `DEF_004_001` presentation). Then

$$\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb Z} S_{\mathrm{2act}} \;=\; \Lambda_{\mathrm{FCC}} \;=\; \{(x,y,z)\in\mathbb Z^3 : x+y+z \text{ even}\}.$$

## 3) Input contract (GR-1)

**Consumed (3 edges):** the two-descriptions identity `Λ_FCC = span{(0,1,1),(1,0,1),(1,1,0)} = {even sum}` ← `DEF_004_003`; the coordinate-presentation grammar (signed permutations, O_h, zero-as-absence) ← `DEF_004_001`; the unique size-12 `(a,a,0)`-orbit `S_12` at `a=1` ← `LEM_004_006`. **Domains:** `ℤ³`. **Quantifiers:** universal over integer triples (the set equality). **Finite-N surface:** the witness's bounded-box enumeration. **No minimal-norm, no superlattice exclusion, no packing, no metric, no 12-port hypothesis.**

## 4) Proof

**(⊆)** Every vector in `S_2act` has two entries `±1` and one entry `0`, so its coordinate sum is `∈ {−2,0,2}` — even. The even-sum set is closed under integer linear combination (a sum of even integers is even), so `span_ℤ S_2act ⊆ {(x,y,z)∈ℤ³ : x+y+z even} = Λ_FCC` (`DEF_004_003`).

**(⊇)** The three defining generators of `Λ_FCC` (`DEF_004_003`), `(0,1,1), (1,0,1), (1,1,0)`, are each a signed coordinate permutation of `(1,1,0)`, hence members of `S_2act`. Therefore `Λ_FCC = span_ℤ{(0,1,1),(1,0,1),(1,1,0)} ⊆ span_ℤ S_2act`.

The two inclusions give `span_ℤ S_2act = Λ_FCC`. ∎

## 5) Scope & non-overclaim

This lemma proves only **norm-free generation**. It does NOT prove that the orbit is *forced* (that is `THM_004_021` (P2) + the orbit-type route, completed in `THM_004_027`). It does NOT select the orbit by minimal norm. It does NOT exclude superlattices by any metric argument (deliberately — that is `THM_004_009` clause 2's minimal-norm route, avoided here). It does NOT assign η, derive cell volume, or import physical geometry. `DEF_S03_004`'s port hypothesis and `THM_S03_024`'s P3 selector are not consumed.
