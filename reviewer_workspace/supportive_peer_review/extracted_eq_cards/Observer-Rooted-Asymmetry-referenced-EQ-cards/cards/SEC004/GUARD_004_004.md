# FCC/RD scope guard (D-028): realization is not thereby ontology — ontology UNRESOLVED, not denied

## 0) Governance & Scope Lock

Prohibition card (GUARD): states the D-028 scope fence on the FCC/RD realization, once.
Other cards REFERENCE this card (TD-013); they never restate it as litany. Never
load-bearing for any computation. The guard scopes one realization; it does not touch
P4's license of geometry as relational mathematics (AX_001_002).

## 1) Plain language

This section builds a specific geometric realization — the FCC lattice and its rhombic
dodecahedron cell — and proves real theorems about it. This card is the standing rule
about what that buys: using the realization never promotes it to what the universe IS.
Equally, nothing here says the realization is NOT what the universe is. The ontology
question is open in both directions, on purpose, and stays open until something at the
right layer could actually decide it.

## 2) Statement (the prohibition, stated once — D-028 consequence-3 wording)

**"FCC/RD may be used as a geometric realization of the bookkeeping structure, but it is
not thereby promoted to ontology."**

Scoped in BOTH directions (TD-018):

1. **No promotion:** no S03–S08 card may cite the FCC/RD realization's use, success, or
   theorem-grade as evidence that FCC/RD IS the physical substrate. Realization standing
   is earned per theorem; ontological standing is earned nowhere in the bridge phases.
2. **No denial:** the guard is never to be read or quoted as "FCC/RD is not ontology."
   Ontology is UNRESOLVED, not denied. FCC/RD stays fully ACTIVE as a theorem-target
   geometry (D-025.R2/R3).
3. **P4 untouched:** this guard scopes one specific realization. Geometry as the
   mathematics of relation (AX_001_002) is not modified, qualified, or weakened by it.

## 5) Scope & non-overclaim

Fences the realization's epistemic status only; it does not restrict any computation,
theorem, or use of FCC/RD objects (those are licensed by their own cards under
DEF_004_001 and DEF_004_002). The Gen-2 demotion enters as lineage — retained as scope,
not adjudicated as right or wrong (D-028's own posture). Every downstream card using the
lattice/cell inherits this guard by reference (dossier seam F-6).
