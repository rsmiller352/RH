# Facet relevance — no Λ_FCC vector with ‖λ‖² ≥ 4 binds the Voronoi cell; minimal vectors are always facet-defining; corollary: C_u = Vor_Λ_FCC(0) at scale 2u

## 0) Governance & Scope Lock

Lemma with complete proofs. Exact arithmetic only. The general faces=nearest-neighbors
reading is BANNED (false in general — §5); what is proved is (a) a general
minimal-vector sub-lemma, (b) the FCC non-binding bound, (c) the Voronoi identification
corollary. Layer ceiling S03.

## 1) Plain language

The cell of a lattice point is carved by perpendicular bisector planes toward every
other lattice point. This page proves that for our lattice only the twelve nearest
points actually carve: every farther point's bisector misses the cell entirely (the very
nearest farther shell just grazes it at six corner points without cutting). It also
proves the general fact that the nearest points of ANY lattice always carve a genuine
face — while carefully NOT claiming the false converse that faces always come from
nearest points (there is a standard counterexample).

## 2) Statement

Work at native scale ($2u = 1$, i.e. $C := C_{1/2}$; the scale law is linear and carried
to general $u$ in the corollary). Let
$\mathrm{Vor}(0) := \{p : p \cdot \lambda \le \tfrac12 \|\lambda\|^2 \ \ \forall
\lambda \in \Lambda_{\mathrm{FCC}} \setminus \{0\}\}$.

1. **(Non-binding bound, FCC instance.)** For every $\lambda \in \Lambda_{\mathrm{FCC}}$
   with $\|\lambda\|^2 \ge 4$: $\max_{p \in C}\ p \cdot \lambda \le
   \tfrac12\|\lambda\|^2$, with equality possible only for $\|\lambda\|^2 = 4$ (the
   $(\pm2,0,0)$-type vectors), where the bisector TOUCHES $C$ at a single axis vertex
   and cuts nothing.
2. **(Minimal vectors facet-defining — general sub-lemma.)** For any lattice
   $\Lambda \subset \mathbb{R}^3$ and any minimal vector $v$ (nonzero, of minimal norm),
   the bisector of $v$ supports a facet of $\mathrm{Vor}_\Lambda(0)$.
3. **(Corollary — the Voronoi identification.)** $\mathrm{Vor}_{\Lambda_{\mathrm{FCC}}}(0)
   = C_{1/2}$; at general scale, $C_u = (2u) \cdot C_{1/2}$ is the Voronoi cell of the
   rescaled lattice $(2u) \cdot \Lambda_{\mathrm{FCC}}$ — "$C_u =
   \mathrm{Vor}_{\Lambda_{\mathrm{FCC}}}(0)$ at scale $2u$."

## 4) Proof (complete)

**Clause 1 (non-binding).** By THM_004_007 (at $2u = 1$), $C$ is the convex hull of its
14 vertices; the maximum of the linear functional $p \mapsto p \cdot \lambda$ over $C$
is attained at a vertex. Vertex norms: cube-type $(\pm\tfrac12,\pm\tfrac12,\pm\tfrac12)$
have norm $\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2} < 1$; axis-type $(\pm1, 0, 0)$-type have norm $1$. So for
every $p \in C$: $p \cdot \lambda \le \|p\| \|\lambda\| \le 1 \cdot \|\lambda\|$
(Cauchy–Schwarz). For $\|\lambda\|^2 \ge 4$, i.e. $\|\lambda\| \ge 2$:
$\|\lambda\| \le \tfrac12 \|\lambda\|^2$ (equivalent to $\|\lambda\| \ge 2$). Hence
$\max_C\ p \cdot \lambda \le \tfrac12\|\lambda\|^2$ — the bisector half-space contains
$C$: no cut. **Equality analysis:** equality forces $\|p\| = 1$ (an axis vertex),
$\|\lambda\| = 2$, and Cauchy–Schwarz equality $p \parallel \lambda$: so
$\lambda = 2p$ — a $(\pm2, 0, 0)$-type vector (which IS in $\Lambda_{\mathrm{FCC}}$,
even sum). For exactly these, the bisector touches $C$ at the single vertex $p$ and the
half-space still contains $C$: touching, never binding. For $\|\lambda\|^2 \ge 6$
(LEM_004_005: norms are even, so the next values are 6, 8, …) the inequality is strict
everywhere. ∎(1)

**Clause 2 (minimal vectors facet-defining — general).** Let $v$ be minimal in
$\Lambda$ and consider the midpoint $v/2$. For any $w \in \Lambda \setminus \{0, v\}$,
suppose $w$'s bisector constraint is tight-or-violated at $v/2$:
$\|v/2 - w\| \le \|v/2\|$. Expanding both sides: $\|w\|^2 \le v \cdot w$. By
Cauchy–Schwarz $v \cdot w \le \|v\|\|w\|$, so $\|w\| \le \|v\|$; minimality gives
$\|w\| = \|v\|$, hence equality in Cauchy–Schwarz: $w = t v$ with $t > 0$ and
$\|w\| = \|v\|$ forces $w = v$ — contradiction. So EVERY other lattice point's
constraint is strictly slack at $v/2$, while $v$'s own constraint is tight
($v/2 \cdot v = \tfrac12\|v\|^2$). A point of the bisector at which all other
constraints are strictly slack lies in the relative interior of the face
$\mathrm{Vor}(0) \cap \{p \cdot v = \tfrac12\|v\|^2\}$ and has a neighborhood in the
bisector plane inside the cell (slackness is open): the face is 2-dimensional — a
facet. ∎(2)

**Clause 3 (corollary).** $\mathrm{Vor}(0)$ is cut out by the bisector constraints of
all nonzero $\lambda$. Norms on $\Lambda_{\mathrm{FCC}}$ are even (LEM_004_005), so the
constraint set splits into the shell $\|\lambda\|^2 = 2$ — exactly the constraints
$p \cdot n \le 1$, $n \in S_{12}$, i.e. exactly $C_{1/2}$'s constraints — and
$\|\lambda\|^2 \ge 4$, all implied by membership of $C_{1/2}$ (clause 1). Hence
$\mathrm{Vor}(0) = C_{1/2}$. Both sides scale linearly in the lattice scale, giving the
$C_u$ form. ∎(3)
