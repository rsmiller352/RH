## 1) Plain language

The geometry section computes with triples of integers and real numbers. This card defines
what those triples ARE in this book: a **presentation** of relational bookkeeping — not a
background space. The three coordinate slots are the presentation of the channel↔axis seam;
that seam is not a choice this card makes — it is **forced** downstream (THM_004_021 derives
it by equivariance), and this card just carries its presentation. A minus sign on a coordinate
is a worn **orientation tag** (which way the relation is read), never a negative quantity; a
**0** is channel-absence in the display, never a (σ, 0) pair. Relabeling the channels or
flipping an orientation gives the presentation's symmetry group — the **48 signed
permutations**, `O_h` — a presentation symmetry, not a symmetry of physical space. And the
norms, radii, and volumes computed downstream are **model-internal magnitudes**, not physical
lengths.

## 2) Statement (the grammar, defined once)

**(1) Ambient as a presentation OBJECT.** S03 computes in the ambient $\mathbb{Z}^3 \subset
\mathbb{R}^3$, defined as the section's **relational presentation** under P4 (AX_001_002):
points, lattices, and cells built in it are presentation-layer objects whose standing is
exactly what their cards prove. **The ambient is not a background space** (GUARD_S02_015).

**(2) Coordinate slots as the presentation of the forced seam (NOT physical axes).** The three
coordinate slots present the channel↔axis correspondence. That correspondence is **FORCED by
equivariance at `THM_004_021`** (the S02→S03 seam: the channel-coupling action `G₃` and the
FCC-shell action `O_h` are the same transitive action ⟹ `G₃ ≅ O_h` derived) — **the
seam-forcing home is THM_004_021, not this card.** This DEF defines the slots as presentation
slots and carries the slot↔channel labeling **as a presentation of that forced seam**; it does
**NOT** declare, claim, or re-derive the correspondence (forward-pointer only; no dependency
edge to THM_004_021 — cycle-avoidance). The slots are **not physical axes** (GUARD_003_009: no
silent channel↔axis identification; the count $c = 3$ and the ambient dimension 3 are not
identified by any theorem *here*).

**(3) Signed entries = orientation × strictly-positive magnitude (DEF_002_002 instantiated).**
A coordinate value $\pm a$ is the pair $(\sigma, a)$: an orientation tag $\sigma$ (the
channel's two relation-readings) and a **strictly positive** magnitude $a$. The inline sign
notation is shorthand for that pair. **NO tag arithmetic**: sign flips are re-descriptions
(GUARD_001_005), never multiplication; products of coordinates inside norms and supports are
arithmetic on MAGNITUDES organized by tags, licensed as presentation-layer computation.

**(4) Coordinate 0 = channel / display absence (no (σ, 0) pair).** A coordinate component
written $0$ denotes **channel-absence** in the display tuple (the record-absent predicate at
the presentation layer, D-045 display form); the entry rule $x \mapsto (\sigma, |x|)$ is
invoked **only for nonzero components** — **no $(\sigma, 0)$ pair exists**. The magnitude
codomain is $\mathcal{D}^+$ (strictly positive, DEF_002_002).

**(5) The presentation-symmetry group `O_h`.** Defined **coordinate-side**: the group of signed
coordinate permutations of the ambient — the $3!$ permutations of the three coordinate **slots**
($S_3$) together with the $2^3$ per-slot **sign-flips** — of order $6 \cdot 8 = 48$
(conventional mathematical name: the full octahedral group; the name imports no physical
content). **`O_h` is a PRESENTATION symmetry** — the coordinate-relabeling symmetry of the
declared model — **not a symmetry of physical space**. (That these coordinate permutations
*present* channel relabelings × per-channel orientation reversals is the **forced-downstream
seam** `THM_004_021` — presented here, **not consumed**: no premise, no dependency edge. The
definition of $O_h$ above is purely coordinate-side and channel-free.) It is the symmetry
vocabulary every S03 card uses (LEM_004_006 enumerates its orbits).

**(6) Model-internal magnitude semantics (TD-019).** Norms, distances-in-the-model, inradii,
and volumes computed downstream are **model-internal magnitudes** of the presentation. They are
**not physical lengths**; no speed reading of $c$ becomes available by their existence (there is
no rate pairing a magnitude with a time at this layer). Their defocused physical readout, if
any, is S07's BRIDGE business (pointer only).

## 4) Justification (definition; the choice-framing superseded by THM_004_021 for the seam)

The grammar is the house entry rule (DEF_002_002) applied per-object to coordinates — exactly
the recast DOSSIER_S02's discharge note 5 routed to S03. **Why a DEF and not a convention:**
the one duty that once read as a *declared choice* — the channel↔axis correspondence — is now
**FORCED by `THM_004_021`** (equivariance, $G_3 \cong O_h$). What survives is not a choice but
a **typed presentation grammar**: the ambient object, the slots, the signed/zero display
discipline, and the presentation-symmetry group $O_h$ — each exact and witness-checked. Type
follows surviving content; the CONV framing under-typed definitional machinery.

**The choice-among-alternatives, retained as justification (not type-determining).** The
presentation is still the *minimal typed presentation machinery* for the existing S03
computations, and the alternatives are named for the record: (1) an **abstract lattice
presentation** (a rank-3 free module with a declared quadratic form) — coherent but strictly
heavier: every corpus anchor and golden value (F-070/F-071/F-105/F-135) is recorded in signed
coordinates, so it would re-derive the same content behind a translation layer, with the signs
already tags by (3); (2) **BRIDGE-typing all coordinate work** — rejected: P4 licenses geometry
as the INTERNAL mathematics (AX_001_002); (3) **silent use** — rejected (the TD-002/TD-016
shape this grammar exists to prevent). That these alternatives exist is *justification for
retaining this presentation*, not evidence that the grammar is a convention — the grammar is a
definition; only the superseded seam-correspondence was ever the "choice," and it is now
THM_004_021's forced output.

## 7) Verification (witness — exact integer/rational; migrated from CONV_S03_003's witness)

Witness `tools/verifications/S03/DEF_S03_034_witness.py` (exact integer arithmetic; no float, no
π, no √2 — this grammar's contract is combinatorial/structural):

1. **`O_h` well-formed (§2(5)).** Construct the map explicitly — all $6 \times 8 = 48$
   (relabeling, reversal-pattern) pairs sent to signed permutation matrices; assert the map is
   **injective**, the image is **closed under product and inverse** (group; $M\cdot M^\top = I$
   per element, identity present), and its **order is exactly 48** (count-reconciled: 48
   constructed = 48 distinct).
2. **Signed/zero entry rule well-formed (§2(3)–(4)).** For a deterministic sample of signed
   integer triples: decompose each **nonzero** coordinate per §2(3) into (tag, magnitude) and
   reassemble; assert the roundtrip is the **identity**, every FORMED pair's magnitude is
   **strictly positive**, and **zero components form NO pair** (value-level: formed-pair count =
   nonzero-component count; no $(\sigma, 0)$ pair exists).
3. **No tag composition (§2(3)).** The tag type defines **NO composition** — attempting
   tag·tag raises / is absent (checked, not assumed; the DEF_002_002 witness pattern).
4. **Can-fail — one mode per falsifier (`--corrupt` / `--mode`), REQUIRED.** Bare `--corrupt` =
   `--mode order` (the card-named default): drop the orientation-reversal generators → the
   **order-48 assertion fails** (collapses to 6 = **F-CG1**). `--mode zeropair`: form a $(\sigma,
   0)$ pair for a zero component → check (2) fails (**F-CG2**). `--mode tagmul`: inject a
   composition onto the tag type → check (3) fails (**F-CG3**). Each mode flips ONLY its target
   check (TD-035 one-mode-one-check).

A pass certifies the **well-formedness of the definition** (the presentation-symmetry group IS
order-48; the entry rule roundtrips with strictly-positive magnitudes and no (σ,0) pair; the tag
carries no arithmetic) — the definitional content, witness-checked (not a truth-claim about the
world; TD-011). Implementation integers (the finite sample, the matrix entries, the display
absence marker) are implementation-side presentation objects (GUARD_001_008 §2; D-045).

## 8) Falsifiers

As in frontmatter — **F-CG1** (`O_h` ill-formed: non-injective / non-group / not order-48),

a convention, a DEF has structural well-formedness to certify; TD-011 applies to the
*adequacy/truth* claim, which this card does not make.)
