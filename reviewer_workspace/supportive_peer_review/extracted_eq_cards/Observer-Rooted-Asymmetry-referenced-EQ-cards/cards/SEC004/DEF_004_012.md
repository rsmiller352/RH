# The residual, in transfer form — D + Ω = I (share-split); Ω = the density of the RD portion not occupied by the sphere; 0 < Ω < 1 proved; Ω the SUPPORT-row residual share of M_cell (row 3, THM_004_023; D-064 re-grounding — the retired 'derived-N1' label superseded, Ω's side is its row)

## 1) Plain language

The ball fills part of the cell; the rest of the cell is the rest of the cell. One
whole cell of volume-share splits into two registers: the filled share D and the void
share Ω, together exactly the whole — that is the bookkeeping sentence, and it is a
sentence about shares, not a subtraction. The void share comes out exact:
1 − π/(3√2), strictly between 0 and 1 (because π is strictly less than 3√2 — proved,
not eyeballed). What the void share MEANS beyond geometry — in particular whether it
has anything to do with mass — is deliberately not decided here; one old hypothesis to
that effect is kept in a locked box, feeding nothing.

## 2) Statement

**The share-split (transfer form, P3):**

$$D + \Omega = I$$

— filled share plus void share equals the whole: a share-split of the unit cell volume
between the filled and void registers (DEF_002_004 §4's complement trichotomy; the
conserved total is the unit, the invariant I). No subtraction occurs; $\Omega$ is the unique holding
whose sum with $D$ restores the whole (existential characterization, AX_S01_006
uniqueness through the chain).

**DEFINITION (D-025.R8, verbatim):** **Ω = density of the RD portion not occupied by
the sphere.**

**Derived value clause (exact, witness-checked):** with $D = \pi/(3\sqrt2)$
(THM_004_011),

$$\Omega = 1 - \frac{\pi}{3\sqrt2}.$$

**Derived bounds clause (proved, §4):** $0 < \Omega < 1$, via
$\pi^2 < 16 < 18 \Rightarrow \pi < 3\sqrt2$.

## 4) Justification (DEF obligation: well-formedness + the two derived clauses)

- **Well-formedness of the split:** D is exact and (by the bounds below) lies strictly
  between 0 and 1, so the complement register's holding Ω exists and is unique in the
  quantity cone (the unique d with $D + d = I$ — AX_S01_006 cancellativity through
  DEF_002_004's characterization; strictly positive by the proved bounds 0 < Ω < 1 — the
  strict-case reading, amendment report §3.9.1,
  reports/2026-06-12_s01_positivity_amendment_proposals.md). The split is a P3
  share-split within the conserved
  unit: no signed object appears. [Wording amended 2026-06-12 — D-046 proposal 5; was
  "unique in the nonnegative cone"; the math is untouched — the existing proof already
  makes Ω strictly positive.]
- **Value clause:** $\Omega = 1 - \pi/(3\sqrt2)$ — direct complement of THM_004_011's
  exact D.
- **Bounds clause (the LEM_02_011 route, proved here):** $\pi < 4$ (the unit disc lies
  strictly inside the circumscribed $2 \times 2$ square, so $\pi$ — the disc's area —
  is strictly less than 4), hence $\pi^2 < 16 < 18 = (3\sqrt2)^2$, hence (positivity)
  $\pi < 3\sqrt2$, i.e. $D < 1$ and $\Omega > 0$. And $D > 0$ (π > 0) gives
  $\Omega < 1$. So $0 < \Omega < 1$: the residual is strictly present and strictly less
  than the whole. ∎
- **Side honesty (native, D-064):** Ω's side is read off `M_cell`'s rows, not invented and not
  inverted from a retired label: `Ω` is the geometry entry of the **support** row `[m,G,Ω]` (row 3,
  `THM_004_023`) — the standing geometric residual share. `D` is the geometry entry of the **service**
  row `[τ,E,D]` (row 2). `D + Ω = I` closes the service-row filled share and the support-row residual
  share to the invariant. (The retired `THM_S02_023(d)` "residual books service-side" typing is
  superseded — Ω's side is its ROW; RSM's caution: the old label made D and Ω identical, so only the
  row decides.)
