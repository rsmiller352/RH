# Scale lock — it is forbidden to eliminate Ω by varying r: the residual is structural, not a tunable

## 0) Governance & Scope Lock

Prohibition card (GUARD): states the scale lock, once. Other cards REFERENCE this card
(TD-013). Never load-bearing for any computation.

## 1) Plain language

The void share cannot be tuned away — and this card forbids trying. The cell size r is
a scale, and the shares D and Ω do not depend on it at all: make the cells bigger or
smaller and the same fraction is filled, the same remainder is left. So any derivation
that "chooses r" to shrink, inflate, or cancel the residual is doing something the
mathematics already proves impossible — which is exactly why attempting it would signal
a broken step, not a clever one.

## 2) Statement (the prohibition, stated once)

**It is forbidden to eliminate (or adjust) Ω by varying $r$.** The residual is
STRUCTURAL: $D = \pi/(3\sqrt2)$ and $\Omega = 1 - \pi/(3\sqrt2)$ are scale-free —
$r$ cancels exactly (THM_004_011 §2: every $r > 0$ gives the same shares) — so $r$ is
never a tuning parameter for the residual, and no card may treat it as one. A
derivation that varies $r$ to move Ω is in P6/TD-004 reversion territory (converting a
geometric residual into a fudge factor) and is blocked.

## 5) Scope & non-overclaim

Locks one knob at the S02/S03 boundary. Does not restrict legitimate USES of $r$ (an
inradius is a real model magnitude — THM_004_010 computes with it freely); only its use
AGAINST the residual. TD-018: the lock is a non-licensing of a move, not a claim that Ω
is physically immutable (what Ω feeds at runtime is S04's business through the identity
card). Referenced by: THM_004_010 §5, THM_004_011 §5, DEF_004_012 §5.

## 6) Per-symbol accounting

Local: r (named knob — the THM_004_010 §6 disposition applies). Imported: Ω, D ←
DEF_004_012 / THM_004_011 (named in the prohibition only, never computed). Undeclared:
none.

## 7) Verification

Not applicable — structural prohibition. The exactness fact making the ban precise
(r-cancellation) is witnessed at THM_004_011 item (2) (scale-freeness check) — pointed
at, not duplicated (TD-013).
