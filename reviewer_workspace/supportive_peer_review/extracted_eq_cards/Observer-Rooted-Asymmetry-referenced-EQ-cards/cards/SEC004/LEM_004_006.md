# O_h orbit enumeration — orbit sizes 6 / 8 / 12 / 24 / 24 / 48; the ONLY size-12 orbit type is (a,a,0), i.e. S_12 up to scale

## 0) Governance & Scope Lock

Lemma with a complete proof; exact combinatorics only. The group is DEF_004_001's model
relabeling group $O_h$ (order 48). Orbit counts are counts of vectors; no count is
identified with any other count here (GUARD_003_009). Layer ceiling S03.

## 1) Plain language

Take any nonzero direction and apply all forty-eight relabel-and-flip symmetries of the
model. How many distinct vectors do you get? The answer depends only on the pattern of
the vector's coordinate magnitudes, and there are exactly six patterns: an axis vector
gives 6, a cube-diagonal gives 8, a "two-equal-one-zero" vector gives 12, and everything
else gives 24 or 48. The punchline used later: TWELVE happens for exactly one pattern —
the pattern of our minimal shell.

## 2) Statement

Let $O_h$ act on $\mathbb{R}^3 \setminus \{0\}$ by signed coordinate permutations
(DEF_004_001 §2(5)). For $v \neq 0$ with coordinate-magnitude multiset
$\{|v_1|, |v_2|, |v_3|\}$ and $a > b > c > 0$:

| pattern of $v$ (up to the action) | orbit $O_h \cdot v$ size |
|---|---|
| $(a, 0, 0)$ | $6$ |
| $(a, a, a)$ | $8$ |
| $(a, a, 0)$ | $\mathbf{12}$ |
| $(a, b, 0)$ | $24$ |
| $(a, a, b)$ (also $(a,b,b)$-type) | $24$ |
| $(a, b, c)$ | $48$ |

These six patterns are exhaustive; the orbit of $v$ is exactly the set of vectors with
the same coordinate-magnitude multiset; and **the only size-12 orbit type is
$(a, a, 0)$** — i.e. $S_{12}$ up to scale ($a = 1$ gives $S_{12}$ literally).

## 4) Proof (complete)

**Orbit = same-multiset set.** Signed permutations preserve the coordinate-magnitude
multiset (a permutation reorders it; a sign flip fixes it). Conversely any two vectors
with the same multiset are connected: permute the coordinates into matching order, then
flip signs coordinate-wise. So $O_h \cdot v = \{w : \text{multiset}(|w_i|) =
\text{multiset}(|v_i|)\}$.

**Exhaustiveness of the six patterns.** A nonzero multiset has 2, 1, or 0 zeros; the
nonzero entries are all equal, two-equal-one-different, or all distinct. Cases: two
zeros → $(a,0,0)$; one zero → $(a,a,0)$ or $(a,b,0)$; no zero → $(a,a,a)$, $(a,a,b)$
(covering $(a,b,b)$ by renaming), or $(a,b,c)$. Six patterns, no overlap.

**Direct counts** (counting the same-multiset sets):
- $(a,0,0)$: position of the nonzero entry (3) × its sign (2) = $6$.
- $(a,a,a)$: signs only: $2^3 = 8$.
- $(a,a,0)$: position of the zero (3) × signs of the two nonzero entries ($2^2$) = $12$.
- $(a,b,0)$, $a \neq b$: position of the zero (3) × which remaining slot holds $a$ (2)
  × signs ($2^2$) = $24$.
- $(a,a,b)$, $a \neq b$: position of the odd entry (3) × signs ($2^3$) = $24$.
- $(a,b,c)$ distinct: orderings ($3! = 6$) × signs ($2^3$) = $48$.

**Uniqueness of 12.** The size list is $\{6, 8, 12, 24, 24, 48\}$; the value 12 occurs
for exactly one pattern, $(a,a,0)$. At $a = 1$ that orbit is precisely $S_{12}$
(LEM_004_005's shell — same set by inspection of the construction). ∎
