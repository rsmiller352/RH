# Exact derived matrix representations do not enlarge primitive ontology

## 0) Governance and scope lock

This lemma applies `GATE_004_031` only to five already-established typed
objects. It does not establish their scientific admissibility or re-derive
their values. Those duties remain with their owning cards.

## 1) Authoritative owning results

The authoritative spine separately establishes the pre-runtime base
quantities:

\[
t_{\mathrm{base}}=2\pi,
\qquad
m_{\mathrm{base}}t_{\mathrm{base}}=I,
\qquad
m_{\mathrm{base}}=\frac1{2\pi},
\]

through `THM_002_014`.

It separately establishes the active base-book quantities:

\[
\tau=2\pi,
\qquad
m\tau=I,
\qquad
m=\frac1{2\pi},
\]

through `DEF_003_002` and `DEF_003_003`.

It separately establishes the positive model-internal edge invariant:

\[
\eta>0,
\qquad
\eta^2=2,
\qquad
\eta=\sqrt2,
\]

through `THM_004_019` and its promoted geometry ground.

## 2) Base/active distinction

The equalities of represented value do not identify the registered typed
objects. The pre-runtime and active roles remain distinct:

\[
t_{\mathrm{base}}\neq\tau,
\qquad
m_{\mathrm{base}}\neq m
\]

as typed objects, even though the two extent objects share the exact value
representation \(2\pi\) and the two support objects share the exact value
representation \(1/(2\pi)\).

The service/support form for active \(m\) is consumed from `DEF_003_003`; this
lemma does not claim that non-erasure independently derives that form.

## 3) Role-specific verification obligations

The relevant exact obligations are not forced into one artificial identity:

1. \(t_{\mathrm{base}}\) is verified by the established least-positive complete
   orientation-preserving closure characterization in `THM_002_014`.
2. \(m_{\mathrm{base}}\) is verified within the admitted base service/support
   account by
   \[
   m_{\mathrm{base}}t_{\mathrm{base}}=I.
   \]
3. \(\tau\) consumes the established closure value-shape while retaining its
   active booking role.
4. \(m\) is verified within its declared active servicing form by
   \[
   m\tau=I.
   \]
5. \(\eta\) is verified as a positive scalar norm invariant by
   \[
   \eta^2=2.
   \]

The last identity recovers the designated squared-norm invariant. It does not
recover the full edge vector, its signs, orientation, idle-channel identity, or
coordinate permutation.

## 4) Lemma

Each of

\[
t_{\mathrm{base}},\quad m_{\mathrm{base}},\quad\tau,\quad m,\quad\eta
\]

is scientifically established by its owning card, typed downstream of admitted
relational ground, equipped with an exact role-appropriate verification
obligation, and not registered as a primitive `rel_token` object. Each therefore
passes `GATE_004_031`.

Consequently, none of the five typed objects is a primitive relation-token:

\[
t_{\mathrm{base}},
m_{\mathrm{base}},
\tau,
m,
\eta
\notin\mathcal P_0.
\]

Their exact value representations

\[
2\pi,
\qquad
\frac1{2\pi},
\qquad
\sqrt2
\]

do not independently admit those expressions, their operators, or their
constituent constants as primitive ontological objects.

## 5) Matrix consequence

The active matrix may therefore carry \(\tau\), \(m\), and \(\eta\) in their
already-established service, support, and geometry roles without enlarging the
primitive ontology. This conclusion does not place the pre-runtime
\(t_{\mathrm{base}}\) or \(m_{\mathrm{base}}\) into the matrix and does not merge
them with the active entries.

Calling \(\eta\) a matrix top invariant or matrix-generating primitive does not
make it a primitive ontological relation-token. The two uses of “primitive” are
typed differently.

## 6) Proof

For each quantity, the cited owning card supplies scientific establishment,
typed role, upstream ground, and its exact verification obligation. The
dependency chain is acyclic: the five owning results precede this lemma, and
none consumes this lemma as its own ground. No cited current authority record
admits any of the five as a primitive `rel_token` object. Existing domain and
typing fences remain in force. Hence each satisfies all conditions of
`GATE_004_031`, which establishes the claimed ontological separation. ∎

## 7) Scope and nonclaims

This lemma does not derive any of the five quantities or their values. It does
not merge base and active objects. It does not introduce \(\pi\) or \(\sqrt2\)
as primitive objects. It does not admit arbitrary exact, fractional,
irrational, transcendental, signed, imaginary, complex, smooth, or continuous
representations. It does not make \(t\) physical time, \(m\) physical mass, or
\(\eta\) physical length. It does not strengthen or weaken any matrix claim.
