# Admissibility classes for cell claims — A (Bravais Voronoi), B (translation-tiling parallelohedra), C (periodic multi-cell); narrowest sufficient class; packing efficiency never a selection premise

## 0) Governance & Scope Lock

Governance definition: declares the admissibility classes in which S03's geometric claims
are stated, and the two selection rules. Derives nothing; no geometric object is
constructed here. Packing-efficiency/optimality reasoning is banned on this card and, by
this card, named as banned as a selection premise everywhere in S03. Layer ceiling S03.

## 1) Plain language

Every geometric claim in this section says exactly which arena it is proved in. The
narrow arena is lattices and their Voronoi cells; the middle arena is convex bodies that
tile space face-to-face by translations; the wide arena is periodic structures with more
than one cell shape per period. A claim proved in the narrow arena may not be quoted as
if it covered the wide one. And no arena is ever entered because some shape "packs
best" — how well spheres pack is never a reason for any choice in this book.

## 2) Statement

**Admissibility classes (declared):**

- **Class A (Bravais Voronoi):** cells of the form $\mathrm{Vor}_\Lambda(0)$ for a
  (Bravais) lattice $\Lambda \subset \mathbb{R}^3$ — one site-class, cells = Voronoi
  domains of lattice points.
- **Class B (translation-tiling convex parallelohedra):** convex polytopes tiling
  $\mathbb{R}^3$ face-to-face by translations.
- **Class C (periodic multi-cell):** periodic tilings whose period contains more than one
  cell shape or site-class (e.g. lattice-with-basis structures).

**Selection rule 1 (narrowest sufficient class):** every S03 claim is stated and proved
in the narrowest class sufficient for its proof, and carries that class explicitly
(TD-010).

**Selection rule 2 (no efficiency premise):** packing efficiency is NOT a selection
premise. No class membership, hypothesis, or cell choice is justified by how densely
anything packs.

## 4) Justification (DEF obligation: well-formedness)

The three classes are well-formed (each names a standard, non-empty family of geometric
structures once the coordinate model exists — DEF_004_001, drafted this wave) and
strictly nested in expressive scope (A-cells are B-cells once the tiling property is
proved; B is inside C with a one-shape period). The two rules implement TD-010
(admissibility explicit) and the section's governance lock (no Kepler input — the
EQ_07_006 route's own in-card lock, adopted section-wide). Scope caution carried
(F-105 §4): the corpus's uniqueness chain proves uniqueness within TRANSLATION tilings
(class B), not within general isometry tilings — every consumer of THM_004_026 inherits
exactly that scope.

asserted, not denied — out of the section's premise set entirely). Guard references:
