That is **two postings** (two unit completions, `ΔI`) sharing **one tick**. And the observer reads this Now by routing across **one** edge of its circulation (`LEM_010_015`: exactly one minimal 2-channel coupling, carrying the edge factor `η` once). - **the posting count = 2** — the two twins that completed together (the *birth* side), and

Because the value of `Ψ` is **open**: the whole point of this ladder is to let the value **fall out** of pricing this event later (the cost step, `DEF_007_009`), not to write it down now. (Note which "2" this is: it is the **twins** — *not* the two distinguishable past cells that make `S_flow = 2/3`, and *not* the two channels an edge couples.
