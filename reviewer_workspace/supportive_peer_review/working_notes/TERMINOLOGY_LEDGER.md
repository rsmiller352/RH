## Summary
- Terms with missing public definitions are exactly the internal codenames (S02, T₃, P0, lens-calibration identity, partially C4) — all captured in the leakage audit with per-item repairs.
- Terms needing a reader-orientation sentence: **closure** (three senses), **forced** (global definition), **observer-rooted** (title assembly), plus optional notes for support, screen, cell.
- The charter's watch-list terms "candle", "circulation", "collective sector" do not occur in the manuscript; no action.
- §11's handling of the Ψ-event (descriptive public name, internal identifier acknowledged but not used) is the model the other codenames should follow.
